A playable pocket satire: you have a 90-second term to grow a hole through a Brasília-inspired plaza of flyers, street furniture, official cars, and Congress. Three fictional candidates change movement, growth, and combo timing. Upstream `LICENSE` is copied next to this file and also kept under `src/`.
