## 1. Findings

### Low

**L1. On the cover, "Card due after this wave" shrinks to 32 px, below the smallest text size in the game.** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/battle_hud.gd:56, `CALL_SIZES`, and :317, the label).

- Steps: cover window 362 x 926. Start a roguelite run, start wave 1, take the card after it, start wave 2, then call wave 3 early during wave 2. When wave 2 clears while wave 3 is on, a card is owed and the call button says "Card due after this wave".
- Expected: the label at no less than `FONT_SMALL`, 40 px (about 13 dp on the cover), the smallest size in docs/art/THEME.md.
- Actual: the call button keeps its fixed height (U7) and this label only fits at 32 px, the last step of `CALL_SIZES`, on three lines. That is about 10 dp on the cover, smaller than any other text in the game. The other labels take 40 to 52 px ("Last wave is out" 40, "Pick a card first" 44, the rest 52). The inner shape is fine: 52 px on two lines. Nothing overlaps and the HUD and map do not move, so this is only about reading it. Screenshots `p_cover\003_run_card_due.png` and the zoom `p_cover\004_run_zoom_card_due.png`; inner `p_inner\010_run_card_due.png`.
- Suggestion: a shorter label for this state, or a floor of 40 in `CALL_SIZES` with a label that fits it. For scale, at the cover's call button width "Card next" fits at 52 px and "Card after this wave" at 36. The words are the DM's and Bart's call.

**L2. A timed status can read "for 0.0 s".** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/battle_text.gd:48, `seconds()`, used by `status_lines()` at :334).

- Steps: on 8-3, tap a Blinker in its last tick of slow (seen while it blinked onto a twin step).
- Expected: a status with time left shows at least "0.1 s", or is gone.
- Actual: the creature panel shows "Slowed 60% for 0.0 s". `seconds()` cuts down to tenths, so the last 50 ms tick of any timed status reads 0.0. The same applies to "Bubble back in", "Breaking a gate", "Low", "Napping", "Dug under" and "Frightened". At 1x it shows for a twentieth of a second, but it stays on screen when the player pauses on that tick. Screenshot `sweep_cover\148_r8_l3_status_blinker_Out.png`. This is not new; I found it this round because the sweep read every status line.
- Suggestion: round up to the next tenth in status lines, so any time left reads at least 0.1 s.

## 3. Checked and fine

### 3.1 U8, the HUD's sound button (cover and inner)

Driver `qa7_u8.gd`, part u8: the real App in a real window, level 5-2 by real taps. Every step was checked on the App, Settings, the AudioService and settings.json on disk.

- At the start: speaker icon, sound on, music on, battle music playing.
- First real tap: sound off and music still on in the App, in Settings and on disk (`{"format":1,"music":true,"sound":false}`). The AudioService has sound off and its music switch still on. The music stops. The icon is the crossed out speaker, so the state never rests on color. The button is never disabled. Through 400 ticks of wave 1 with 9 Sling Posts firing, no voice started.
- Second tap: sound back on, music back on as it was, battle music playing again, and up to 9 voices at once in the next 300 ticks.
- Settings from Pause (real taps on Pause and Settings) read "Sound effects: On / Music: On". Music off in Settings: music stops, the HUD icon stays the plain speaker. Sound effects off in Settings: the HUD shows the crossed out speaker as soon as Settings closes. A HUD tap then brings the sound back with Music still off: no music, sound effects play. Settings opened again reads "Sound effects: On / Music: Off". Music back on in Settings: music plays.
- Restart: with the sound off mid-wave, the app was left (focus out opened Pause and saved), the App freed and a new one made on the same folder. It came back with sound off and music on, the front door's Settings read "Sound effects: Off / Music: On", Continue resumed the same tick on Pause with the crossed out speaker, and one tap brought sound and music back.
- A real process kill (`qa7_kill.gd`, `kill.sh`): a tier 2 run in wave 2, the HUD button turned the sound off, focus out, then the process was ended by its own PID (21096). The fresh process read sound off and music on, Continue resumed the run at the same tick (847) with the crossed out speaker, Settings over the resumed run read "Sound effects: Off / Music: On", and one tap restored both.
- Size and place: the round button is 160 x 160 logical px in both shapes, inside the window, and overlaps no other HUD button in any state checked: "Start wave N", "Call wave N +B", "Card due after this wave", "Pick a card first" and "Last wave is out". Cover: call button 340 px wide, sound button to its right at x 894. Inner: call button 544 px (516 while the draft panel's scroll bar shows), sound button at x 1814 (1786). The layout audit (every visible tap target at least 160 px, nothing off screen, no label wider than its box) printed nothing in any run of this round. Zooms `u8_cover\003_u8_zoom_on.png`, `u8_cover\005_u8_zoom_off.png`; screens `u8_cover\004_u8_sound_off.png`, `u8_inner\004_u8_sound_off.png`, `u8_cover\007_u8_settings_off_off.png`, `u8_cover\010_u8r_resumed_off.png`.
- In landscape the HUD box grows by one line when a dock message such as "Wave 1 cleared: +25 gold." shows inside it in the side column (438 to 532 px). The map does not move, so this is the side column layout, not a finding.
