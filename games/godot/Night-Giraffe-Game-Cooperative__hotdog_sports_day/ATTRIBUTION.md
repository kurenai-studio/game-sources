## Art and fonts

Sprites, palette, UI, and the app icon are generated by scripts in this repository (`tools/pix.py`, `tools/gen_art.py`, `tools/font.py`, and related tools) and are covered by the project's GPL-3.0 license. Bitmap fonts live in `src/assets/fonts/` (`big.fnt`, `led.fnt`, `small.fnt`). The app icon is `src/assets/art/app/icon.png` (set as `config/icon`); it is not the Godot default project icon.

## Sound effects

`src/assets/audio/sfx/` is produced by `tools/gen_audio.py`. Credits from `src/assets/audio/sfx/CREDITS.md`:

- Real instrument samples are from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) (CC0).
- Remaining effects (cartoon spring, squish, whoosh, sand, mat landing, splash, sizzle, crowd clap, and similar) are synthesised by the project tools and are covered by the project's GPL-3.0 license.

## Music

The public upstream repository does not ship music files (Pixabay tracks are fetched by `tools/fetch_music.py` for local/release builds only and are not redistributed here). Without them the game plays to a steady beat.
