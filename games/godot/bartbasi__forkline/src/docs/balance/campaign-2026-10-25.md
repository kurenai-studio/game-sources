# Balance report: the campaign pass, all 24 levels and every Twist (B3 and the campaign half of B4)

Date: 2026-10-25. For the game designer (G4) and the level designer. Written by the balance analyst.

Code and data: commit b1137be (HEAD when the pass started), played from a clean `git archive` of that commit, because other agents had
uncommitted edits in the working tree. Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-25-b3/`, one JSON file per job, with the job lists
(`jobs.txt`, `post_jobs.txt`, `special/jobs_part*.txt`, `whatif/jobs*.txt`). Every table below comes from `summary.gd` with `pass=1` run on that folder.
The same output, with the plain recipe of every level and the simplest three-star build of every level written out, is
`docs/balance/raw/2026-10-25-b3/summary.md`. Every seed is in its table and in the job lists. The main pass uses `SEED_SHIFT=100000` on the seed
bases of `run_pass.sh`.

The commits since b1137be (run tuning fdbfec7, save limits d8ccfde, UI fixes aeed462, quit fix c5d36b8) change no campaign level and no stat file.
To prove the bots and the numbers still stand, all 48 determinism jobs (24 levels, plain and Twist, the same seeds) were played again on a clean
archive of c5d36b8. Every chain hash equals the one from b1137be, and in every job all 24 repeated plays and all 72 replays through a save and load in
the middle of a wave were identical. Nothing tripped the new save limits (`docs/balance/raw/2026-10-25-b3/head_c5d36b8/`).

Covered: 24 campaign levels and 24 Twists. Any-tower searches tried 3000 builds, Twist searches 1500, lone attacker searches 6000 (two seeds per
attacker that needs one, stopping at 2500 builds when neither of the two had won a game, RULES 12.6 as revised by G2). Random play: 250 strategies per
style. All numbers are Normal unless a table says Relaxed.

## Findings

**Short version.** Every level can be won, and all three stars are reachable on all 24. The plain recipe meets its Normal bar on all 24 levels,
random play gets three stars on 3.3% of plays at most, every level takes 5.4 to 8.5 minutes with the plain recipe, the same seed plays the same,
and all 24 Twists are won on Normal by some strategy (3-2's only in a longer search). Two checks fail.
**"No super tower" fails on four region 3 levels: 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 and 3-6.** A lone Sling Post (plus Bells) earns three stars with 20 lives on each.
**The Relaxed bar fails on 3-6:** with no gates placed, the plain recipe loses in wave 9 of 12 with 40 lives. Regions 1, 2 and 4 pass every check.
F2 has tested replacements for the four star 3 rules.

### F2. "No super tower" fails on 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 and 3-6

A search that may use only the Sling Post plus the level's Bells found three-star wins with 20 lives:

| Level | Star 3 now | Lone Sling Post, three-star builds in 6000 (seed a, seed b) |
|---|---|---|
| 3-1 | Lose no lives. | 77, 646 |
| 3-2 | Have at most 6 towers. | 667, 517 (seed b won nothing in its first 2500 builds, so it was played in full) |
| 3-3 | Use at most 2 gates. | 1156, 806 |
| 3-6 | Lose no lives. | 481, 1272 |

Why: none of these rules takes away anything a lone Sling Post needs. "Lose no lives" and "at most 2 gates" limit nothing it uses, and on 3-2 a lone Sling Post
with Bells needs only 5 towers. Region 3's early waves are small (3-1: 10, 12, 30, 18 creatures; 3-2: 10, 16, 14, 10), so RULES 12.5's "crowd before the gold" is
not there. No other attacker needed a search on these levels (flyers cost 46 to 108 lives, walkers 313 to 381; RULES 12.6), so the Sling Post is the one super tower, as in region 2 before G2.

Region 4 passes. A lone Sling Post wins nothing on 4-2 to 4-6 (no win in 2500 builds, both seeds). On 4-1 it wins 1587 and 124 of 6000 builds (one star or two, best 14 and 4 lives, never three).
Region 1 passes (best lone result under the rule: Catapult 14 lives on 1-3, Sling Post 12). Region 2 passes (best: 11 lives, on 2-1).

**What I tested as replacements.** Every search and seed is in "What-if" and "Longer checks" below. A rule works when the any-tower search still finds three-star wins and no lone
Sling Post search does. The 2500 build screens are not the verdict: a 6000 build check overruled one of them.

| Level | Rule tried | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 3-1 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 799 three-star builds in 2500. Lone Sling Post: 0 in two 6000 build checks (2107 and 2203 wins, none under the rule). |
| 3-1 | Build no Sling Posts | **Works.** Any tower except the Sling Post: 2090 and 2145 three-star builds in two 6000 build searches. (Fireworks hit the flyers. A lone Catapult, Longbow or Firework cannot keep 15 lives, RULES 12.6.) |
| 3-1 | Have at most 5 towers | Fails. Lone Sling Post: 12 three-star builds in 2500. |
| 3-2 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 873 in 2500. Lone Sling Post: 0 in two 6000 build checks (2416 and 2091 wins). |
| 3-2 | Build no Sling Posts | **Works.** Any tower except the Sling Post: 2474 and 2393 in two 6000 build searches. |
| 3-2 | Have at most 5 towers | **Fails the longer check.** The 2500 build screen found 0 lone Sling builds. A 6000 build check found 138 three-star builds with 20 lives. |
| 3-2 | Have at most 4 towers | No build earns three stars (no build that wins with 4 towers keeps 15 lives). |
| 3-3 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 404 in 2500. Lone Sling Post: 0 in two 6000 build checks (2396 and 2389 wins). |
| 3-3 | Have at most 5 towers | **Works but thin.** Any tower: 92 three-star builds in 2500. Lone Sling Post: 0 in two 6000 build checks. |
| 3-3 | Build no Sling Posts | **Works.** Any tower except the Sling Post: 1798 and 1842 in two 6000 build searches. (The 2500 build any-tower screen found none: it misses ban rules, F6.) |

| 3-6 | Have at most 6 towers | Fails. Lone Sling Post: 394 three-star builds in 2500. |
| 3-6 | No tower above tier 2 | No build earns three stars (the boss needs tier 3). |

Region 3 already has "Build no Sling Posts" on 3-4 and "No tower above tier 2" on 3-5. Giving the four failing levels only bans and caps would make
the whole region one of two rules, so mixing them keeps some variety (for example a cap on 3-1 and 3-3, a ban on 3-2 and 3-6). That choice is for G4.
I tested only the rules above. A wave change (a dense Pip crowd by wave 5, RULES 12.5) was not tested.

### F3. 3-6 on Relaxed with no gates

The plain recipe on Relaxed (40 lives) with no gates placed loses in wave 9 of 12. It lost 20 lives in wave 5 and the last 18 in wave 9. With its best placement (gate at 5,3) it keeps all 40 lives.
Four of the 27 placements lose on Relaxed: no gates, and the three that use only socket 1,5 and/or 1,7. On Normal the recipe with no gates also loses, in wave 5.
The losses are Pip crowds: wave 5 (56 creatures) lets 19 Pips through, wave 9 (74 creatures) 18 Pips (`special/r3_l6_replay_plain_relaxed.json`, `mode=replay relaxed=1`).
A human can place a gate, but RULES 12.6 asks that the plain recipe win on Relaxed with no gates, and every other level meets it.

### F6. About the searches: they can miss

- **Ban rules.** The any-tower search found no three-star build on 3-4 ("Build no Sling Posts") or on 4-2 ("Build no Pumpkin Catapults"), and none under the 3-3 ban rule, but searches with the banned tower left out
  found about 2000 in 6000 builds on each. A search that may use every tower seldom reaches builds that avoid one. For a level with a tower ban a pass needs a search over the allowed towers
  (`run_special.sh` part 3). This pass did it for 3-4 and 4-2. Its three-star champions are the ones used for the Dusk Moth question (F9).
- **2500 build screens.** 3-2 "at most 5 towers": 0 lone Sling Post three-star builds in 2500, 138 in 6000. The same lesson as 2-2 in B1.
- **Dry stops.** 2-2 seed b, 3-2 seed b and 4-1 seed b won no game in their first 2500 builds while their partners did, so each was played in full (6000). Results: 2-2 1255 wins, none under the rule;
  3-2 517 three-star builds; 4-1 124 wins, best 4 lives. Pairs where both stopped at 2500: 2-5, 2-6, 4-2 to 4-6.
- **Twist searches.** 1500 builds missed 3-2's Twist (F5).

### F8. The Moon Moth on 3-6 (the game designer asked)

The Moon Moth flies a straight line, so the share of the line flown is the share of the path. 62 Moon Moths were watched: 27 plain recipe placements (16 reach wave 12), the same placements with Double Trouble (32 moths),
the 12 best three-star builds, and the Double Trouble search champion (2 moths). Health at halfway, of 1120:

- **Never dead before halfway.** All 62 are alive at halfway. Plain recipe: 686 (61%) in every play. Champions: 565 to 882 (50% to 79%). Double Trouble: 661 to 904 (59% to 81%).
- **Where they die.** Plain recipe, single moth: 73.8% of the line. Champions: 61% to 78%. In Double Trouble every moth passes three quarters of the line (60 to 536 of 1120 left there), and the second one of the plain recipe dies at **96.7%**, almost at the base. The Twist champion's moths die at 70.6% and 87.2%.
- In all, 26 of 62 moths die before three quarters of the line, and 36 pass it (all 32 of the plain recipe's Double Trouble moths and 4 others).

So the rule "raise if it dies before halfway" does not trigger. The weakest champion kills it at 61% of the line, with 50% of its health still on it at halfway.

### F10. Suggestions

For the game designer (G4):

1. **Region 3 star 3 rules (F2).** Replace the rule on 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 and 3-6. Tested and passing the 6000 build checks: 3-1 tier 2 cap or no Sling Posts; 3-2 tier 2 cap or no Sling Posts; 3-3 tier 2 cap, at most 5 towers (thin: 92 three-star builds) or no Sling Posts; 3-6 no Sling Posts.
   Not these: at most 5 towers on 3-1 or 3-2, at most 6 towers on 3-6, no gates on 3-3, a tier cap on 3-6, at most 4 towers on 3-2. Whatever G4 picks, I can confirm it with two 6000 build lone Sling Post searches and an allowed-towers search.
2. **3-6 on Relaxed with no gates (F3).** The Pip crowds of waves 5 and 9 are what an ungated recipe cannot stop. Either soften those two waves (a Pip wave at strength 115 or less lets one pumpkin pop a Pip, RULES 12.5) or accept that Relaxed on 3-6 needs a gate. Not tested.
3. **Moon Moth.** Leave it. If the designers want it to last into the last quarter of its line, the lever is the boss wave's strength (70 now; G2 named 85 to 100), not a stat. Not tested.
4. **Dusk Moths.** No stat change (F9). Builds that beat them exist; the recipe loses 6 to 10 lives to them on 3-4 to 3-6 only because of where its Fireworks stand.
5. **Thin Twists.** 4-1 Thin Purse: 5% of searched builds win, best 10 lives. If the phone says it is too hard, 4-1's start gold is the lever. 3-2 No Catapults: fair, a narrow field of winners; nothing to change unless the phone says so.
6. **Bakery paths** are rare in winning builds (Grand Oven 22, Spice Kitchen 18), and the Bakery is absent from the winning builds of 4-4 and rare outside 4-6. A flag, as the Bell paths were in B1.

For the level designer:

1. If G4 changes the star 3 rules of 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 or 3-6, the data and the PLAN in `tests/levels/test_level_design.gd` change with them.
2. 3-4 and 3-5: send the highlight to a spot under the flight line for a Firework (F9). 3-6: the same for the Moon Moth's line.
3. 4-1: the hint should lead the player to two gates (1,3 with 5,7, or 5,3 with 1,7). With one gate the recipe keeps 3 lives, and with the wrong pair it loses.
4. 2-3: the recipe lets six Flitters through (9 lives). The hint is about armor and Longbows, so nothing points the player to the Sling Posts the Flitters need. A line about Flitters would help.

For the balance tools: for a level whose star 3 bans a tower, always search over the allowed towers. 2500 builds are never the verdict for a lone attacker.
