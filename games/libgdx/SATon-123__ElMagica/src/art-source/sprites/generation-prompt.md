# Промпт генерации

Инструмент: встроенный `image_gen.imagegen`, `transparent_background: true`.

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one production game sprite atlas PNG for a tiny top-down LibGDX game ElMagica. Generate a perfectly aligned 4-column by 2-row atlas of EIGHT isolated simple pixel-art game character sprites on genuine transparent background. Each cell equal square size, each sprite entirely inside its own cell with generous transparent padding, same center alignment and camera: front-facing slightly top-down RPG view. NO text, NO labels, NO decorative borders, NO floor, NO cast shadows outside character. Crisp low-resolution chunky pixel silhouettes, small limited palette, dark navy pixel outline, readable at 60x60 or 124x124 display pixels, no antialiasing, no realistic rendering, no gradients. Exact cell order left to right: TOP ROW cell 1: a humble wizard, broad pointed hat, robe, short staff, face hidden in navy shadow with two tiny eyes; hat and robe LIGHT NEUTRAL GRAY and WHITE, staff neutral gray, all grayscale so runtime tint can recolor identically for four elements. TOP ROW cell 2: a simple friendly round squishy slime blob, two dark eyes and a tiny mouth, LIGHT NEUTRAL GRAY and WHITE body with simple bright pixel highlight, entirely grayscale for runtime tint. TOP ROW cell 3: fire+water boss, steam spirit: a solid blue boiling cauldron-like armored body with orange ember core and dense white steam forming two clawed arms and a looming face; water boiling into steam, both elements genuinely combined in anatomy, NOT slime. TOP ROW cell 4: fire+earth boss, lava golem: hulking rock body, jagged shoulders, molten orange-red fissures glowing through dark brown rock armor, short thick legs and fists. BOTTOM ROW cell 1: fire+air boss, flame tornado monster: orange flame spiral physically wrapped around pale wind bands, fierce face in whirlwind, tapering cyclone lower body, NO slime. BOTTOM ROW cell 2: water+earth boss, bog colossus: squat mossy stone turtle-like swamp guardian, muddy stone plates and blue water channels pouring between plates, stout legs, clear blue eyes. BOTTOM ROW cell 3: water+air boss, storm jellyfish: floating blue jellyfish storm creature, pale cloudy bell, curling rain-water tentacles spun by visible wind spirals, expressive face. BOTTOM ROW cell 4: earth+air boss, sand guardian: upright tan stone mask held aloft by a twisting sandy whirlwind, floating rock chunks integrated into arms, desert wind motif. Boss silhouettes should be visibly distinct and cohesive combinations, not two-color blobs. Atlas contains only these eight sprites, one per cell, no duplicates or extra objects. Sprite bodies fill around 75-85 percent of each cell, consistent baseline; atlas grid must be exact so code can split into four equal columns and two equal rows.
