# Roadmap

What's planned for SPPPD after the next release, in no particular order. Nothing here
has a date: each thing ships when it's ready, and the [changelog](CHANGELOG.md) says
when it does.

Last updated 2026-11-07.

## Planned

- **Elimination races:** last place drops out at the end of every lap.
- **Sprint races:** point to point instead of laps. These need new tracks, because
  every track today is a loop.
- **Career:** a chain of events that earn cash or stars, which unlock cars and paint.
- **Cop chases:** a pursuit mode with roadblocks and a heat level that rises the
  longer you run. It's the biggest job on this list, because the cops have to drive
  well enough to be fun to escape.
- **Ranked races and profiles:** accounts, rankings and a profile that keeps your
  record. These need the server to check race results, which it doesn't do today.
- **Drift combos in freestyle:** chain slides for a multiplier, with a best score kept
  for each car.
- **More to change mid-drive in freestyle:** time of day and handling from the pause
  menu, as car and paint already are.

## Not planned

- **Weather and tyre wear:** SPPPD is an arcade racer, and these push it towards a
  simulation.
- **Car upgrades:** not before there's a career to earn them in.
- **Drift scoring in races:** tried and dropped. Sliding fills your boost, and that's
  the reward.
