# Spotting, Bots & Match

## Spotting (`src/game/spotting.ts`, `SPOTTING`)

- Every 0.3 s for each "observer → enemy" pair: visible if `d < 50` or (`d < view range · (1 − camouflage)` and the line of sight is clear over the terrain (6 m step, eye 1.6 m above the hull) and not blocked by buildings).
- A vehicle's view range is multiplied by the map weather (`WEATHER`: clear 1, overcast 0.95, dawn 0.9, dusk 0.85, rain and snowfall 0.8, fog 0.6) and by 0.85 with a wounded commander.
- Camouflage = vehicle base value + bush 0.35 / forest 0.18 − movement (0…0.08 by class) − firing 0.85 for 5 s. Capped at 0.9.
- A bush hides completely: a tank in a bush is not visible from any distance until it fires (then it is visible for 5 s, under the usual camouflage rules) or until an enemy comes closer than 50 m.
- Spotting is shared: a vehicle spotted by one is visible to the whole team; after it is lost it stays on screen for 2 s. Wrecks are always visible.
- Invisible enemies are not drawn, labelled or shown on the minimap. «Вас видят» (You are spotted) in the status — when you are spotted. "Sixth sense": at the moment an enemy spots you (and had not seen you for at least the 2 s of memory before that), a lamp lights up above the clock for 4 s and a signal sounds.

## Bots (`src/ai/bot.ts`, `src/ai/roles/`, `BOTS`)

- Structure: `bot.ts` — the shared core (target selection, firing, driving, consumables, reaction to being hit, hunting the last enemy); class behaviour is a separate module in `src/ai/roles/`: `light.ts`, `medium.ts`, `heavy.ts`, `td.ts`, `spg.ts` (mediums and heavies sit on top of the shared strike group `strike.ts`; shared helpers — `common.ts`). The role plans the route at the start and decides every frame about retreating, engaging, waiting and relocating (`RoleAi`).

- Skill 0.2–0.85: aiming error up to 1.6 m × (1 − skill), rerolled every 1.5 s; reaction after aiming 0.4–1.8 s × (1.3 − skill); lead by target speed × 0.9 × skill. Experienced ones (> 0.6) aim at the lower part of the hull.
- They fire when the gun is loaded, dispersion has settled (`bloom < 0.7`), the turret is on target, the barrel has reached the required elevation (howitzers — to within 0.006 rad; if the angle is beyond the aiming limit because of hull tilt — they do not fire, `elev`), the line of fire from the gun is clear of buildings, terrain, wrecks and friendlies, and the penetration chance is not hopeless (otherwise they wait for a better angle). If the AP penetration chance is < 35 % — they load APCR. When choosing a target they prefer one visible to their own gun (×2.2 to the "cost" of a hidden one); ambush roles, if the target has been hidden for 6 s, shift position 25 m towards the target — to high ground from which it is visible.
- Roles by class (module in `src/ai/roles/`):

| Class  | Role      | Behaviour                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| ------ | --------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| light  | scout     | `light.ts`: does not sit in bushes — drives from point to point (2–5 s pause) 70–140 m ahead of the leading strike group and spots for the SPGs; fires only at the track of an exposed side (or finishes off a target below 25 % HP); after firing, being hit or an enemy closer than 110 m it immediately moves 40–60 m farther away from it. When ≤ 60 % of enemies remain — a raid: round the flank (150 m off the straight line) to the last spot of an enemy SPG or to the enemy base, the SPG is the priority target; without an enemy SPG it looks for an enemy |
| medium | assault   | `medium.ts`: leads its strike group along its lane to the centre; does not go ahead of the rest of the group by more than 45 m; retreats at low HP                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| heavy  | cover     | `heavy.ts`: goes in the same group ~20 m behind the mediums, in combat moves up level (+10 m); priority — enemies firing at friendlies; does not retreat                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| TD     | ambush    | `td.ts`: on high ground (in a bush if there is one) ~80 m behind one of the strike groups, watches ahead of it and catches up when the group has gone more than 50 m; at the start — a third of its lane; relocates after being hit; in the late phase also goes looking for the enemy                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| SPG    | artillery | `spg.ts`: a flat spot 40 m from the spawn point (the base is flattened), otherwise stays put; drives away from an enemy closer than 90 m; does not engage                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |

- Team tactics (`src/ai/team.ts`): lanes are ordered across the axis (flank — centre — flank). At the start of the battle a side picks a plan: one strike group on a random lane (flank or centre) or, with 55 % probability when there are two or more lanes and at least three mediums/heavies, two groups on different lanes (both flanks, or a flank and the centre). Mediums and heavies are split evenly between the groups, the TD covers one of the groups. Progress is measured along the base → base axis, waiting is within one's own group: a medium that has got ahead of the rest of its group (excluding the player and those retreating) by more than 45 m stops on the best high ground within 25 m and waits (down to a 12 m margin; at most 25 s, then 15 s without waiting). A heavy waits the same way if it has got ahead of its group's mediums. Nobody waits in the first 80 m from the base.
- Fight for the centre: the group goes along its lane to the high ground before the middle (before the crossing or at the open point in the middle) with a view of the other side and holds it; a target appearing within reach — immediate combat. If nobody is within reach for 12 s — the group pushes on along the lane towards the enemy base, and having arrived and stood 10 s without a target — goes looking for the enemy by the last spots.
- Late phase (≤ 60 % of enemies remain or 2 minutes have passed): everyone except SPGs closes in — the waits in the centre and at the end of the route are cut to a third (to ~4 s), TDs also go looking for the enemy, scouts go on a raid, in combat they do not wait for the group and keep a 30 % closer distance.
- Firing position (`src/ai/fight.ts`): in combat mediums, heavies and TDs pick a point within a ~30 m radius (farther from the target — 40–70 m closer to it, if they need not stay with the group): higher on the terrain, with a line of fire to the target, not on a steep slope and not right next to friendlies; reconsidered every 9 s or when the target has been hidden for 2.5 s. Lights still drive in directly and keep moving.
- Retreat below 28 % HP (except heavies, SPGs and during the hunt for the last enemy): behind the nearest building towards their own base, then hold position.
- Reaction to being hit: if the attacker is visible and within reach — fire is switched to it; if not visible — 2.2 s of reverse with the front towards the direction of the hit (heavies only turn).
- Cover ("shoot and scoot"): after firing the bot looks 7–22 m behind itself (away from the target) for a point where the target's gun cannot see its turret — behind a building or a crest — and reverses there with its front to the target; it comes out to the firing point when the gun is almost loaded. If there is no cover — it stays in position. Lights, SPGs and guns with a reload < 3 s do not do this.
- Hunt: when the enemy has one tank left, all bots, including damaged ones (retreat is cancelled) and SPGs, sweep the area where it was last seen (each from its own direction, 40–130 m from the point), and if the spot is older than 45 s — the enemy half of the map; an SPG, on seeing the target, stops and fires.
- Target selection: distance × (0.6 + 0.4 · HP fraction) × (1.6 − penetration chance) × 0.7 for the attacker × 0.85 for the one who last hit an ally (heavies × 0.65) × 0.8 for an immobilised one × 0.7 for an SPG closer than 220 m × 2.2 for one hidden from the bot's own gun.
- Shells: HE if the target's armour ≤ 85 % of HE penetration; APCR if the AP chance is < 35 %; derp guns always on HE.
- Artillery: if the barrel is against the aiming limit for 3 s (standing on a slope), it relocates to flat ground 35 m away. Aiming error ×0.4, full lead; fires only at a target moving slower than 2.5 m/s or immobilised, does not drop a shell if a friendly is closer than splash + 4 m from the target. Target selection among those spotted by the team: ×0.25 for one standing still for more than 3 s or immobilised, ×0.5 for a slow one (< 1 m/s), ×1.5 for a moving one, ×3 for a fast one (> 4 m/s), ×0.75 for a thin roof (≤ 15 mm), ×0.85 for SPGs and TDs, ×3 if a friendly is nearby.

- Navigation: three lanes base → ford → base (`terrain.lanes`); the route starts from the lane gate, not from the base centre. Feelers at 1.5L/2.5L straight ahead and 1.8L at ±29° (world edge, water, steep slope by mean rise and local gradient, buildings, rocks); the avoidance side is kept for 1.5 s.
- Tanks in the way (hull in the movement corridor, up to 1.5L + 0.8 s of travel): the bot steers away from the tank, follows a tank going the same way with throttle ≤ 0.35, goes round everything else with throttle ≤ 0.55. If a tank is driving at the bot and has priority (the player always, among bots — the one created earlier), the bot yields: stops, and on contact backs up; after 3 s of waiting it goes round for 2.5 s. A waypoint or hold point occupied by a tank counts as reached from 25/20 m.
- Stuck > 2.5 s → 1.8 s of reverse turning the nose away from the obstacle (if there is a tank or an obstacle behind — a pivot turn in place); a third stuck in a row → route via the nearest lane point (not the base centre). The rows leave the base by actual position: 3.5 s per row + up to 2 s random.
- Base capture: if the enemy is capturing our (or the neutral) base, the team sends there the nearest bots not engaged in combat (except SPGs): two in Standard, three in Encounter, four when defending in Assault, and in any case one more than there are capturers in the circle. Once more than 50 points have been scored (and in the Assault defence immediately), bots already in combat are pulled off too. In the circle together with the capturers they freeze the capture, and once alone there — reset it. They are released when the points are reset. Capturers in the circle are a priority target (×0.45 to the "cost"). Bots with nothing to do (stood without a target and went looking for the enemy), with a chance of 30 % (Standard), 50 % (Encounter), 70 % (attacking in Assault) — in the late phase 55 / 80 / 100 % — drive into the circle and stand there instead of searching: they do not look for the last enemy and do not back away from an invisible attacker. A bot going to take or defend a base does not get drawn into combat on the way (fires on the move), and in the circle fires from where it stands: it does not move to a firing position, does not hide in cover to reload, does not retreat at low HP and does not drive off for ammo. A light tank on such a task (and on the player's order) stops scouting and fires without restrictions. A bot that was sent to the base, given an order or sent to hunt the last enemy while on its way for ammo abandons the resupply. In Encounter and in the Assault attack the strike group, pushing on after the centre, drives not to the enemy base but into the circle. The Assault attack goes in a wave: the groups wait for the late phase (80 s or an enemy loss) rather than driving in one by one. The Assault defence holds the centre until it has taken losses (time does not hurry it). 110 s before the end of Encounter and Assault all attackers not in combat and not defending go to take the base. The search for the enemy (hunt, light raid) — only over the passable part of the map, not in the mountains at the edge and not in the sea.
- Player team signals (`src/game/teamSignals.ts`): T — «Атакую!» (Attacking!) (an enemy under the reticle: the whole team prefers it for 25 s, ×0.45 to the "cost"; otherwise the three nearest bots drive to the high ground at the aim point), G — «Нужна помощь!» (Need help!) (the two nearest drive to the player), B — «Защищайте базу!» (Defend the base!) (the two nearest — to their own or the neutral base). Those sent by a signal stay put for 40 s, then return to normal behaviour. A ping on the minimap, the bot's reply in the message log; no more than once per 2 s.
- Repair kit, first aid kit and fire extinguisher are used automatically but not instantly: the bot "notices" the damage after 1.5–4 s (fire — 1–3 s), multiplied by (1.3 − skill).
- On the result screen bots no longer think: throttle and steering are reset, vehicles stop.
- Friendly damage and kills are dealt but do not count towards statistics (damage, «Уничтожил» (Destroyed)).

## Match (`src/game/match.ts`, `src/game/capture.ts`, `MATCH`, `CAPTURE`)

- Garage: the player's nation and vehicle, the map (random or one of the named ones, `MAP_PRESETS`) and the mode (random or fixed); the map and mode choice is remembered in the browser. The player's team: the player + bots by the roster "1 light, 2 medium, 2 heavy, 1 TD, 1 SPG" minus the player's class; the enemy — the full roster.
- Balancing (`pickRosters` in `src/game/match.ts`): every vehicle has a battle rating `rating` (T-60 2 … IS-2 and Ferdinand 7). A bot's vehicle is chosen at random among the vehicles of its class with a rating within ±1.5 of the player's vehicle (if there are none — the nearest by rating), then single swaps bring the teams' rating sums to a difference ≤ 0.5 (no more than 20 passes). Both sides have the same classes.
- Line-up in two rows at the base facing the enemy, SPGs at the back. 4 s countdown.
- Modes (random 60 / 25 / 15 %, `modeOdds`): **Standard** — each side has its own base; **Encounter** — one neutral base in the middle (on the line equidistant from the bases, closer to the map centre; there is no river on such a map, the city moves to a flank); **Assault** — one side defends its base, the other has nothing to capture, on time-out the defence wins.
- Capture: a 30 m circle, a flag and a ring of stakes. Points belong to the capturing side, not to individual tanks: leaving the circle and being hit do not reset them.
  - More capturers in the circle than defenders: +1.25 points/s for every tank of superiority, no more than three counted (one — 80 s, three — 27 s).
  - As many or fewer capturers than defenders: the capture is frozen («заморожен» (frozen) on the capture bar).
  - Only defenders in the circle (the capturers left or died): the capture resets to zero immediately, the defenders in the circle share the defence points.
  - Empty circle: the points stay where they are.
  - Your own base can only be taken by the enemy; the neutral base starts being taken by the side with more tanks in the circle, the other side counts as defenders for it.
  - 100 points — victory. The capture bar under the clock (green — we are capturing, red — we are being captured), circles and progress on the minimap, a warning in the log when the enemy starts taking the base.
- Victory: the enemy team is destroyed or the base is captured; after 6 minutes — in Assault the defence, otherwise whoever has more survivors, a draw if equal. Result screen 11 s: the reason, personal numbers (damage, spotting damage, blocked, destroyed, spotted) and medals; then the garage and a **new map** (new seed, everything is regenerated; a named map — its own seed).

## Battle report (`src/game/battleReport.ts`)

- In the garage after a battle — a collapsible block «Разбор последнего боя» (Last battle report): shots, hit and penetration percentages, damage dealt and received; two tables («Нанесено» (Dealt) and «Получено» (Received)) per enemy vehicle: hits, penetrations, ricochets, non-penetrations, crits (modules, crew, fire, track), damage with splash, where the hits landed (hull, turret, lower plate, mantlet, cupola, screen — with counts) and the kill (your own — in green, of the one who destroyed you — in red). Friendly fire is not counted.

## Ribbons and medals (`src/hud/ribbons.ts`, `src/game/medals.ts`)

- Ribbons in battle above the shell bar, with counters: kill, penetration, crit, set on fire, track, spotting, spotting damage (an ally damaged an enemy that this tank is spotting), armour (hit without penetration), ram, capture, base defence.
- Battle medals (7 × 7 scale): «Колобанов» (Kolobanov: victory while left alone against three or more), «Воин» (Warrior: 3+ kills), «Основной калибр» (High Caliber: most damage, ≥ 1000), «Снайпер» (Sniper: 4+ penetrations from 250 m), «Разведчик» (Scout: 4+ spots), «Поддержка» (Support: ≥ 800 spotting damage), «Стальная стена» (Steel Wall: ≥ 1200 blocked over 6+ hits), «Захватчик» (Invader: base taken in a capture victory), «Защитник» (Defender: ≥ 40 capture points knocked off), «Мастер критов» (Crit Master: 4+ crits).
- The scoreboard (Tab) has the added columns «По засвету» (Spotting damage) and «Броня» (Armour).
- A dead player spectates allies (Space — next), the battle runs to the end.
- Pause: while the mouse is not captured (Esc, window switch) during the countdown or battle, time stands still and sound is paused; clicking the screen resumes. With `?autostart` the pause is disabled (there is no mouse capture in automated tests).
