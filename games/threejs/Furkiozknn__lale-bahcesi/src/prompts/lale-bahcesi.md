# Arena round 4, game 4: Lale Bahçesi (Tulip Era garden builder) — built by Claude Haiku 5.5

```text
Build a single-file browser game (one index.html with inline CSS and JS) using Three.js r170 loaded as an ES
module from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/ (import map; addons from examples/jsm/).
A 3D cosy garden game called "Lale Bahçesi" (tulip garden) set in the Ottoman Tulip Era. Do not ask questions,
build it end to end.

WORLD: a walled palace garden terrace by the Bosphorus on a grid: paths, flower beds, a marble fountain, a kiosk
(köşk) with a wide roof, cypress trees, plane trees, lanterns, a pool with turtles (the tulip-era night festivals
had candles on turtles' backs). Spring sun, petals blowing in the wind, butterflies; at night lanterns and
candles. Soft pastel toy-like look, flat shading, soft shadows, one accent colour (#BE185D tulip magenta).

GROWING: plant tulip bulbs in beds; tulips sprout, bud and bloom over the season, then you lift the bulbs.
Watering (fountain range and a watering can), soil quality and sun matter. Crossbreeding: two neighbouring
blooming tulips can make a seed of a new variety with mixed colour, petal shape (round, almond, needle-like
"lale-i rumi") and pattern (flames, edges). A tulip catalogue fills as you discover varieties (named rare
varieties are special finds). Pests (aphids) and a late frost can damage beds.

GAME RULES: start with a small terrace, 6 bulbs and 50 akçe. Sell bulbs and cut flowers at the bazaar (prices
rise for rare varieties: a gentle tulip-mania market that can also crash), buy decorations, expand the garden.
Each season ends with a garden festival judged on beauty (variety, colour harmony, symmetry around the fountain,
decorations): a score with three stars. Goal: complete 4 seasons and win the Sultan's tulip festival with 3 stars,
discover at least 20 varieties; an endless free-garden mode afterwards. Varieties, prices and scoring are data in
one table; balanced for a relaxed but real challenge.

INTERACTION: click a bed tile to plant / water / lift, drag to place decorations, a build menu for paths, beds and
decorations, rotate with Q/E, drag to pan, wheel/pinch to zoom, day speed control (1x, 2x, 4x) and pause, a
catalogue panel with all discovered varieties drawn as small previews.

HUD: top bar (logo, season and day, akçe, varieties found, beauty score, speed, pause, sound, language), tile panel,
catalogue, bazaar, toasts, title, festival result, season summary, ending.

EXTRAS: fountain water, birds, wind, petal chimes, festival crowd, a gentle Ottoman court-music-style loop (ud,
kanun, ney timbres), all synthesised (CC0 one-shots from asset-forge allowed).
```

The shared rules in `_sablonlar/arena-round4-common.md` are appended to this prompt.
