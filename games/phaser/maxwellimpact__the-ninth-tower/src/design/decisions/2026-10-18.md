| 2026-10-18 | **No more band of background on iPhone.** iOS Safari sometimes reports an in-between size while its toolbars slide or the phone turns, and then never sends a final resize, so the game kept a shorter height and a dark band showed until the phone was rotated again. | 2026-10-18 | **New cottages, each its own.** The user found the cottages' proportions odd; after proportion and door studies they chose two body types: **A** (the hipped 3/4 cottage) and **C** (a storybook cottage: a big rounded roof over a low stone-footed wall, an arched door). A generator (src/gfx/cottage.js) rolls each house's **roof** (red tile, blue slate, thatch, green moss, plum, brown shingle), **door** (plain with hinges, panelled, or with a lit window; arched or square; no centre seam; six colours), **windows** (four-pane, round, tall arched, shuttered, small, diamond-leaded; usually a matching pair; flower boxes or a sill), **chimney** (none, a stone stack, a thin brick one, a narrow pipe, either side, standing in the roof with a shadow at its foot) and up to two **ornaments** (lantern, barrel, ivy, pots, bench, weathervane, dormer). Brightwater's six alternate A and C down each street; roofs and doors are dealt so no neighbours match, and the rest is rolled once from a fixed seed, so each building always looks the same. Each keeps its sign; the mayor's flies the banner; the tavern and steam house keep stone chimneys. **Every lit chimney smokes** (from wherever it stands), the open-doorway overlay is cut to each door's own shape, and the boarded-up homes get dark planked windows, a nailed door, dead flower boxes and a few slipped tiles. tools/house-mix.js renders a random street or the town's six |

| 2026-10-18 | **Dodges and misses.** Now and then a basic attack misses, both ways (tuning.evade). Heroes have a **DODGE** stat (shown on the gear page): Sable and Wren 10%, Brann in plate 3%, the rest 4-5%, and Fleet gear adds 4%. Foes: 5% by default; wolves, hounds, imps, raiders and rats 12%; golems, trolls, brutes, knights, beetles, boars and the like 1%; bosses, and anything stunned, frozen or flipped, never. Only basic attacks can miss (a hero's plain hit or shot; an ordinary foe's swing or arrow) - skills, area attacks and boss moves always land. A miss shows MISS or DODGE, a little sidestep and a whoosh; a hero who dodges **counters**, their next basic hit within 3 s landing 30% harder (COUNTER!). **Benchmarked** (7 fights x 2 seeds, levels frozen, the lab's gear seeded so every run is the same party, the game's own loop asleep so only the bench steps it): old code twice for a noise floor, then new. Party damage taken totals 253.5 / 234.5 (old) vs 232.9 (new); party damage dealt 977 / 956 vs 946 - both inside the old-vs-old spread, so the misses on each side cancel out as intended; 35 hero dodges and 35 foe misses over the 14 fights. tools/evade-bench.js; results in tools/bench-results/evade-*.json |
