Trampoline is a French word game designed by Michel Cheenne and made by Maximilien Naveau: build 13 nine-letter words on a grid, with letters that "bounce". The game, its words and its dictionary are in French.

Every tile has two faces, a yellow one and a green one, with a different letter on each side. Lay a word along a row, tap a letter to flip it, lengthen the words already on the board, swap a letter with one from your hand, move a block of letters… Yellow scores, green costs, and a word's last letters score the most.

• Solo, or 2 to 4 players sharing one device
• Free play and 10 crosswords
• "Comment jouer": the rules as short animations
• Complete French dictionary, works offline
• Original music and sound effects
• No ads, no tracking, no data collected
• Free software (GPL-3.0)
