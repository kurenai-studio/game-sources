KINDRED LIGHTS
A game by Rida Kalam for thatgamejam #02.

Sing into the dark. Someone will answer.

HOW TO PLAY
  Move  : hold the left mouse button (you drift toward the cursor), or WASD / arrow keys
  Sing  : Space, or the right mouse button (also J / E / Z)
  Pause : Esc   (from the pause screen, Q returns to the title)
  Fullscreen : F11 (or Alt+Enter)

Sing near sleeping lights to wake them. They follow you, and their light parts the deep dark.
In the dawn fields, sing over the sleeping buds. If you sing on the beat, your friends sing with you.
Find the Old Light on the hill. It takes about 6 to 9 minutes, and there is no way to fail.
Headphones recommended.

WINDOWS: unzip the whole folder, then run KindredLights.exe. Keep the .dll files next to it.
  The game isn't code-signed, so SmartScreen may warn you: click "More info", then "Run anyway".
MAC: unzip, then right-click "Kindred Lights.app" and choose Open. The app isn't signed or notarized.
  If macOS still blocks it, go to System Settings > Privacy & Security and choose "Open Anyway".
ANY OS WITH LOVE 11.5: run  love KindredLights.love
