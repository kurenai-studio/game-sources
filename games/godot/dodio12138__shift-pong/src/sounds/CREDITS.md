# Sound credits

Every sound in the game is cut from the live match audio of
"2 tekma finale državne ženske lige v namiznem tenisu Ravne 2015" by
Televizija Uršlja, CC BY 3.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/),
via Wikimedia Commons:
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2_tekma_finale_dr%C5%BEavne_%C5%BEenske_lige_v_namiznem_tenisu_Ravne_2015.webm

Changes: clipped to single impacts (racket hits `hit_hard_*` / `hit_*`, table
bounces `table_*`) chosen for the least background, shaped with an exponential
decay so commentary behind them falls away; hits high-passed at 350 Hz; all
normalised.

Footsteps (`step_0.ogg` … `step_4.ogg`) are `footstep_wood_000` … `footstep_wood_004`
from Kenney "Impact Sounds" (https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), CC0.

Shoe squeals (`squeak_0` … `squeak_7`) are whole squeaks cut from
"shoesqueakingonfloor" on Pixabay (Pixabay Content License):
https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-shoesqueakingonfloor-95716/

Music (`music/menu.ogg`, `music/match.ogg`) and the skill / menu sounds in `fx/`
are original music and sounds generated by `tools/make_audio.py` (MIT, like the code).
