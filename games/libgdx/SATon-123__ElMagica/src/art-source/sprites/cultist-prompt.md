# Стрелок-культист

Файл: `cultist-v1.png`. Создан встроенным imagegen с `transparent_background: true`.
Один спрайт окрашивается в цвет стихии, прицел и снаряды рисуются в коде.

Промпт:

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated production game enemy sprite PNG for ElMagica, a simple pixel-art top-down RPG arena. Subject: a small ranged cultist enemy, not a player wizard: hunched hooded figure with a very short blunt hood (NO tall pointed wizard hat), a dark empty face with two pale eyes, simple light gray cloak, gray leather belt, holding a compact gray magic crossbow horizontally in both hands. Front-facing slightly top-down view, strong readable silhouette at 60x60 screen pixels, limited chunky pixel palette, dark navy outline, neutral grayscale clothing so runtime tint can give Fire/Water/Earth/Air colors. A subtle white bolt tip and small shoulder plates distinguish the shooter from the friendly wizard. One character only, centered fully visible, 10 percent transparent padding, actual transparent background. No text, no labels, no floor, no shadow outside character, no multiple poses, no sheet, no weapon floating separately. Crisp pixel art matching a cute gray wizard and round gray slimes, no smooth painterly shading, no photorealism. Only the one enemy character.
