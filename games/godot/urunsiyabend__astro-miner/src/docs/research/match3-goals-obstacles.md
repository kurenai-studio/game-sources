# Match-3 level goals and obstacles (research, 2026-10-19)

Main source: Dream Games' official Royal Match Help Center, "Game Elements" (108 element FAQs,
one clearing rule each). Others are fan wikis and blog analyses. [unsure] marks unverified.

## Goals against real games

- **Collect**: everywhere (Candy Crush Candy Order; in Royal Match most obstacles are goals).
- **Excavate**: Gardenscapes gnomes/bunnies under grass, Soda frosting bears, RM Curtain and
  Rock (power-ups reveal a gem). Candy Crush jelly is a cover-clear, not a hidden item.
- **Deliver**: Candy Crush ingredients; RM Bird, Frog, Space Shuttle ("bring to the bottom");
  Gardenscapes Lemonade (falls faster with vertical matches in its column).
- **Activate**: no pure "turn on N machines"; it is "fill an element until done": RM Drill
  (collect colours, then it clears a line), Bush (5 adjacent matches spread grass), Tesla Coil.
- **Connect**: rare. Candy Crush Rainbow Rapids (clear a path from cannon to mold; now mixed into
  other level types). Soda Sweet Escape (guide a bear to a portal) is close.
- **Rescue**: Soda honey bears (up to 6 layers of honey), RM Polar Bear (5 adjacent matches).
- **Sequence**: inside one element: RM Royal Bot (3 matches break the crate, then drop it),
  Furnace/Marble (power-up first, then matches), Bowling Box, Shelf, Cupboard.

## Obstacles by interaction (RM examples)

- Layered, hit by an adjacent match: Box, Vase (2), Royal Egg, Honey, Oyster (3), Birdhouse (5).
- Power-up only: Owl Statue, Porcelain Piggy, Stone (3), Safe (5), Chain, Metal Plate.
- Colour-specific adjacent match: Potion Bottle, Light Bulb, Color Box, Dynamite Box.
- Collector / meter: Curtain, Drill, Royal Cryptex.
- Generator (adjacent matches spawn goal items, closes when the count is reached): Mailbox,
  Magic Hat, Cauldron, Soap, Jar, Igloo.
- Spreader: Grass/Bush, Jelly, Turtle; Soda Chocolate grows on a move that clears none.
- Under-layer: Gardenscapes gnomes, Soda frosting bears, RM Rock.
- Lock cover: RM Chain (power-ups only), Candy Crush Liquorice Lock.
- Drop to bottom: Bird, Frog, Royal Bot, ingredients, Lemonade.
- Multi-cell: Porcelain Bar ("once for each tile it occupies"), Cupboard, Safe, Magic Hat,
  Cauldron [unsure on sizes]; damaged as one object, one hit per adjacent match.

Adjacent-match elements keep early levels readable; power-up-only ones make players build
specials; colour ones make them plan which colour to match; late elements chain two of these.

## Pacing

RM: Grass level 4, Cupboard 11, Mailbox 21; boosters at 7, 8, 14, 17, 19. Levels 1–22 short and
easy (~27 blockers, ~1.1 attempts); first hard level 39. Types per level: 1–2 early, 3–5 later
[unsure]. Gardenscapes tunes difficulty partly by colour count (3–4 normal, 4–5 hard).
