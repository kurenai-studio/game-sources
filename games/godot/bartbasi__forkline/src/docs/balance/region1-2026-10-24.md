# Balance report: region 1 rerun on the tuned levels

Date: 2026-10-24. Author: balance-analyst. For: the game designer and the level designer.
Tree: commit 473d768 (the tuning plan in docs/design/TUNING_R1.md). data/levels/, data/stats/ and src/sim/ had no uncommitted changes during the runs. Raw files: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24/` (git ignored); the tables below are in `summary.md` there.

This is an independent rerun. It used the same bots with new seeds (section 7), longer searches, and checks the designer did not run. About 520,000 plays in all. Battles have no randomness, so a level plus the player's choices gives one result. Rates from 750 random plays carry an error of about 3.5 points, rates from 250 about 6.

## 1. Findings

1. **The designer's headline is confirmed: no single tower type earns three stars on any level.** Sling Post only and Catapult only, 3000 builds on each of two new seeds per level: 0 three-star wins in all 24 searches. Longer searches (8000 builds, 8 more) and an exhaustive check of level 1-3 (162,720 builds per tower) also found none. Catapult only cannot win 1-5 or 1-6 at all on Normal (Flitters). Section 4.1.
2. **The check holds for different reasons on different levels, and on 1-3 it holds by one life.** A Sling Post only build cannot win under the star 3 rule on 1-2, 1-4 and 1-6 (no win with tier 1 only, or with no gate, in 3000 builds on two seeds). Catapult only cannot win 1-5 or 1-6 at all. In every other case the one-tower builds that meet the rule do win, but keep too few lives for star 2 (15 needed). The closest call: **1-3, Catapult only, at most 2 towers: best 14 lives**, in the exhaustive set, in 8000 searched builds and on both seeds. One more life would break the check. Best lives under the rule elsewhere: 1-1 Sling 10, Catapult 12; 1-2 Catapult 9; 1-3 Sling 12; 1-4 Catapult 12; 1-5 Sling 11.
3. **Every tower and all four tier 3 paths appear in a three-star win.** The Sling Post and the Catapult are in 100% of the three-star builds on every level. Tier 3 paths can only appear on 1-3 and 1-6, because the other four star 3 rules cap the tier. There: Quickshot 322 and 645, Stone Sling 131 and 591, Prize Pumpkin 232 and 284, Jam Pot 259 and 484 builds (of 727 and 1016). Nothing is dead. The thinnest is the Stone Sling on 1-3 (18% of three-star builds). Region 1 has no armored creature, so Stone Sling and Prize Pumpkin cannot show their real job here.
4. **The plain mixed recipe matches the designer's table exactly.** Sling Posts and Catapults in turn, most road first, one upgrade each: best gate 16, 19, 17, 20, 19 (three stars) and 15 lives; worst gate 16, 10, 9, 20, 16 (three stars) and a loss in wave 10. On Relaxed it wins every level with every gate choice (40 lives, 39 on 1-5; worst 18 on 1-6). Section 4.3.
5. **One star on Relaxed is reachable on every level.** Section 4.4. Caveat: a Relaxed player who builds Slings only and never upgrades loses 1-4, and on 1-6 the gate choice decides (4 of 16 recipes win with every gate choice).
6. **Random rates reproduce the designer's.** Typical style on Normal: 69, 62, 44, 80, 20 and 11% (designer: 73, 58, 47, 73, 21, 13). Random plays with three stars: 4, 2, 0, 2, 1 and 0% (all under the 10% bar). Section 4.2.
7. **The bots cannot test the RULES 12.5 first-try targets (95, 90, 85, 85, 75, 70%).** They can say a winning build exists, how wide the margin is, and where random play breaks. A new "guided" random style (every spot, both towers, all tier 2, random places and gate) wins 67, 73, 61, 99, 43 and 26%. That is a floor for a thoughtful player. It is above the target on 1-4, near it for Sling-leaning mixes on 1-1 (96%) and 1-3 (85%), and well below on 1-5 and 1-6. Section 3.
8. **Third stars.** 1-1, 1-2 and 1-4 ask for "no tower above tier 1": the plain mixed recipe with no upgrades earns three stars on 1-1 (16 lives), 1-2 (19) and 1-4 (15), so it rewards restraint. **1-5's third star does not bite:** 82% of searched wins meet it and a plain recipe earns it with 19 lives. 1-3's is hard but findable (727 of 3000). 1-6's is met by never placing the gate. Section 4.5.
9. **Length, size, speed.** Length at 1x with no early calls: 5.2 to 6.6 minutes (target 5 to 10). Largest wave 74 creatures (1-4), under the 80 readable limit. Most creatures on the map: 74. A tick costs 21 to 62 microseconds on average, 123 to 136 where 50 or more creatures stand, 99th percentile 251 at worst (budget 2000). Section 4.6.
10. **Same inputs, same result.** All 144 repeated plays, all 432 plays with a save and load in the middle of a wave, and all six search repeats matched. The chain hashes match in a separate process. Section 4.7.
11. **Not covered.** Roguelite runs and the daily seed (src/run/ does not exist). The bots never sell a tower, so selling and moving towers is untested. The other four tower types are not offered in region 1. Twists.

## 3. The RULES 12.5 targets: what the bots can and cannot say

The targets are first-try win rates for a thoughtful player on Normal. The bots have no first try and no learning, so they cannot measure these. Bart's playtest at hard stop B is the check. What the bots add:

| Level | Target | Random, typical style | Random, all styles | Guided random | Guided, Sling share 75% or more | Mixed recipe, best / worst gate |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-1 | 95% | 69% | 60% | 67% | 96% (n=83) | won 16 / won 16 |
| 1-2 | 90% | 62% | 52% | 73% | 68% (n=50) | won 19 / won 10 |
| 1-3 | 85% | 44% | 21% | 61% | 85% (n=71) | won 17 / won 9 |
| 1-4 | 85% | 80% | 59% | 99% | 100% (n=77) | won 20 / won 20 |
| 1-5 | 75% | 20% | 10% | 43% | 72% (n=79) | won 19 / won 16 |
| 1-6 | 70% | 11% | 6% | 26% | 61% (n=56) | won 15 / lost |

Guided random is 400 plays a level, seeds in section 7. It builds on every spot, uses both towers, takes every tower to tier 2, and picks places and the gate (or none) at random. The Sling share column is a subset: plays where the random draw gave 75% or more Sling Posts. It is a proxy for a player who leans on Slings, not a measured player.

What the bots can say:

- Every level has a win with all 20 lives left (search), and the plain recipe wins every level at its best gate. So no level is out of reach.
- 1-4 is easy against its target. Guided random wins 99% with 83% keeping 15 lives, and every guided play with 25% or more Sling Posts won (335 of 335). A thoughtful player should clear 85% easily.
- 1-1 is near its target for a player who leans on Slings (96%), and loses mostly to Catapult-heavy mixes (25% when under a quarter of the towers are Slings).
- 1-5 and 1-6 need both towers, a gate that fits, and tier 2 everywhere. Random play wins 10% and 6%. Whether a thoughtful player reaches 75% and 70% depends on how fast people learn the Flitter and gate lessons. The bots cannot say.
- On 1-6 most random plays are lost before the boss. Of all random plays 8% die in waves 1 to 3, 46% in 4 to 6, 35% in 7 to 9, and 4% in the boss wave. Only 78 of 750 reach the boss, and 45 of those win (58%, was 1 in 150 at strength 110). The boss at 1000 health still stops 42% of the plays that reach it.
- The gate is a hidden lesson on 1-2, 1-3 and 1-6. The mixed recipe with no gate keeps 10, 9 lives (one star) and loses on 1-6. With the right socket it keeps 19, 17 and 15. The hint highlights the socket, so this is by design, but it is the largest swing between a 1-star and a 2-star player.

The first third of every search (1000 builds) is random starts. A first three-star build after build 1000 on 1-1 to 1-4 means no random start earned three stars, and edits found them soon after. That fits the 0 to 4% random three-star rates.

- **Restraint stars.** On 1-1, 1-2 and 1-4 the third star is "do not upgrade". The mixed recipe with no upgrades earns it (16, 19 and 15 lives). The recipe with upgrades earns two stars. A player who upgrades from habit loses the third star for good, because the sim keeps the highest tier ever reached (selling does not reset it).
- **1-5 does not bite.** 82% of wins meet the rule, the plain mixed tier 2 recipe earns three stars at 19 lives with all four gate choices, and a 3-star build exists with only 3 towers (and with 2 towers under a "at most 3 towers" rule, 73 of 3000). Tower count caps did not change that (at most 4 towers: 1174 three-star builds in 3000; at most 3: 951). The level is hard for random play (10%) and easy for a good build, and star 2 (15 lives) does the real work.
- **1-3 is the tight one.** Two towers, one of each, upgraded. A thoughtful player can find it from the hint ("spots are few"). It is also the level where the single-tower check is thinnest (14 lives).
- **1-6 is met by not placing the gate.** The gate matters for the plain recipe (no gate loses in wave 10) but the searched three-star builds win without it: the rule is only hard because the waves are.

## 5. Suggestions

Everything here is a suggestion. The bots changed no rule and no level.

**S1. Game designer: keep an eye on 1-3, Catapult only.** The best one-tower win under the rule keeps 14 lives, and star 2 needs 15 (exhaustive over 162,720 builds, plus 8000 searched). Anything that makes the Catapult a little stronger, the 1-3 Scamps a little weaker, or star 2 a little lower can break the check there. ## 7. Seeds and how to repeat

Run everything from the repo root. Each job writes one JSON file. The job lists are in the scripts.

```
bash tools/balance/run_rerun.sh 2026-10-24 10        # about 1 hour on 12 cores
bash tools/balance/run_exhaust.sh 2026-10-24 11      # about 2 hours
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/summary.gd -- dir=res://docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24
```

One job by hand, for example level 1-3 with the Catapult only, seed A:

```
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/run.gd -- mode=search level=r1_l3 seed=24300 evals=3000 only=pumpkin_catapult
```
