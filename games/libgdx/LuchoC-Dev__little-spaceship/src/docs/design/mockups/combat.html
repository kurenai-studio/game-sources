What the scene proves

    What to look for

    R1 · two disjoint sets

    Set map

    Switch the view to Set map. Everything the background may draw goes flat blue, everything gameplay may draw goes white, enemy fire goes magenta. The three never mix, because the colours to mix them with are not available.

    R3 · the value gap

    Greyscale

    Strip the colour and the bullets are still the brightest things on screen. That is the 3.2-point gap between the sets doing the work; hue is the second signal, not the first.

    R4 · never hue alone

    Protanopia and deuteranopia

    Cyan player fire and magenta enemy fire converge under both simulations. What keeps them apart is shape — elongated against compact — and value.

    R5 · outlines

    Squint

    Blur the screen until detail is gone. Bullets, ship, enemies survive in that order. If the city survives instead, R7 was broken.

    Hitboxes

    What actually kills you

    The ship’s circle is 6 px across a 15 px sprite. Enemy fire collides smaller than it looks, pickups larger, enemies as drawn — generosity always points at the player.

    Boss

    Five circles, not one

    Switch to the boss scene with hitboxes on. Collider is a circle with no offset, so one circle over 119 px would swallow the gaps the player flies through.

    The palette it is drawn from

    Generated from docs/design/mockups/combat.html. Silhouettes stand in for art that has not been drawn yet — what is exact here is the footprint, the palette and the HUD geometry, not the craft. This page keeps the reserved hue band too: nothing in its own interface is magenta.
