# Technical notes

Decisions and working defaults that are not covered by `INTENT.md` or `AGENTS.md`. Items marked **default** were proposed by Claude and the owner has not rejected them. Items marked **from the prototype** come from the design prototype and should be re-checked after the version 1 spike.

## Toolchain

- Godot 4, current stable at the time of installation. Record the exact version in the project when it is created.
- JDK 17, required for the Android export.
- Android SDK command-line tools, platform-tools (`adb`) and build-tools. Godot's export settings point at the SDK folder.
- Godot's Android export templates, installed from the editor.
- A debug keystore for development. A separate release keystore for GitHub release builds, kept out of the repository and out of any container image.
- Development happens directly on the owner's Linux machine, not in Docker. The Godot editor needs the display, and the phone connects by USB. Docker may be used later for CI builds in version 3.
- Android package name: `io.github.aaryash_shakya.pieceful`. **default** minimum Android version: 8 (API 26).
- Tests: a GDScript test framework (GUT is the likely choice, not yet verified on the current Godot version), run headless with `godot --headless`.

## Android permissions

- Internet, for the collection store.
- Vibration, for haptics.
- Notifications are not planned. See `docs/open-decisions.md`.

## Images and collections

- **default** Images are about 2048 px on the long edge, saved as WebP, roughly 300 to 600 KB each.
- **default** Four generated placeholder collections ship, `assets/collections/abstract/`, `geometry/`, `landscape/` and `pattern/`, each with six pictures (3:2, 2:3, 1:1, 4:3, 16:9, 9:16 at 2048 px on the long edge) generated by `tools/make_collection.gd` (run once per collection with `--out res://assets/collections/<id> --id <id> --title <Title>`; the recipes are in `tools/art_synth.gd`, `SETS`), CC BY 4.0, marked `"generated": true`, with 256 px thumbnails in `thumbs/` under the same file name. The manifest holds `id`, `title` and `pictures`, each with `id`, `title`, `file`, `width`, `height`, `credit`, `licence` (`CC0-1.0`, `public-domain` or `CC-BY-4.0`) and `generated`; a bad entry is skipped with one warning. Pictures import as Lossy (quality 0.8) with mipmaps. The generated collection is a placeholder: its tests only show that colour and grain are present, so whether each picture is pleasant to solve is an owner check.
- **default** `CollectionLibrary` (`src/game/collection_library.gd`) loads the bundled manifests listed in its `BUNDLED` constant and merges them into one `Collection` whose `parts` are the collections, so the board still finds a picture by id through one collection. Picture ids must be unique across collections; a duplicate is skipped with one warning. The setup screen shows a row of chips, one per part, above the picture strip; choosing one selects its first picture and keeps the nearest valid count, and Resume opens the picker on the saved picture's collection. A single collection shows no chips.
- Each collection has a manifest listing, for every image, its file, title, credit and licence. Allowed licences are public domain, CC0, the owner's own, or AI-generated. Mark AI-generated images as such in the manifest.
- The app ships with four collections, about 40 images, so it is fully usable offline. The daily puzzle is chosen only from bundled images.
- More collections are downloaded from Cloudflare R2, served from a custom domain and not the default `r2.dev` address. Downloaded collections live in app storage and survive a progress reset. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes them.
- The prototype assumes a 3:2 picture. Real pictures will have other shapes. The piece-count slider and the grid split must adapt to each picture's aspect ratio.

## Piece counts

- The range is 12 to 400 pieces.
- The setup slider offers about 20 of the valid counts (`SetupScreen.step_counts`, log-spaced from 12 to 400), not every valid split. The daily puzzle and saves still use any valid count. For Regular and Irregular, only row-by-column splits whose cells stay close to square are valid. For a 3:2 picture there are about 100 valid counts between 12 and 600, fewer up to 400. The slider moves between valid counts only.
- Wild accepts any count, but the slider uses the same list for consistency.
- On a 3:2 picture, 54, 96, 150, 300 and 400 are valid grid splits; 200 and 250 are not, which only works because those are Wild days. On a square picture none of 54, 96, 150 and 300 is valid, and the smallest valid count is 16.
- What the daily puzzle does on such a picture is open decision 13 in `docs/open-decisions.md`.

## Behaviour details from the prototype

- Pieces never rotate. All pieces in a cluster share one offset from their home position.
- A drop locks a cluster when its offset from home is within a tolerance, about 22% of the average cell size, with a minimum of 10 image pixels. A drop joins two clusters when they are neighbours in the solved picture and their offsets agree within the same tolerance. Joins are checked on drop, not while dragging.
- Locked pieces are painted into one cached layer, so the screen draws them as a single image.
- **default** In the Godot build, locked pieces move to their own layer under the loose pieces and are drawn as ordinary pieces. Painting that layer into one cached image is a follow-up, made only if the phone check shows locked pieces cost frames.
- Only loose pieces within the view are drawn.
- Dragging a piece near the screen edge pans the camera.
- The cleanup button packs loose pieces in shuffled order, row by row, in two bands, one above and one below the board, then animates them into place.
- **default** `CleanupLayout` puts a gap of 1.25 times the join tolerance (about 0.28 cell) between clusters, so no tidied cluster is within join reach of a neighbour, and 0.7 cell between the board and the first row; each band is 1.45 board widths wide and grows outward from the board. The order is shuffled from the cut seed and a press counter. Clusters animate over 0.4 s (`Board.CLEANUP_DURATION`); a touch or app pause completes the animation at once. The camera bounds grow to cover the bands. The button is "Tidy", top right.
- A slot holds clusters as a stack. A slot shows a count of pieces, not clusters.
- Sound: a light tick on pick-up, a short click on a join, a lower, firmer thump on a lock, and a rising chord on completion. In the prototype all sounds are generated by code. The real app should use short sound files.
- **default** The sounds are `assets/sounds/*.wav`, generated by `tools/make_sounds.gd` (CC BY 4.0): mono 16-bit PCM at 44 100 Hz, imported uncompressed. Pick 50 ms, join 70 ms, lock 120 ms, complete 930 ms.
- Haptics: a light pulse on a join and a firm pulse on a lock.
- **default** Join 12 ms at amplitude 0.5, lock 25 ms at 1.0, completion 60 ms at 1.0, none on pick-up. A drop that joins and locks plays only the lock. The switches are `Feedback.sound_enabled` and `Feedback.vibration_enabled`, set by the version 2 settings.
- **default** The Godot theme is built by `UiTheme.build()` from `Palette` and the bundled fonts in `assets/fonts/` (SIL Open Font License): Monda 500 at 20 for body, Laila SemiBold at 22 for buttons and Laila Bold at 28 for titles, in viewport units.
- **default** Saves: one puzzle in `user://save/current.json`, sorted JSON with tabs and full-precision floats, written to a `.tmp` file and renamed over the old one. It holds the image id, size, style, requested count, seed and a checksum of the cut, every cluster's root, members, offset and lock, the loose draw order, elapsed seconds and the tidy counter, never geometry. The board saves after each lock and on pause, focus loss, close and back, deletes the save on completion, and on start regenerates the cut from the save; a save that cannot be read, parsed or used (another version, a cut that no longer matches, a loose offset more than 8 image sides out) logs one warning, is moved to `current.bad.json` (replacing an older one), and a fresh puzzle starts. A leave that would write the same text as the last write skips it. The camera is not saved. Elapsed time counts while the window has focus (not merely resumed) and the puzzle is not complete, at most 0.25 s per frame.
- **default** The app opens on the setup screen: picture thumbnails, Regular or Irregular, a slider over the picture's valid counts (default 54, the nearest valid count kept across picture changes) and the cut preview, which regenerates 0.15 s after the input settles, and while the slider is dragged at most every 0.12 s so it follows each step, with the seed Start will use. Resume shows only for a save of a collection picture that loads. A save that cannot be read or parsed, or that names a picture the loaded collection lacks at that size, is set aside; a sound save whose pictures are unavailable this run (no collection, or the picture fails to load) is left in place with one warning. Start replaces the save without asking. Home (top left) and back leave the board with a save; back on the setup screen quits.
- On completion: the full picture fades in over the board, confetti falls, and the board shakes briefly. All three are controlled by the effect settings.

## Test devices

- **Main test phone: Nothing Phone (4a).** Android 16, Snapdragon 7s Gen 4, 8 or 12 GB RAM, a 6.78 inch AMOLED screen with a 1224 by 2720 resolution and an adaptive refresh rate from 30 to 120 Hz. This is a recent mid-range phone. Because its screen can refresh at 120 Hz, record which rate each frame-rate check ran at. The target is at least 60fps.
- **Low-end stress test: the owner's old Samsung Galaxy On7 Pro (2016).** Snapdragon 410, Adreno 306 GPU, 2 GB RAM, Android 6.0 (API 23). A worst-case check: if 400 pieces run smoothly there, they should run almost anywhere. Godot 4.3's system requirements list the Adreno 306 as an example of a GPU that supports the Compatibility renderer. That renderer is OpenGL-based, and this phone has no Vulkan, so it could not run the Mobile renderer. Whether Godot 4 can export to Android 6.0 is not verified, and some forum users report black screens on Godot 4 below Android 10. The owner agreed on 2026-10-22 to treat the On7 Pro as optional. Drop it if the app cannot install.

## Owner phone checks for feedback

- Hold a piece near its place, press home (or pull the notification shade), come back: the lock plays once and nothing plays late on resume.
