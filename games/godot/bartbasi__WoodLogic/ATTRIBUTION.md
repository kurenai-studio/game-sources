A playable screw puzzle: tap screws out of a wooden contraption into color-matched boxes, clear every screw to win, and do not let the tray fill up. Levels are generated on device and checked by a solver first. Upstream `LICENSE` (GPL-3.0) and `NOTICE` are copied next to this file and also kept under `src/`.
