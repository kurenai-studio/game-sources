The eight work families of the closed phase, kept as the record.

Phase 4 is closed, with five documented limits (D66 and D71 in DECISIONS.md). This ledger is updated for later detector decisions. A counterexample can stop its exact target, not the whole phase. No source-navigation count, case count or completed publication closes a family.

## Preserved outcomes from the removed Phase 4 evidence summary

`phase-4-work-in-progress.md` was removed on October 17 under D76. It was a selected summary from before Phase 4 closed; its bytes remain in git history. These are selected outcomes from it, as they stood then; the rest of its detail (for example the ledge and deadline restart checks and the later source-review counts) stays in git history and in the records the other ledger sections cite. Each is a bounded check with its limit. D66 and D71 record how Phase 4 closed.

- Legal deterministic clears: a public-action trace reached single and multiple clears and saves. Not every 3 or 4 line clear, level, timing or history variant. New saves omit the history fields, so they are not certified good saves.
- Input alias and resume suppression: a held soft-drop sequence paused and resumed while the key stayed held, and a later neutral gamepad poll registered a new soft-drop interval before the key was released. Hardware and other event schedules were not covered.
- Saved-state withdrawal: pausing lost the reset count after Continue, and controlled flag failures left Continue offered in the same session. Getter evidence is not native UI evidence.
- Failed reads and retained timers: controlled failed reads changed the stored flag, and a retained lock callback after a failed cancellation changed a stopped game. Artificial failure providers only.
- Cache version consistency: controlled service-worker cases showed an HTML and module version mismatch after the clock advanced, and a delayed opening across two publications at a fixed clock. Not a native lifecycle.
- Drawing registration refusal: a refused animation-frame request left the game paused with stale Pause and menu pixels, and a later request was suppressed by the retained latch. One private observer clone; direct redraw recovers.
- Continue and reset-bag failure: one artificial dependency cut interrupted Continue. The logger lost operation tags, so equal before and after fields are not a zero-write count.
- Resize sizing-prefix attempt: the first attempt failed its declared checkpoint and was not silently retried. A later partial trace recorded the board setTransform refusal before delegation and stale size records. It is not a completed resize witness.
- Four later checks, each run once and independently reviewed, were Candidate recommendations at their stated scopes, not Confirmed Defects: New Game withdrawal followed by a factory failure (S2); a held soft-drop callback firing while paused with no safety checkpoint (U4); cache deletion that removed a foreign cache's name binding (APP-6); and a ResizeObserver drawing prefix with no player notice (U2). Limits: the prior save was not shown to be a valid last-good save; native timing, all timers and every interleaving were not covered; the approved cache wording attachment was not recovered; the blank resize image is an adverse display observation, not a failure-policy verdict.
- Source reasoning: reviewed conditional arguments cover descending row removal and survivor order, the 1 to 4 line score table, the no-full-row history induction, and level division for nonnegative safe integers. Imported full-row states, larger integers, aliasing, exceptions and whole-turn composition were excluded. None is certified Proven.
- The narrated 3,738-lock random trace had no seed, harness or raw log. D69 and Phase 5 records section 4 replace it with new runs.
- Four compressed replacement archives (UC348, NG357, CACHE362, W28-366) were kept. They do not show the original attachments' identity or that their evidence applies to current code. UC348 finished but its comparison failed and its comparison file is empty. Backup download and recovery on iPad stayed unverified.
- Unclassified candidate: a resting O piece resumed from pause at lock-delay count 15 locked at once and started a new piece, unlike the independent expected result. At count 16 it also locked, as stated.
