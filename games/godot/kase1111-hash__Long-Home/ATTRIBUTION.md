A playable first-person mountaineering descent simulation (Godot 4.2, Forward+): plan a line, descend from the summit to base camp, and deal with fatigue, weather, avalanches, glaciers, rope work, and skiing. Upstream `LICENSE` is copied next to this file and also kept under `src/`.

## Icon

`src/icon.svg` (md5 `0f1c028aeb3cb13b740247c1269e6814`) is a custom mountain-gradient mark that shipped upstream; it is not the default Godot icon.

## Third-party assets

No third-party art, music, or font files are bundled. Terrain, sky, vegetation, the climber and base camp are procedural. Sound effects under `src/assets/audio/` (257 `.wav` files, no music) were synthesized in-repo by `src/tools/sfx/generate.py` and are documented in `src/assets/audio/README.md`.

## Fonts and audio

No custom font files are bundled. Audio is original synthesized SFX as noted above.
