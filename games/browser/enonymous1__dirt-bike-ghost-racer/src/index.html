Controls

  • ↑ / ↓ — Change lane (4 lanes)
  • ← / → on ground — Wheelie
  • ← / → in air — Pitch bike: Left = nose up, shorter jump; Right = nose down, longer jump
  • Z — Standard acceleration (slow heat build-up)
  • X — Turbo acceleration (fast, but heats up rapidly)
  • After a crash — Mash Z / X to run back to your bike faster
  • Enter / R — Start / restart
  • E — Open the custom track editor (from the title screen)
  • Ghost race — Use Race Ghost in the Ghost panel below to race a translucent ghost on its original track

Land with your bike angle matching the ground or you will crash. Blue chevrons are Cool Zones — ride over them to drop your temperature instantly. Mud slows you down, it does not crash you.

Custom Track Editor

Press E (or Edit Track) to open the editor, then click the track on the canvas to place an obstacle, or use the form below. Scroll the editor with ← / →, the scroll buttons, or the mouse wheel. Right-click an obstacle on the canvas to delete it. Press Esc to go back to the title.

    Ghost Races — Shareable Ghosts

    Every race you finish is recorded (20 samples per second). Your fastest run is kept as your best ghost. To race a ghost, use Race Ghost — it loads that ghost's original track, so ramps and mud line up exactly. Export your best ghost, send the JSON to a friend, and they can import it and race you. If you beat a ghost, your run becomes the new best ghost.
