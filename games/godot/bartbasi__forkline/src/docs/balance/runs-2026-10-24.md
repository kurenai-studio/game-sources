# Balance report: the first run and daily report (B2)

Date: 2026-10-24. For the game designer (G3), the roguelite engineer (R3) and the level designer (L5). Written by the balance analyst.

Code and data: commit ec871fc (HEAD when the pass started), played from a clean `git archive` copy, because other agents had
uncommitted edits in the working tree. Commit c015b05 (Ironshell armor 6 to 10) landed while the pass ran. Everything below was
measured at armor 6, except the section "Ironshell at armor 10", which plays every Ironshell seed again on a clean copy of
c015b05. Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24-b2/`, one JSON file per job. Every table comes from
`tools/balance/runs_summary.gd`; the whole output is `raw/2026-10-24-b2/summary.md` (it also holds the long card tables).
The job lists are `jobs.txt` and `extra_jobs.txt` in the same folder. How the bots work: `docs/balance/README.md`, "Runs and the daily".

Every play below uses the real `RunSession` with the real segments, packs and cards. Normal and Relaxed, "no unlocks" (the 22
base cards) and "all cards" (34 cards) are separate settings.

## Findings

**Short version.**

1. **The same seed always plays the same.** 50 runs and 50 dailies: map, waves, offers and final state are equal, also across a save
   and load in the middle of a wave and with a draft open (F1).
2. **Runs and the daily can be won.** Some bot strategy wins 50 of 50 run seeds on Normal. For the daily, 390 of the next 400 days
   are won by one of 116 strategies, and 399 of 400 by one of up to 1500. One day, 2026-12-01, was not won (F2).
3. **Win rates (Normal, no unlocks) are 24.8% for 16 named strategies**, from 6% to 51.5% by strategy. **Relaxed is 83.6%.**
   **All cards halves the Normal rate to 12.9%** (F3).
4. **A run lasts about 14 minutes, not 15 to 20.** Won runs average 13.3 minutes; without early calls 14.5. 16% of wins are inside
   15 to 20 (F4).
5. **The final boss is decided by lives, not by the towers.** 21 or more lives at the start of wave 18 wins 61% to 100%; 20 or fewer
   wins 9% to 19%. The boss takes 20 lives, so a run with 21 or more outlives it. **Tea Break is the card that does this:** runs
   with no Tea Break win 6%, runs with three or more win 61% to 74% (F5).
6. **Gold piles up unspent from wave 14.** By then every spot has a tier 3 tower and the bot has nothing left to buy. The median
   gold on hand is 627 at wave 14, 2,542 at wave 16 and 4,643 when the boss wave starts. Income is about 1,000 a wave late (F6).
7. **The Bakery is a trap on run maps.** On all 42 map stacks the best Bakery spot reaches one other spot at most, and on 8 stacks
   none. No bot run ended with a Bakery standing, and a bot that builds one anyway wins less (F8).
8. **Some map stacks are walls.** `mid_cross` with `top_fork` or `top_twin` loses 62% to 70% of plays by wave 6. `mid_ladder` wins
   6.3%. `bot_gate` and `bot_yard` win about 13%, against 37% for `bot_porch` and `bot_square` (F7).

What to take from it:

- **Economy picks win most, risk picks least.** The economy styles win 40% on Normal, the risk-taking styles 9%. The reason is Tea Break (F5).
  Taking every risk card is a losing habit (F9 shows what each risk card costs), unless the player is strong.
- **All cards is worse than no unlocks** by half on Normal (24.8% to 12.9%) and by 10 points on Relaxed. Two causes show in the data.
  The economy kind has 7 cards instead of 5 once Piggy Bank and Bargain unlock, so Tea Break comes up less often: 68% of all-card
  plays pick no Tea Break at all, against 49% with no unlocks. And the 12 new cards are mostly sidegrades or risks that the bots
  take without caring (F9). A human who picks well will see less of this, but the unlock cards do not make runs easier.
- **Early calls cost about 6 points on Normal.** They end a run sooner (F4) and the bots call at 6 or fewer creatures.
- **Relaxed is easy** (83.6%): its 40 lives outlive the boss's 20 (F5).
- Random strategies, which are not tied to a style, win 37.4% on Normal (F2), more than the average named strategy, because their
  card order is often economy first. The named strategies are archetypes, not the best a player can do.

Where Normal, no unlocks runs end (3,200 plays): 39.3% are lost in wave 18, 14.3% are lost by wave 6 (5.3% in wave 5), and 24.8% are won.
The rest are lost between waves 7 and 17 (1% to 3% a wave).

**Answer: yes, a run lasts about 14 minutes.** The level designer's number holds: 14.5 minutes without early calls (10.0 of wave time
plus 18 build phases at 15 s), 13.3 minutes counting every winner. Only 16% of Normal wins reach 15 minutes. To reach 15 to 20 minutes
at the same 15 s build phases, the 18 waves would need about 10.5 to 15.5 minutes of wave time, against 10.0 now. The
15 s per build phase is only a convention: a draft pick and a build take a player longer than that in practice, so the real length
is probably above these numbers. The designer may set the target from a real playtest.

### F5. The final boss: lives decide it, and Tea Break is the card that supplies them

Runs that started wave 18 (boss) by lives at that moment, Normal, named strategies:

| Lives at the start of wave 18 | No unlocks: runs | Won | All cards: runs | Won |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 to 10 | 529 | 12.9% | 590 | 8.5% |
| 11 to 20 | 819 | 19.0% | 800 | 12.0% |
| 21 to 25 | 342 | 62.6% | 191 | 60.7% |
| 26 to 30 | 216 | 90.7% | 91 | 86.8% |
| 31 or more | 162 | 98.8% | 72 | 100.0% |

The win rate jumps at 21 lives. The boss and the escort take lives when they get through, and the boss alone takes 20. A run with
21 or more lives can let the boss reach the base and still win: in those wins the boss reached the base 68% to 93% of the time.
With 20 or fewer lives a win needs the towers to kill the boss, and that happened in 9% to 19% of runs. The towers alone are a wall.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| Ironshell (armor 6) | 504 | 37.5% | 17 | 79.4% | 40% |
| Moon Moth | 489 | 19.0% | 16 | 97.8% | 60% |
| Old Tusk | 473 | 39.3% | 18 | 57.5% | 40% |

Across all four bosses, 38.4% of runs that start wave 18 win on Normal (23.7% with all cards). In lost runs the boss's health was
down to 43% at its lowest (median). Moon Moth is the hardest: 97.8% of its wins let it reach the base, so the towers almost never kill it.
The wave 16 to 18 packs lose a lot of the runs that enter them (inside the pack, Normal, no unlocks, `b6_*` packs): Grumblesack 46%
and 50%, Ironshell 63% and 64% (armor 6), Old Tusk 63% and 65%, Moon Moth 86% and 80%.

The card-by-card experiment (F9) agrees: taking Tea Break instead of another offered card raises the win rate by 9.6 points on
average, 11.6 with all cards, and by 13 to 14 points in the last six drafts. Nothing else comes close. What-ifs on the same 50
seeds, Normal, no unlocks (baseline 25.6%): **Tea Break giving 3 lives: 19.1%; 1 life: 13.3%.**

**What-ifs on the boss's strength** (same 50 seeds; run strength is `100 + step * (wave - 1)`, step 10 now, so wave 18 is 270):

| Final strength | Step | Won | Reached wave 18 | Won of those |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 270 (now) | 10 | 25.6% | 63.4% | 40.4% |
| 236 | 8 | 32.3% | 67.8% | 47.6% |
| 202 | 6 | 43.8% | 75.4% | 58.0% |

A lower step eases every wave, not only the boss, so this is a different cure from a boss-only change. The run-wide win rate rises 6.7 points
for the first 2 points of step and 11.5 for the next 2.

**Answer: the final boss is a wall for the towers, and a run is won by outliving it.** 60% of runs that start wave 18 lose on Normal,
and the ones that win mostly do so on spare lives. Whether that is the intended design is for the game designer (suggestions below).

random path the bot picks, so they only show every path wins. **The Bakery and its paths: 0 wins, 0 of 12,800 plays.**

Why: the bot builds a Bakery only on a spot that has two other spots within 1.5 tiles (the rule of the plain recipe, RULES 12.6). I measured
the run maps. Over all 42 stacks, the best spot has one other spot within 1.5 tiles on 34 stacks and none on 8; within 2.5 tiles (the Bakery
with Warm Ovens) the best spot has 1 other spot on 8 stacks, 2 on 32, 3 on 2. So a Bakery reaches one tower at most, usually none. About 88% to 92%
of runs still took the Bakery card, because the bots treat tower cards alike, so the card wasted a pick.

What if a bot builds the Bakery anyway, on a spot that reaches one other spot (`bakery_min=1`, same 50 seeds, Normal)? No unlocks: 25.6% falls to
15.3%. All cards: 12.8% falls to 7.8%. The Bakery takes a spot and gold from a real tower and boosts one tower. It is a trap on run maps.

## Suggestions for G3 (who decides what)

These are suggestions. I do not change rules.

**Game designer (RULES 14, section 12 run targets)**

1. **Decide whether a run may be won by outliving the boss.** Today the boss costs 20 lives, exactly the Normal starting lives, and Tea Break gives 5. Options, each measured or
   close to it: (a) leave it, since a good run does build a buffer; (b) make Tea Break weaker or once per run (3 lives: 25.6% to 19.1%; 1 life: 13.3%), or cap lives; (c) change how many
   lives the boss takes; (d) give the towers a way to kill the boss (the board is full and maxed by wave 14, F6). The target should say which: "a win needs the boss killed" or "a win may use
   spare lives".
2. **Set the Normal win-rate bar from the best bots, not the average.** The named strategies average 24.8% on Normal, 51.5% for the best one (`economy_tall`), and 37.4% of random
   strategies win. A thoughtful player should do better than the bots. A bar such as "the best named strategy wins about 50% and the 16 average about 25%" would hold today; if the bar is
   higher, the boss and the early waves are the places to ease (the boss strength what-ifs: step 8 gives 32.3%, step 6 gives 43.8%).
3. **Daily bar.** The architecture's open question was whether "every daily seed for the 400 days after the report is won by at least one bot strategy" is the right bar. At 16
   named strategies it fails (70%). With 100 random strategies it is 97.5%, with 400 it is 99.0%, with 1500 it is 99.75%, and the one miss is a map (F7). I suggest the bar
   "a search of 400 random strategies wins at least 98% of the next 400 days", and that the map segments in F7 are fixed rather than the bar loosened.
4. **Run length.** Wins run 13.3 minutes on average (14.5 without early calls). Either set 14.1 to about 12 to 16 minutes, or lengthen the waves. The lever is the pace of waves 16 to 18
   (45 s a wave) and the middle bands (30 to 35 s). The 15 s build phase is only a convention (F4).
5. **Late gold.** From wave 14 the board is full and maxed. If the late run should keep gold meaningful: a gold use after tier 3 (for example more tiers, a spot card, or turning gold into
   lives), a lower late clear bonus, or fewer spots. The Piggy Bank (+10% interest, up to 50) is aimed at the wrong end of the run; gold is plentiful late and short early.
6. **Risk cards.** Their gold reward is worth 140 to 310 but their cost in lives is 0.7 to 6.6 a wave. Hurry Night and Gateless Night are nearly free for the bots, Moonlit Wager, Pip Parade and
   Crowded Night are expensive. If the reward should track the cost, raise Moonlit Wager and Pip Parade or cut Hurry and Gateless.

**Roguelite engineer (R3, cards and the run plan)**

7. **Tea Break dilution.** The 34 card pool lowers Tea Break's chance (economy 7 cards, not 5). If Tea Break stays, consider a Tea Break of its own weight or leaving the pool's economy
   cards at 5. The unlock cards halve the bots' win rate; most of them do not help a bot (F9), so consider whether they should look more attractive.
8. **The Bakery tower card is a trap** (F8). Options: leave it out of run drafts, offer it only when the map has a spot with two others in reach, or have the level designer put spots
   closer together in the segments.
9. **Sharp Pebbles is the best perk** (+6.3 points) because it buffs the one tower every run has. The other perks are 0 to +1.

**Level designer (L5, segments and packs)**

10. **Fix the map walls.** `mid_cross` with `top_fork` or `top_twin` (loses 62% to 70% by wave 6), `mid_ladder` (6.3% win rate), `bot_gate` and `bot_yard` (about 13%). The data for why
    is not in this report; I would start by looking at where the build spots sit against the roads, and at the gate sockets of those pieces. Better stacks win four times as often.
11. **Band 2 packs** `b2_slow_and_fast` (20.1% lost inside), `b2_night_wings` (15.6%), `b2_hard_shells` (13.6%) are the early hurdle. The band 6 packs for Moon Moth lose 80% to 86% of the
    runs that reach them, against 46% to 50% for Grumblesack.
12. The spot count on run maps is 9 to 11, and every spot is full and maxed by wave 14. More spots, or spots closer together, would also help the Bakery (F8).

**Balance analyst (B4)**

13. After G3's changes I will play the same seeds again so the numbers can be compared. The bots can be improved in three ways that would raise the floor: skip a card the run has no use for
    (the Bakery), sell and rebuild, and choose gates with a simulation instead of a route rule.

## Limits of these numbers

- The bots are a floor. They buy by a fixed rule, never sell except to place a tower type for the first time, place gates by a route rule, and do not read the wave preview. A person
  reading the preview should win more often.
- Pick styles are archetypes. The random strategies, which are not tied to a style, win more often than the average named one.
- Win rates depend on the number of seeds: 3,200 plays a setting. The standard error of a win rate is about 0.8 points.
- Early drafts are noisy in the card experiment, as the "+/- 2 to 4 points" says.
