# Kittyhm Skate

## Short description

Space for the street. Shift for the song. One smug cat, three tracks, all swag.

## Page body

Own the night, one beat at a time.

Kittyhm Skate is a music-led skate rhythm game about a turquoise street cat with
more confidence than common sense. Ollie over pink barriers, land on cyan rails,
and hit the beat while your board spins underneath you.

The rhythm lives in the street. Notes arrive alongside the obstacles, so you can
keep your eyes on the cat. Build your combo and the whole alley starts showing off:
pixel sparks, shock rings, fireworks, and an unreasonable amount of attitude.

### How to play

- **Space** — ollie. Tap for a short pop, hold for a higher arc.
- **Shift** — tap diamonds as they reach the cat's ring. Hold cyan long notes
  until their tails reach the ring. Either Shift key works.
- **Rails** — jump and land on them from above to grind automatically.
- **P / Esc** — pause. **R** — retry.
- **Tab** — compare the street rhythm view with the classic lane.
- **V** — full / reduced effects. **M** — mute.

Start with **Learn to Ride**, a 30-second practice track. Then take on the night tour:

1. **Alley Shift** — 128 BPM. Tap and ollie your way into the groove.
2. **Rooftop Radio** — 136 BPM. Sustained notes and grind rails join the mix.
3. **Midnight Overdrive** — 148 BPM. Put it all together and own the city.

There is no mid-song game over. A mistake breaks your combo; the music keeps going.
Finish a track to open the next, then return to chase better scores and an S rank.

### Make it feel right

Use headphones if you can. If the hits feel early or late, open **Settings** from
the home screen and press **C** for the tap-to-audio timing check. You can also
adjust the input offset manually. Music level and hit sounds have separate options.

Keyboard required. For browser play, click the game first and use fullscreen for
the clearest view. Your best scores and settings are saved locally by your browser
or desktop build. Clearing browser site data removes that local progress.

### Game Off 2026 — SHIFT

Shift is the rhythm key, and the music's drop shifts the street into its neon
dimension. Every jump, note and scene transition follows the same music timeline.
