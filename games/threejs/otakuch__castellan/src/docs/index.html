A holding in the marches · 1327

Castellan

Cut the ground, raise the walls, and then let a day run through the place to see whether anyone can actually live in it.

  1. GroundGenerate a valley, then raise, lower, flatten and smooth it by hand. The water finds its own level.
  2. BuildWalls, towers, a gatehouse, cottages, a mill, fields and pens. Arrows on the ground show which way each door and wall face.
  3. RunEveryone you housed goes to work: field hands, traders, monks, the watch on the walls, flocks out to the common and back at dusk. Build nothing and nobody comes.
