## Chromeless recording: excursion and reported recovery

A separate recording has no Safari address bar. Held sampled frames were inspected. The board enlarges and moves down around 2.95-3.15 seconds; later return is mainly an upward shift and is not exact restoration of the initial board geometry. Score and preview sizes stay approximately constant.

The owner reports an intentional pinch-only recovery attempt failed and a tap recovered the view. Pixels establish a content/layout excursion and return. They do not alone establish global browser magnification, viewport-scale telemetry, exact contacts or a double tap. Absence of browser chrome does not by itself establish Home Screen execution or a new OS attestation. This remains a bounded recovery candidate, not a confirmed root cause or pinned-build defect. The separate Safari recording shows a different recovery sequence.

## Safari magnification and pinch-associated return

A separate recording shows Safari chrome and the game's address through gameplay. Sampled transition and recovery frames were inspected. The described inspection is held evidence; no recording was replayed for this record.

Around 23.1-23.5 seconds the board, score and preview grow consistently by approximately 1.6 times while browser/system chrome retains size. The enlarged page persists through about 26.8 seconds. Shrinkage around 27-29 seconds briefly undershoots and returns to approximately the earlier board/score/preview geometry; gameplay continues. Approximate pixel ratios and sample brackets are not exact gesture timestamps or viewport telemetry.

The owner identifies the recovery as a pinch and reports ability to zoom once in each session but not a second time after recovery. The recording does not itself measure a second attempt or session reset. This recording measures one enlargement/return sequence, not universal repeatability. Native Safari magnification and pinch-associated return are evidenced for this run. Exact double-tap recovery, a still-enlarged reload followed by recovery, and both quick taps' delivery are not demonstrated.
