## The worst first-frame hitch

On 10-27 every run had one frame of 33 to 53 ms in the first window. Now the worst frame in any run is 14.8 to 25.0 ms, and the first window reads 8.3 to 14.8 ms at worst. The 16 to 25 ms frames sit in windows with 72 to 87 live, when the crowd forms, and they are single frames (the window p99 stays at 8.3 to 11.1 ms). So the first-frame hitch is smaller (at most 25 ms against 33 to 53 ms) but not gone. One frame of 25 ms is above the 20 ms line for that frame only. It is not over the p99 budget.
