KITTYHM SKATE — THE NIGHT TOUR

Windows: keep KittyhmSkate.exe and KittyhmSkate.pck together, then run the exe.
Browser: click the game first. Keyboard required. Use fullscreen for a clear view.

SPACE: ollie. Tap for a short pop, hold for the full arc.
SHIFT: tap diamonds at the cat's ring; hold long cyan notes to their tail.
RAILS: jump and land on them to grind automatically.
ENTER: start / next track / resume. R: retry. P or ESC: pause.
TAB: street / classic rhythm layout. V: reduce effects. M: mute.
S: settings from home or pause. T: warmup from home / track selection.
F1: credits from home / track selection.

Start with the 30-second Learn to Ride warmup. Then complete three tracks:
Alley Shift (128 BPM), Rooftop Radio (136 BPM), Midnight Overdrive (148 BPM).
There is no mid-song game over. Finish a track to open the next, then chase scores.

If the timing feels early or late: home -> S -> C, tap Shift to the click,
then Enter to apply the measured offset. Music volume and hit sounds are adjustable.

Progress is saved locally. In a browser, clearing site data removes saved progress.
