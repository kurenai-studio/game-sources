# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First public release: the prototype is live at [tanks.miksoft.pro](https://tanks.miksoft.pro).

### Minor Changes

- Team battles 7 vs 7, USSR vs Germany, 1943: Standard, Encounter and Assault modes with base capture
- A new procedural 900 × 900 m map every battle: five terrain types, eight named maps, weather, seasons
- 28 vehicles in five classes, each with its own armour, gun, speed, view range and camouflage
- World of Tanks style armour: impact angle, normalisation, ricochet, weak zones, AP / APCR / HE shells
- Modules, crew, consumables, fire, ammo rack detonation and ramming
- Spotting with camouflage, bushes, line of sight and shared team vision
- Team bots with roles: scouts, strike groups, tank destroyers and SPGs
- Arcade, sniper and artillery views, penetration-coloured reticle, gun marker, reload ring
- Ribbons, medals, hit log, scoreboard, minimap, post-battle report, team signals
- Procedural sound with stereo panning
- Added this changelog
