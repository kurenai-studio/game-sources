# WoodLogic privacy policy

WoodLogic is a game made by Play, Inc. This policy says what WoodLogic does with information
about you. The short version: it collects nothing.

Effective 22 October 2026.

## What WoodLogic collects

Nothing. WoodLogic has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no crash reports and no cloud saves.
It does not collect, send or share any information about you or your phone, with us or with
anyone else.

## What WoodLogic saves on your phone

To work as a game, WoodLogic keeps a few small files in its own private storage on your phone:

- `progress.json`: the level you are on, your stars and best times, the helpers you own and have
  used, your daily streak and dates, the woods and metals you have unlocked, your stats, and a
  few game flags.
- `settings.json`: sound, music, symbols, and the wood and metal you picked.
- `next_level.json`: the layout of the next level, so that it starts faster.
- If `progress.json` or `settings.json` is damaged, WoodLogic keeps what it can read and sets the
  damaged copy aside once, named with `.bad` on the end. `next_level.json` is simply deleted and
  made again.
- The game engine's graphics cache, kept in the game's own storage, which holds only drawing
  data.

Nothing in these files says who you are. They never leave your phone, and other apps cannot
read them. Android's backup is turned off for WoodLogic, so they are not copied to Google's
backup either.

**To clear them:** in WoodLogic, open Settings and choose Reset progress (this keeps your
settings). Or open your phone's Settings, Apps, WoodLogic, Storage, and choose Clear data (the
names vary a little between phones). Uninstalling WoodLogic removes everything.

## Internet and permissions

WoodLogic does not use the internet and asks for no permissions.

Three buttons hand an address to another app on your phone, and the game itself sends nothing:

- **Send feedback** opens a new message in your own email app, addressed to bartbasi@gmail.com.
  The last line of the draft names the game's version, your phone's model and its Android
  version, so that problems can be looked into. You can change or delete any of it, and nothing
  is sent unless you send it.
- **Source code** opens the game's source code page on GitHub in your browser.
- **Buy me a coffee** opens buymeacoffee.com in your browser. Whatever you do there is between
  you and Buy Me a Coffee.

## Other companies

Google Play, the App Store, GitHub and Buy Me a Coffee are outside this promise. If you get
WoodLogic from one of them, or open one of its pages, that company's own privacy policy
applies to what it collects.

## Children

WoodLogic collects nothing from anyone, children included.
