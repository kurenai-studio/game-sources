# Response: Phase 2 world critic (docs/critic/p2-world.md)

New frames are in `shots/p2b/`: the 6 default spots × 4 times, plus `river`, `lake` and a new close spot `vlow` (low over the village heart). Every run printed `ANGLE (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 … D3D11)` and logged no shader or console errors.

| # | Critic item | What changed | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Village is sparse boxes on an empty lawn | The village plan was rewritten (`src/world/villageplan.ts`). Everything is stamped onto a 2 m occupancy grid, so nothing overlaps.<br>**Streets:** joined terraces of 2–6 cottages sit right on the street in the heart. Semis and detached cottages with front gardens sit further out. About half the houses have back extensions (wings) and sheds.<br>**Square and green:** a paved market square on the crossroads, with a market cross, pump and stalls. The green moved to the north-west quarter and has cottages facing it.<br>**Infill:** the land behind the streets is filled with hedged orchards, paddocks (with field shelters), allotments (with sheds and greenhouses), barns with yards, copses and flower meadows.<br>**Ground:** a mottled meadow is painted under everything, so no bare lawn is left.<br>482 buildings in total, up from 119. | fixed |
| 2 | Garden plots are floating black wire rectangles | Every garden is the plot behind its own house, as wide as the frontage, so plots tile like burgage strips. A plot cannot exist without its house.<br>**Bounds:** hedges and low drystone walls with coping, drawn as geometry with no ink (`uber(ID.fence, -1)`). A dark green or stone line is painted under them.<br>**Contents:** each plot has lawn stripes, a patio, flower borders, veg rows in two or three crops, a path, a shed or greenhouse and small trees. They are painted into a new 0.26 m/texel village detail map (`src/world/villagemap.ts`), sampled by the terrain shader. | fixed |
| 3 | Khaki haze, flat mustard sky, olive greens | **Sky:** the golden sky now runs from a blue zenith through a pale blue mid-sky (the muddy band about 20° up is gone) to a peach-cream horizon. It has a stronger warm glow toward the sun.<br>**Haze:** the fog colour is peach-cream. `applyFog` warms and brightens it toward the sun and cools it to blue-lilac away from the sun. Its optical depth now eases in (quadratic near, linear far), so foreground darks stay clean.<br>**Grade:** the world tint is less orange (greens stay green), the highlight split-tone is more neutral and the paper grain is halved. | fixed |
| 4 | Trees not hazed with the ground; all the same lime | All foliage already went through the same `applyFog`. | 5 | down_blue streaks, ghost circles, dark wedge | **Streaks:** the sun shadow fades out through blue hour (`shadowVis`: `uNight` 0.55→0.95). The el 3° shadows were stretched into pale streaks. I did not isolate its root cause, so it may still be possible to provoke. | fixed (wedge: not reproduced) |

## Perf (unverified: the GPU may have been contended)
At 1600×900 the frame times were 7.9 ms (`down`), 11.5 ms (500 m), 17.5 ms (200 m) and 21.2 ms (`vlow`, 4.0 M tris, mostly near card trees).

Draw calls are unchanged at about 23–37. All new geometry is merged:
- the village near and far LODs;
- one bounds mesh for hedges, walls and graves;
- the church and the windmill as their own meshes.

The plan uses a 400² occupancy grid, so it stays linear with no pairwise checks. Boot to ready is about 5.5 s, roughly 1 s more than before (village plan plus the 3072² detail map).
