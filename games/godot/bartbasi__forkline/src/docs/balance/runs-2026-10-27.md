## Suggestions

These are suggestions. I do not change rules or levels.

1. **Nothing to tune for the two misses.** `towers_tall` on 600 seeds is 73.5%, and `mid_eye` over whole runs is 0.80. If the DM wants the bars to read as met, the choice is the game designer's: state the segment bar for whole runs of all of its stacks, and state the best strategy bar on 600 seeds. Both would be changes to RULES 14.8, not to a bot.
2. **`mid_eye`'s weak stacks** (`bot_square` with four tops, and `top_meet, mid_eye, bot_porch`, 0.62 to 0.74 times all runs) lose at the boss or in the middle waves, not early. No bar applies to them. If the level designer wants `mid_eye` to match its neighbors, those five stacks are where to look.
3. **Phase C and the forced `mid_eye` runs** (`run_b4.sh C`, `run_b4.sh D`) took about 4 and 3 hours with 6 processes. A faster check after a segment change is to force only the stacks of that segment.
4. **The largest wave** is 2-2's waves 9 and 10 (116 live creatures, p99 1.0 ms on this PC). The render engineer can measure it on the Fold5 with `level_r2_l2_w9.json`. If the sim's 99th percentile is over 2 ms there, the sim engineer speeds up first (ARCHITECTURE 9.2). The run file is the one to test if runs are suspect.

## Limits of these numbers

- The bots are a floor. They never aim, sell to rebuild or read the preview (with `boss_aim=1` they won 12.5 points more in B4).
- A standard error is not the whole story for a bar met by a hair. The two misses are 0.17 and 0.02 standard errors away. The same runs on another 200 seeds would give other numbers around the same true value.
- The peak creature counts are the most crowded play found in a random search, not a proof that nothing is more crowded. They stopped rising with more tries.
- Tick times are this PC, headless, with events off. The Fold5 decides.
- The extra seeds 201 to 600 for `towers_tall`, the whole-run `mid_eye` stacks and the saved games are additions beyond TUNING_RUNS 9.6's list. They changed no bar.
