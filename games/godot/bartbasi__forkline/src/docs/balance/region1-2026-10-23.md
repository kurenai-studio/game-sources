# Balance report: region 1, Lantern Hill

Date: 2026-10-23. Author: balance-analyst. For: the game designer and the level designer.
Levels: r1_l1 to r1_l6 as in the repo at commit 6d1570d. data/levels/, data/stats/ and src/sim/ are the same as in commit da40b54.
Sim: the upgrade reload cap (RULES 5.2) has landed. It is in commit da40b54, and every number below was run on a tree that has it. The raw files are in `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-23/`.

Battles have no randomness, so a level plus the player's choices gives one result. The bots search over the choices (towers, spots, upgrades, paths, gate, targeting, early calls). Every random draw is seeded, and the seeds are in section 7. "Random strategies" are worse than a thinking player. Read their win rates as a floor and use them to compare levels. Rates from 100 random plays carry an error of about 5 points.

## 1. Findings

1. **Levels 1-1 to 1-4 are far too easy.** A casual player who drops a tower on every spot and never upgrades wins 1-1, 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4 on Normal 100%, 98%, 100% and 100% of the time (section 4.1). A plain recipe, a Sling Post on every spot with no upgrades and no gate, earns all three stars on 1-1 and 1-4. One Sling Post upgraded twice to a Quickshot, with no gate, wins 1-4 with all 20 lives and all three stars. The waves follow the RULES 12.5 guide of about 0.8 times the gold on hand (section 4.9), but the guide ignores that towers are reused every wave. Suggestion S1 has tested numbers.
2. **The check "no single tower type earns three stars alone" fails on all six levels.** A Sling Post only build gets three stars on every level. A Catapult only build gets three stars on 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4. The level designer's 1-5 build is confirmed with numbers (section 4.3). Making the waves bigger does not help: Sling only keeps its three stars until the level is unwinnable for every build (section 4.9). Changing tower numbers did not open a window either. This needs a decision, not a wave tweak. See S2.
3. **The Sling Post is dominant and the Catapult is never needed.** The Sling Post is in 95% to 100% of the three-star builds on every level. A Catapult is in 38% to 77% of them, but a Sling only search finds three-star builds at the same rate as the any tower search on 1-1 to 1-5 (for example 56% of the builds tried on 1-4 against 56%), and at 27% against 32% on 1-6. Prize Pumpkin and Jam Pot are in 3% to 16% of three-star builds. They are the closest thing to dead towers in region 1 (section 4.5).
4. **Level 1-6 can be won, but only just, and the boss is the wall.** In each of the 12 best three-star builds saved, Grumblesack dies with 2 to 28 health, at most 3 road steps from the base (section 4.8). None of 750 random Normal plays won. Of the 150 that reached the boss wave, 1 beat it. Gold is not the limit: 11 of those 12 builds use all six spots, with 3 or 4 towers at tier 3, and 45 to 153 gold is unspent when the boss wave starts. A weaker boss is the lever that works (S3). More gold or one more spot barely moved anything.
5. **Level 1-6 star 3 ("Call 4 waves early") is not free.** The best three-star build, replayed with its early calls switched off, loses. So the early call gold is part of how the three-star builds win. Level 1-6 is still winnable with no early calls (998 of 3000 builds tried won, 953 of them with 2 stars).
6. **Level 1-5 is the first real test and it is fair.** Random Normal win rate is 28%, 59% to 68% when most of the build is Sling Posts, and 1% when it is mostly Catapults (which cannot hit Flitters). The hint on this level says so.
7. **A Relaxed player at one star can finish every level.** A plain recipe, "a Sling Post on every spot, then one upgrade each", wins all six levels on Relaxed (section 4.7). On 1-6 it wins by letting Grumblesack through (it costs 20 of 40 lives).
8. **Length is 4.5 to 7.5 minutes at 1x with no early calls.** Levels 1-2 to 1-6 are inside the 5 to 10 minute target. Level 1-1 is under it (4.5 minutes), which confirms the level designer's figure. Bigger waves also make levels longer (section 4.9).
9. **Speed is not a concern.** The most creatures on the map in about 54,000 plays was 45. A tick costs 18 to 47 microseconds on average and 49 to 120 at the 99th percentile on this PC (section 4.10).
10. **Same inputs, same result.** All 144 repeated plays matched, all 432 plays with a save and load in the middle of a wave matched, the search repeats exactly, and the numbers match between separate processes (section 4.11).
11. **Not covered yet.** Roguelite runs and the daily seed (src/run/ does not exist yet), and the other four towers (the Bell, Longbow Loft, Firework Tower and Bakery are not offered in region 1, and the sim does not run the Bell or the Bakery yet).

## 3. Suggestions

Everything in this section is a suggestion. The bots changed no rule and no level. The numbers come from what-if runs (section 4.9) that edit a copy of the level or of the tower numbers in memory.

**S1. Level designer: make 1-1 to 1-4 bigger.** Multiply every group's count by 1.5. On 1-4, twice the count gives a 108 creature wave, over the readable 80, so 1.5 is the safer step there.

| Level | Count | Random Normal win rate (typical style) | Random three-star rate | Length of a typical win | A three-star build still exists |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1-1 | 150% (wave 8: 16 to 24 Scamps) | 67% (now 98%) | 24% | 5.5 min (now 5.2) | yes |
| 1-2 | 150% | 62% (now 76%) | 14% | 5.8 min | yes |
| 1-3 | 150% | 56% (now 90%) | 2% | 6.3 min | yes |
| 1-4 | 150% | 90% (now 100%) | 5% | 6.1 min | yes |
| 1-4 | 200% | 59% | 3% | 6.4 min | yes |

Pick the count by the win rate you want. The full curve, up to 400% where the spawn limit allows, is in section 4.9. A bigger wave takes longer to spawn, so the levels also run longer. That lifts 1-1 over the 5 minute target. It does not fix finding 2.

**S2. Game designer and DM: decide how the "no single tower" check can pass.** The tests in section 4.9 show that wave size alone cannot do it. Two tower number changes did not either:

- Catapult splash +0.5 tile on every tier and path. Catapult only becomes viable (1-4 at 200%: 354 of 800 builds get three stars, was 0; 1-2 at 150%: 480, was 72). Sling only does not change (367 and 457 of 800), so a Sling only build still gets three stars.
- Sling Post damage x0.8. It lowers the Sling only three-star builds on 1-4 at 200% from 367 to 237 of 800 but does not remove them, and it makes 1-5 at 150% unwinnable for every build (0 of 800), because the Sling Post is the only tower that hits Flitters.

Options, with the contract cost of each:

- (a) Add a star 3 kind "Use at least 2 kinds of tower" (`kinds_at_least`). This makes the rule true by construction on any level that uses it. It needs RULES 10.3, `LevelData.STAR3_FIELDS`, `Battle._star3_met`, the star sentence and tests/levels/. It is the only option I found that is certain to work. A level has one star 3 rule, so the level's own challenge would give way to it.
- (b) Redesign so that a Sling Post only build fails on the mixed levels. The data say this needs a Catapult that is clearly better than a Quickshot on crowds, and waves that need both. The spawn limit of 120 creatures a wave stops 1-4 from getting dense enough with the current numbers.
- (c) Apply the check from region 2 on, where the Bell and the Longbow Loft exist, and write the exception into RULES 10 and the brief. Region 1 has only two attacking towers and the Sling Post does both jobs.

**S3. Level designer: weaken Grumblesack.** Setting the final wave strength from 110 to a lower number gives this (random plays, then the search, both at start gold 350):

| Final wave strength | Random plays that reached the boss wave | Of those, won | Search: 3-star builds in 1500 tries |
|---|---|---|---|
| 110 (now) | 20% | 1% | 952 of 3000 |
| 90 | 20% | 7% | 421 of 1500 |
| 70 | 22% | 21% | 478 of 1500 |
| 50 | 19% | 52% | 677 of 1500 |

At strength 70 the boss has 1400 health instead of 2200. Extra gold hardly helped (strength 110 with 550 gold: 1% of the random plays that reached the boss still won it). One extra build spot at four different cells left the boss win rate at 2% to 3% (section 4.9). So do not spend effort on gold or spots for 1-6. If a hard boss is the goal, 90 is the smallest step that moves the numbers.

**S4. Level designer: look at waves 4 to 9 of 1-6.** Random plays that fail do so mostly before the boss: 7% are lost in waves 1 to 3, 40% in waves 4 to 6, and 34% in waves 7 to 9. Only 20% reach the boss. The waves mix Flitters, Pips and Scamps in a way that catches Catapult heavy builds. A Sling Post heavy build is the only thing that works on 1-6, so the level teaches "build Slings" and not "use both towers".

**S5. Game designer: change the RULES 12.5 wave size guide.** "A wave's total health is about 0.8 times the gold a player could have" gives a free win with the current towers. At counts of 150% the same ratio is about 1.15 on average (1.0 to 1.3 wave by wave) on 1-1 to 1-3, and random Normal win rates fall to 56% to 67%. At 200% the ratio is about 1.37 and the rates are 23% to 58%. Level 1-5 sits at about 0.8 and is already hard because of the Flitters, so the ratio is not a stable difficulty measure across levels. Treat it as a starting point and tune with the bots.

**S6. Game designer: give Prize Pumpkin and Jam Pot a job.** They are in at most 16% of three-star builds. At tier 3 a Prize Pumpkin costs 230 for about 20 damage per second per target, and a Quickshot costs 200 for 40. The Catapult splash test above is the cheapest fix to try: it lifted the typical random win rate on 1-4 at 200% from 68% to 95%.

**S7. Level designer: star 3 on 1-2, 1-3 and 1-4 is nearly free.** "At most 4 towers" (1-2), "at most 3 towers" (1-3) and "use no gates" (1-4) are met by builds with only 1 to 3 towers. A three-star build with 2 towers exists on 1-2 and 1-3, and with 1 tower on 1-4 (section 4.3). Star 3 only bites if the level is also made harder (S1).

### 4.8 Level 1-6 and the boss
