## Full description (4,000 characters at most)

SumUp is a calm, clever number puzzle. Drag your finger across matching tiles to join them into one bigger number, then keep going and see how high you can climb.

HOW IT WORKS
Start a chain on any tile and slide to a neighbor with the same number, in any of eight directions. After the first pair, each next tile can match the last one or be double it. Lift your finger and the whole chain becomes one bigger tile, and every tile in it adds to your score. Join 2, 2, 4 and 8 and you get 16. Planning chains that add up just right is the trick. New tiles drop in from the top, and as your numbers grow, the smallest tiles clear away so the board keeps moving.

BOOSTERS WHEN YOU NEED THEM
Stuck? Spend some of your score on a booster:
- Hammer breaks any one tile
- Swap trades two tiles anywhere on the board
- Shuffle mixes up the whole board
- Double doubles any one tile
- Undo takes back your last move
Earn stars as your tiles climb past new milestones. Every three stars give you a free booster.

THREE LOOKS
Play in Gumdrop, soft and playful; Slate, clean and focused; or Nova, glowing and bright. Switch any time in Settings. Each one changes the whole game: the tiles, colors, buttons, effects and motion.

PLAY YOUR WAY
- No timers and no lives. Play for a minute or an hour.
- Your game saves after every move. Close the app and pick up right where you left off.
- Your best score and highest tile are always on show, so there is always a record to beat.
- A short guide inside the game explains scoring, stars and every booster.

FREE, PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
SumUp is free, and it always will be. No ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts and no tracking. It works fully offline, asks for no permissions and collects no data at all. Your game stays on your phone.

SumUp is free software under the GNU GPL version 3. The source code is at github.com/bartbasi/sumup.
