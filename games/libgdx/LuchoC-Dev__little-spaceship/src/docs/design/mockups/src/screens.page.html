little-spaceship · phase 06 · visual direction

Every screen in the flow

The six screens of 02-mvp-functional-spec.md, drawn at 480×270 from the same palette and the same two bitmap fonts as the game itself. They share one frame, fixed in 04-hud-layout.md: the title at 40,32, ten-pixel lines, sixteen pixels between entries, W4 and a marker for the selected one, and a 24 px safe area so nothing sits against the letterbox. What is not here is as deliberate as what is — no difficulty selector, no continue, no locked modes.

The flow

    Decisions these screens make

    Selection

    Colour and a marker

    The selected entry is W4 and carries a > twelve pixels to its left. R4 applies to menus too: a player who cannot separate amber from white still sees which line is live.

    Type

    Two fonts, no third

    font-title sets the screen title and the totals. Everything else is font-mini at a fixed six-pixel advance, which is why no column ever shifts as a number changes.

    Ground

    The city keeps burning

    Menu, victory and defeat sit over the level’s own parallax, checkered down so it reads as depth rather than content. The pause panel does the same to the frozen playfield.

    Absence

    What is not drawn

    No difficulty selector, no checkpoints, no profiles, no continue, no shop, no locked modes and no second ship. Every one of them is an explicit MVP exclusion, and drawing a greyed-out version would promise something the build does not have.

    What the checks say

      Generated from docs/design/mockups/src/ by build.py, and verified by check.js. The ship on the selection screen is drawn at ×3 inside the framebuffer, which is still an integer factor of a logical pixel.
