Reviewer: qa-reviewer. Build: HEAD d8ccfde, in a clean copy made with `git archive`, renamed so its user:// folder stayed apart from the real one. Windows only; the Fold5 was not connected. The balance analyst ran bot jobs on this machine at the same time, so no timing or performance is judged here.

Severity scale: blocker (Critical), major (High), minor (Medium), polish (Low).

No blocker and no major found. Every level and Twist of regions 2 to 4 loads, plays through the real App and BattleScreen to a win and a loss, and records exactly what the rules say. Runs and dailies play to a win and a loss, record their counters and the daily record, and resume to the exact tick after a real process kill, mid-wave and with a draft open. Map cells stay at 49.1 dp on the cover screen and 59.8 dp or more on the inner screen in every state, on every map.

## 2. Findings

### Minor

**m1. Info has no Twist rules and only one paragraph on runs and the daily.** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/info_screen.gd). The contract (ARCHITECTURE 3.6) says InfoScreen holds "the rules of Twists, runs, cards and the daily (RULES 13 to 15)", and RULES 9.6 says Info holds the full rules. Steps: front door, Info, scroll to the end. Expected: what a Twist is and how it unlocks (3 stars, no stars of its own, a win on either difficulty clears it), the card kinds and the risk card rule, and the daily's best result and streak. Actual: no Twist text at all. "More ways to play" has two sentences on runs and the daily. Targeting modes (First, Last, Strong), which 4-2 teaches, are not in Info either.

**m2. Under the No Selling Twist the tower panel still offers to sell.** Owner: ui-engineer (tower_panel.gd). Steps: 4-4 Twist, build a Sling Post, tap it. Expected: the panel shows that towers cannot be sold. Actual: "Sell value 100", "Full refund: bought this build phase." and "Sell for 100"; a first tap arms "Tap again to sell for 100"; the second tap is refused with "Towers cannot be sold on this level." Nothing breaks, but the panel invites a sale twice before saying no.

**m3. Under a tier cap Twist the tower panel offers tier 3 paths.** Owner: ui-engineer (tower_panel.gd). Steps: 3-5 Twist (Tier 2 Cap; 1-3's Twist is the same), build a Sling Post, upgrade it to tier 2, tap it. Expected: the panel shows the tower is at the cap (`upgrade_options()` returns the refusal `tier_cap`). Actual: "Pick a path for tier 3. The choice is permanent." with both paths and costs; picking one shows "Choose Quickshot for 200"; confirming is refused with "This level caps the tower tier."

### Polish

**p1. No Gates Twist: a tap on a socket says "No gates left in hand."** Owner: ui-engineer (battle_screen.gd:1200 checks `level.gates`, not the Twist's `set_gates`). Steps: 2-4 Twist, tap any socket. The gate chip is hidden, which is right; the message reads as if gates ran out. Suggest saying nothing, as on a level with no gates.

**p2. Upgrade rows with odd labels.** Owner: ui-engineer (battle_text.gd). The Night Bell path card reads "Hits: Walkers → Both", while the tower panel for the same Bell says "Affects: Walkers and flyers". The Skyrocket path card reads "Splash: 0.75 tiles → 0 tiles" for a one-target rocket (RULES 5.7: "none").

**p3. Fair Trade text.** Owner: roguelite-engineer (data/cards/cards.json). "Selling a tower refunds 90% of what you paid." A tower bought in the current build phase still refunds 100% (RULES 4.6, 14.5: Fair Trade raises the 70% to 90%). Suggest "Selling refunds 90% instead of 70%."

**p4. A daily finished after midnight says "Best today" for the day before.** Owner: ui-engineer (run_end_panel.gd:44, run_card.gd:126). From code reading, not run: start a daily, leave it saved, move the phone's date to the next day, resume and finish it. The panel shows the run's own day (right, RULES 15) with "Best today:" and that day's best. Suggest "Best that day" or "Best on 2026-10-25" when the day is not today.

**p5. "Offline, no data" in About names only the coffee button.** Owner: ui-engineer (about_screen.gd). The source link and Send feedback also hand an address to another app. The privacy policy has to name all three (CLAUDE.md); About does not, but a reader may take the sentence as the full list. Question for the DM: keep the playbook wording?

**p6. One audio leak at exit.** Owner: audio-engineer (src/audio/). `godot --headless --verbose --path . --quit-after 400` on the real main scene reports "Leaked instance: AudioStreamPlaybackWAV" and "AudioStreamWAV", 2 ObjectDB instances, and an orphan StringName "Music". Harmless at exit; worth clearing so new leaks stand out.
