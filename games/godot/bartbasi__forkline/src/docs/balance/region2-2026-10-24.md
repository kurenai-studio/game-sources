# Balance report: the region 2 pass (B1)

Date: 2026-10-24. For the game designer and the level designer. Written by the balance analyst.

Code and data: commit 9a1bbba (HEAD), played from a clean copy of that commit, because other agents had uncommitted
edits in the working tree while this ran (see "Notes on this run" at the end). Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24-b1/`,
one JSON file per job. Every table below comes from `summary.gd` with `pass=1` run on that folder. The same output, with
the plain recipe of every level and the simplest three-star build of every level written out, is
`docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24-b1/summary.md`.

Covered: all 12 campaign levels (regions 1 and 2) and the 12 Twists. Each search tried 2500 builds, except the Twist
searches (1500). All numbers are Normal unless a table says Relaxed.

## Findings

**Short version.** Region 2 can be won, three stars can be reached on every level, random play almost never gets three
stars, length and speed are fine, and the same seed always plays the same. One check fails: **"no super tower" fails on
all six region 2 levels (2-1 to 2-6).** One attacking tower plus Bells earns all three stars on each of them: the Sling Post
on five, the Catapult on 2-3. Region 1 still passes (with the caveat in F7). F1 shows each failure and what I tested to
fix it: a changed star 3 rule fixes 2-1, 2-2, 2-4 and 2-5, a few Flitters fix 2-3, and **no rule I tried fixes 2-6**.

### F1. No super tower fails on all six region 2 levels (the Sling Post, and on 2-3 the Catapult)

Each level has two searches per attacking tower (2500 builds each, two seeds). A search that may use only one attacking
tower plus the level's Bells found three-star wins here:

| Level | Star 3 rule now | Tower that wins alone | Three-star builds in 2500 (seed a, seed b) | A typical winning build |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-1 | Call 6 waves early | Sling Post | 565, 360 | 7 Sling Posts, 1 or 2 at tier 3, 20 lives |
| 2-2 | Have at most 6 towers | Sling Post | 0, 0 in 2500 builds; 0 and 252 in two 6000 build searches (F7) | 3 Sling Posts at tier 3, 3 Lullaby Bells, gate at (2,2), 19 lives |
| 2-3 | No tower above tier 2 | Pumpkin Catapult | 841, 923 | 4 to 6 Catapults, 1 or 2 Lullaby Bells, all tier 2, 20 lives |
| 2-4 | Use at most 1 gate | Sling Post | 730, 473 | 5 Sling Posts at tier 3, 1 or 2 Bells, 20 lives |
| 2-5 | Build no Longbow Lofts | Sling Post | 336, 273 | 4 Sling Posts at tier 3, 1 or 2 Bells, 20 lives |
| 2-6 | Use at most 3 kinds of tower | Sling Post | 373, 0 (see F7) | 4 to 6 Sling Posts at tier 3, 2 or 3 Bells, 20 lives |

Why it happens: none of these rules takes away something the lone tower needs.

- **2-1:** early calls cost nothing a lone Sling Post needs. The Catapult alone cannot win 2-1 (all 5000 builds lost; the
  best reached the last wave), because 2-1 has Flitters in waves 6, 8 and 10 and the Catapult cannot hit flyers.
- **2-2:** a lone Sling Post with three Bells fits under "at most 6 towers" with room to spare (3 + 3). It only comes out of
  a long search, which is why the first 2500 build searches missed it (F7).
- **2-3:** it is the only region 2 level with no Flitters. The Catapult is never shut out, and at tier 2 with a Bell or
  two it wins with 20 lives. The tier 2 cap only stops the Sling Post (which needs tier 3 here: its 2500-build searches
  win but never meet the rule).
- **2-4:** a lone Sling Post needs one gate at most, so "at most 1 gate" limits nothing.
- **2-5:** "Build no Longbow Lofts" bans the one tower that never won alone.
- **2-6:** a lone attacker plus Bells is 2 kinds, and the rule allows 3.

**The root cause is flyers.** The Catapult and the Longbow Loft cannot hit flyers (their info cards say so). Every
region 2 level except 2-3 has Flitters, so the Sling Post is the only tower that can stop them: it is required on 2-1, 2-2,
2-4, 2-5 and 2-6, and nothing else wins those levels without it. A lone Sling Post is enough on every one of them
(on 2-2 only a long search shows it, F7). The Catapult alone wins nothing on 2-1, 2-2, 2-4, 2-5 and 2-6 (every build lost).
The Longbow Loft alone never earns more than 1 star (2-3: 11 lives at best) and wins nothing on 2-4 to 2-6. 2-3 has no
flyers, which is why the Catapult can carry it. Only the Sling Post and, on 2-3, the Catapult are super towers.

**What I tested as replacements** (the sections "What-if" and "Longer checks" below have every search and seed). A rule
works if the any-tower search still finds three-star wins and no one-attacker search does. The ones I recommend were
checked again with a 6000 build lone-attacker search, because 2500 builds can miss (F7).

| Level | Rule tried | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 2-1 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 1068 three-star builds. Sling Post alone: 0. It wins 1155 builds in 2500, and 2623 in a 6000 build check, but none meets the rule. |
| 2-1 | Have at most 5 towers | Fails. Sling Post alone: 366 three-star builds. |
| 2-3 | Have at most 5 towers | Fails. Catapult alone: 953 three-star builds. |
| 2-3 | Upgrade no towers | No build at all earns three stars. |
| 2-3 | Build no Pumpkin Catapults | Fails. Sling Post alone: 415 three-star builds (it needs tier 3 and the old tier cap is gone). |
| 2-3 | Add Flitters to waves 4, 6, 8 (4, 5, 6) and keep the tier 2 cap | Works but too tight: 10 three-star builds in 2500, best 15 lives. |
| 2-3 | Add Flitters to waves 4, 6, 8 (2, 3, 4) and keep the tier 2 cap | **Works.** Any tower: 393 three-star builds, best 19 lives. Catapult alone wins 1224 builds, at most 11 lives under the rule (15 needed); in a 6000 build check, 2937 wins, 2510 under the rule, best 11 lives, 0 three-star. Sling Post alone wins but never meets the rule. |
| 2-2 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 972 three-star builds. Sling Post alone: 0 in two 6000 build searches (1620 and 1539 wins, none under the rule). |
| 2-2 | Have at most 4 towers | **Works.** Any tower: 841. Sling Post alone: 0 in the same two searches. (Here a count limit works, because the lone Sling Post needs 3 Slings and 3 Bells.) |
| 2-4 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 763 three-star builds. Sling Post alone: 0, also in a 6000 build check (2824 wins, none under the rule). |
| 2-4 | Have at most 5 towers | Fails. Sling Post alone: 617. |
| 2-5 | No tower above tier 2 | **Works.** Any tower: 712. Sling Post alone: 0, also in a 6000 build check (2266 wins, none under the rule). |
| 2-5 | Have at most 5 towers | Fails. Sling Post alone: 147. |
| 2-6 | No tower above tier 2 | No build earns three stars (the boss needs tier 3). |
| 2-6 | Lose no lives | Fails. Sling Post alone: 529. |
| 2-6 | Build no Sling Posts, or at most 4 towers | No build earns three stars. |
| 2-6 | Build no Lullaby Bells | **Fails the longer check.** The 2500 build search looked good (any tower 942 three-star builds, lone Sling Post 0), but a 6000 build lone Sling Post search found 1500 three-star builds, none using a Bell, up to 20 lives. |

A tower count limit works only where the lone Sling Post needs many towers (2-2: 4 or fewer stops it). On 2-1, 2-3, 2-4
and 2-5 a lone attacker wins with 4 or 5 towers, so the limit does not. A tier cap works where a lone Sling Post needs
tier 3 (2-1, 2-2, 2-4, 2-5), and not where the boss needs tier 3 from every build (2-6). Suggestions S1 to S3 below turn
this into proposals.

### F3. Gate placement decides region 2 on Normal

The plain recipe wins on these shares of its gate placements, and loses with no gates on every level from 2-2 up:

| Level | Placements that seal no entrance | Wins | No gates | Best placement | Lives |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2-1 | 4 | 4 | won, 12 lives | (2,6) | 18 |
| 2-2 | 4 | 1 | **lost in wave 3 of 10** | (2,2) | 19 |
| 2-3 | 4 | 1 | lost in wave 6 of 10 | (3,4) | 15 |
| 2-4 | 9 | 3 | lost in wave 4 of 11 | (1,3) and (1,7) | 14 |
| 2-5 | 9 | 1 | lost in wave 4 of 11 | (1,4) and (5,8) | 15 |
| 2-6 | 13 | 6 | lost in wave 6 of 12 | (5,8) | 13 |

Every bar of RULES 12.6 is met, but a first-time player who places a gate in the wrong socket, or none, loses on 2-2 and
2-3 on three placements out of four. 2-2 is the worst: wave 3 has 46 creatures after waves of 6 and 11, and the recipe
loses there with no gate. The designers' target for a region's first levels is about 85% first-try wins, so 2-2 in
particular rests on the hint and the highlighted socket being clear. (The bots cannot judge that.)

### F4. Every other bar passes

- **Plain recipe, Normal:** all 12 levels meet the bar. Region 1: 16, 19, 17, 20, 19 lives on 1-1 to 1-5 (bar 15) and a win
  on 1-6 (15 lives). Region 2: 18 and 19 lives on 2-1 and 2-2 (bar 15), wins on 2-3 to 2-6 (15, 14, 15 and 13 lives).
  Region 1 equals the old region 1 recipe: `compare_old=1` found no difference on any of the six levels, and the six plain
  files equal the ones of the 2026-10-24 bots check apart from the new `lives_at` field and the save hashes.
- **Plain recipe, Relaxed:** wins on all 12 levels, with no gates and with its best placement. Thinnest: 2-6 no gates,
  5 lives (above); 2-5 no gates 18; 2-4 no gates 19.
- **All three stars:** found on all 12 levels. The first three-star build turned up after 51 to 894 builds (2-5: 888, 1-2: 894,
  1-1: 878, 1-6: 875), so 2500 builds is enough but not by a wide margin on those four.
- **Random play:** three stars in under 10% everywhere. Highest: 1-1 with 5.1% (38 of 750, all from the
  casual style). All others are 2.3% or lower.
- **Length:** the plain recipe's best win takes 5.4 to 8.4 minutes at 1x with no early calls (2-4 the longest at 8.4;
  1-1, 2-1 and 1-4 the shortest at 5.4 to 5.5). All inside 5 to 10.
- **Largest wave:** at most 80 creatures (2-2), under the ceiling of 120 and at the readability aim of 80. 1-4 has 74, 2-1 76,
  2-3 78.
- **Most creatures on the map at once:** 112 (2-2, in the search plays), against a planned peak of 200. The plain recipe's
  best play peaks at 62 (2-2).
- **Time per tick** (this PC, headless): mean at most 74 microseconds, 99th percentile at most 267 microseconds, worst tick
  571 microseconds, on the most crowded plays. The budget is 2 ms at the 99th percentile. The Fold5 decides, not this PC.
- **Twists:** every one of the 12 is won on Normal by some strategy (search wins from 146 to 1147 of 1500).
  See the Twist table. The plain recipe alone does not win 1-5, 2-4 or 2-5's Twists; that is not a bar.
- **Same seed, same play:** all 24 determinism jobs (12 levels, plain and Twist) played identically: 24 of 24 repeated plays,
  72 of 72 replays through a save and load in the middle of a wave, and the repeated 60 build search. The 12 jobs run again
  in a new process gave the same chain hash.

### F5. Towers: what the three-star wins use

From the any-tower searches (9853 three-star wins on 12 levels; table "Towers and paths" below):

- **Pumpkin Catapult:** in every three-star win. **Sling Post:** in 94% (9227). Both are in nearly every winning mix.
- **Longbow Loft:** in 2087 wins, but only on 2-3 (944 of 993), 2-4 (291 of 1001) and 2-6 (852 of 852). On its own it is the
  weakest attacker: 1 star at best, and no win at all on 2-4, 2-5 and 2-6. The any-tower wins use it in nearly all builds on
  2-3, in all on 2-6 and in 29% on 2-4.
- **Lullaby Bell:** in 1307 wins (2-2: 235 of 981, 2-3: 555 of 993, 2-4: 401 of 1001, 2-5: 116 of 897, 2-6: 0 of 852). Never
  required by an any-tower three-star win; the lone-attacker builds on 2-3, 2-4, 2-5 and 2-6 lean on it instead.
- **Tier 3 paths:** Quickshot 4133 wins, Stone Sling 3046, Jam Pot 2668, Prize Pumpkin 1997, Giant Bow 722 (2-6 only),
  Triple Bow 409 (406 on 2-6, 3 on 2-4), Night Bell 66, Hush Bell 34. Every path appears at least once, so none is
  unreachable. The Bell paths and the Triple Bow are thin: a flag for the game designer, not a failed check.
- **Not yet testable:** Firework Tower and Bakery are not offered before region 3 and region 4, so their three-star wins
  wait for later passes. Across regions 1 and 2, every tower type the levels offer appears in a three-star win.

### F6. Region 1 again

Everything region 1 held before still holds, on the new seeds (all 12 levels in one table below). The closest a lone
attacker comes to three stars on a region 1 level is 14 lives against the 15 needed, in a win that meets the star 3 rule

region 1 either.

### F7. About the searches: they can miss, and one did

A search that finds nothing does not prove nothing exists. Same-kind searches on different seeds disagreed three times in
this pass:

- **2-2, Sling Post alone.** Seed a (2500 builds) lost every build. Seed b (2500) won 309 builds, best 13 lives, two stars.
  Two longer searches (6000 builds, folder `extra/`): seed 742201 won 851 builds, all one star, best 10 lives under the rule;
  seed 742202 found **252 three-star builds**, best 19 lives (3 Sling Posts at tier 3 and 3 Lullaby Bells, 6 towers).
  So the 2500 build "pass" on 2-2 was a miss, and 2-2 fails too.
- **2-6, Sling Post alone.** Seed a found 373 three-star wins, seed b lost all 2500 builds.
- **1-1, Sling Post alone.** Seed a: 893 builds met the star 3 rule (best 10 lives); seed b: none.

The search starts from random builds and edits the best ones, so a seed that never lands on a winning start gets stuck.
For region 1 the earlier reports ran 3000 and 8000 build searches and an exhaustive check of 1-3 and found no lone-tower
three-star win, so I trust the region 1 "pass". For region 2, a "pass" from two 2500 build searches is only evidence.
Where the lone tower can win at all (the Sling Post on a Flitter level), a 6000 build check is the minimum; the rules I
recommend in S1 and S2 all passed one. Suggestion S8 asks for a higher minimum in RULES 12.6.

The other lone attackers on region 2 cannot win a level with Flitters (the best Catapult and Longbow builds lose); that
holds by the info cards ("Can't hit flyers"), not just by search.

## Suggestions

I report and suggest. I changed no rule, level or stat. Each suggestion names who decides.

**S1. Game designer and level designer: change the star 3 rule of 2-1, 2-2, 2-4 and 2-5.** "No tower above tier 2"
passes all four (any tower: 1068, 972, 763 and 712 three-star builds; the lone Sling Post: 0, each checked with a 6000 build
search). For 2-2 "Have at most 4 towers" also passes (841; lone Sling Post 0), and it keeps 2-2 close to what it has now.
Costs of the tier cap: 2-1's star 3 no longer teaches calling early (the level still can, through the hint and the bonus
gold); 2-4's no longer teaches "fewer gates"; 2-5's no longer teaches "bubbles". 2-3, 3-5 and these four would all carry
a tier cap. If that is too many, the other levers are wave changes that make a lone Sling Post lose, which I can test if
given the waves. A tower count limit does not work on 2-1, 2-4 and 2-5 (a lone Sling Post wins with 4 or 5 towers there).

**S2. Level designer: give 2-3 a few Flitters.** 2-3 is the only region 2 level without them, so the Catapult carries it
alone at tier 2 with Bells. Tested: Flitters added to waves 4, 6 and 8, **2, 3 and 4 of them**, one group each, entrance 1,
starting 2 s into the wave, one every 0.4 s, keeping "No tower above tier 2". Result: any tower finds 393 three-star
builds (best 19 lives), the Catapult alone wins but at most 11 lives under the rule, the Sling Post alone cannot meet the
rule, the Longbow Loft alone wins nothing. 4, 5 and 6 Flitters is too many (10 three-star builds, best 15 lives). The level
files used are `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-24-b1/whatif/levels/r2_l3_flitters_b.json` (and `r2_l3_flitters.json`). Largest wave
stays 78. This also changes what 2-3 teaches (Armor blocks small hits, Longbows ignore it): the Sling Post is needed too.

**S3. Game designer: 2-6 needs a decision, and a rule alone will not do it.** None of the rules I tried works. A tier cap
or at most 4 towers leaves no three-star win at all. "Lose no lives" and "Build no Lullaby Bells" let the lone Sling Post
keep winning (529 and 1500 three-star builds; the Bell ban looked good in a 2500 build search and failed a 6000 build
check). The lone Sling Post wins 2-6 with 4 to 6 Slings at tier 3, with or without Bells. If the boss lesson is "pierce
its shell", Ironshell's armor (6) is not stopping tier 3 Sling Posts. I can run what-ifs on Ironshell's armor or health or
on Sling Post tier 3 numbers (`run.gd` takes `enemies=PATH` and `towers=PATH`) if the designer names the numbers.

**S4. Game designer, for G2: check 2-2.** The plain recipe wins on 1 of 4 gate placements and loses with no gate in
wave 3 of 10. Wave 3 jumps from 11 creatures to 46. For the region's first levels the aim is about 85% first-try wins. If
the highlighted socket and the hint do not make the (2,2) gate obvious, a smaller wave 3 is the lever. The bots cannot test the hint. Worth a look on the phone.

**S5. Game designer: Bubble Night and the 2-5 margins.** Bubble Night is winnable but in 10% of the searched builds. The
