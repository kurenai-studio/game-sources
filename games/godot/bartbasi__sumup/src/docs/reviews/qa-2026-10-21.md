The rules are about 1.5 times slower per commit (BigNum score and the fit test) but stay under half a millisecond, and the App's per frame CPU time during drags and animations is the same as main's. Memory is about 0.5 MB higher. One Nova run on this branch had a single 584 ms frame during a drag; it did not repeat in three more Nova runs on this branch, and main had single spikes of 296 ms in its runs too, so I read it as this shared machine, not the build.

Big numbers (headless): the first 2^65600 costs 53 ms, then it is cached; to_decimal and NumberFormat.score of a 19,748 digit score take 1.8 and 1.2 ms; the move check on a near ceiling board with a ceiling score 1.8 ms; a commit at 2^30000 tiles with a 2^65599 score 3.1 ms. Normal play never reaches these paths (the fit test's fast path skips the headroom below 2^65599). Full boards of 40 different huge labels: the first frame that shows them took 60 to 91 ms (labels measured once), later frames 1 to 3 ms. These only arise from hand edited saves.

## 6. Findings

### QA21-01 (Low) AT-170 (a) expects 5,008, but the result depends on the path to the button

- Owner: lead-game-designer (docs/GDD.md, AT-170).
- Steps: Fold5 cover shape, Board P at 5,000. Drag (1,6), (2,6), (3,6), then slide straight to the coffee button below the board and lift there.
- Expected (AT-170 (a)): the chain commits, score 5,008.
- Actual: the slide passes over (3,7) = 8, which joins after the 4 (D-04, a doubling), so the chain 2, 2, 4, 8 commits for 16 points: 5,016. In the gutter shape the slide passes back over (2,6), which backtracks (D-05): 5,004. Both are correct rules behaviour, and no hand off happens in either case, which is what the AT is about.
- Suggestion: say "the chain as built commits as usual (D-06)" instead of a fixed score, or prescribe a path that crosses no tile. A tester on a phone will meet the same thing.

### QA21-02 (Low) The AT-168 tests are weaker than the AT

- Owner: core-logic-dev (tests/core/test_ceiling.gd) and boosters-dev (tests/boosters/test_random_play.gd).
- `test_at_168_the_highlight_never_shows_a_tile_the_commit_would_refuse` makes 15 actions per seed, not 500, makes only drags, and at each step tries one random highlighted tile and one random other tile, where the GDD asks for every one. The booster half makes 500 actions with all five boosters but does not check the highlight. So drags after boosters near the ceiling are never highlight checked.
- My scratch version of AT-168 exactly as written (200 seeds, 500 actions each, drags of up to 15 moves with backtracks and lifts mixed with all five boosters on random targets, every touching neighbor tried on a copy at every step, even seeds starting near 4,095 so blocked boards come up): 3,967,608 neighbor checks, 20,592 lifts, 7,495 accepted boosters, 192 offers, 0 failures, 6 minutes on this machine.
- Suggestion: either bring the test closer to the AT (for example every neighbor, fewer seeds) or have the lead-game-designer accept a sampled version.

### QA21-03 (Low) AT-165 and AT-166 run reduced sizes

- Owner: core-logic-dev (tests/core/test_number_format.gd).
- AT-165: only seeds divisible by 10 draw a length from 1 to 19,748 digits; the other 900 seeds are 1 to 60 digits. AT-166: the score side checks every step to 700, then every 37th.
- My full versions: AT-166 every step 1 to 6,553 on both sides in 4.6 s, AT-165 1,000 full range seeds plus the 120 fixed scores in about 1.4 s (also checking the commas). 0 failures.
- Suggestion: run them at full size; the cost is a few seconds.

### QA21-04 (Low, question) Slate's capitals make the plural run into a capital suffix

- Owner: senior-graphic-designer (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md 7.10 and 14.1); built by ui-motion-artist (src/design/shared/ui_copy.gd).
- Seen: "NO MORE 8QS", "NO MORE 512TS", "NO MORE 32QS TO 128QS" in Slate's clear notice (the tests expect exactly this). The label keeps its case, but the plural "s" is cased to "S" and sits against the capital suffix, so "8QS" reads like one label with a two letter suffix. GDD 14.1 keeps named suffixes to exactly one capital so they can never be misread, and since 2026-10-21 Q and T labels are common in long runs. The same happens with K ("2KS").
- Question: keep it, or leave the plural "s" lowercase after a label in Slate ("NO MORE 8Qs"), or drop it there?

### QA21-05 (Low) The Fold5 inner screen gutter has very little margin, and could not be checked here

- Owner: senior-graphic-designer (DESIGN_SYSTEM.md 7.15); placement code ui-systems-dev (src/ui/game_screen.gd).
- 7.15 measures the Fold5 inner gutter at 445, 445, 421 px against 436, 414, 419 needed: margins of 9 px (Gumdrop), 31 px (Slate, with Segoe UI; Roboto is narrower) and 2 px (Nova), with a 40 dp status bar and a 22 dp gesture bar assumed.
- The column width follows the safe area's height (SafeArea.column_in), and the board is about 0.63 times as wide as tall, so every extra logical px of height takes about 0.3 px off each gutter. My estimate: with the gesture hint hidden (a One UI option, bottom inset near 0), the safe area grows about 64 px, each gutter shrinks about 20 px, and Gumdrop and Nova lose the button on the inner screen; Slate keeps it. With the taskbar shown the gutter grows instead.
- Desktop has no system bars, so at the inner shape (906 x 1088) the gutters are 389, 389 and 364 px and no skin shows the button. The rule itself works: at 1000 x 1000 every skin puts it in the gutter, clear of the column, with all real input checks passing.
- Not a bug in the code: when there is no room the button is simply not shown, and Pause has the row. But Bart may see it come and go between skins on his inner screen. Please check on the Fold5 inner screen in Nova and Gumdrop, with and without the gesture hint.

### QA21-06 (Low) AT-158 has no unit test

- Owner: ui-systems-dev (tests/ui).
- AT-158's Unit bullet (TH18: the run and profile read from the save after each case equal the copies set aside) has no matching test that I could find; AT-171's unit test covers the play screen button and the Pause row only.
- My App check (every skin, a run at Bart's score with a snapshot and 1 free held): the splash button, About over the splash, and About over Settings over Pause each made one hand off, kept the screen as it was through a pause and resume, and left run.json and profile.json byte for byte unchanged. So the behaviour is right; only the test is missing.
