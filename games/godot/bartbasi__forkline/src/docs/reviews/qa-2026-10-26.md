# QA review, 2026-10-26: first pass on the Galaxy Z Fold5

Reviewer: qa-reviewer. Build: debug APK of HEAD 496cd46 (versionName 0.1, targetSdk 36), installed on Bart's Galaxy Z Fold5 (SM-F946U1). The phone was driven over adb with `input tap`, `input swipe` and `input keyevent`, and read with `screencap`, `logcat` and `run-as`. Bart did the two real folds by hand.

Severity scale, as before: blocker (Critical), major (High), minor (Medium), polish (Low).

No blocker, no major and no minor found. The game ran without a script error, an engine error or a crash. Folding mid-wave paused the game with nothing lost. Every Back press went exactly one step. The saved game came back at the exact tick after leaving the app and killing it. Four polish items and one question follow.

## 1. Findings

### Polish

**p1. After a fold mid-wave the game showed the Paused strip, not the Pause menu.** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/battle_screen.gd). This needs one more fold to tell whether it is a bug.

- What happened: Bart folded the phone during wave 7 of 1-1 on the inner screen. The phone went to its lock screen. After he unlocked it, the cover screen showed the HUD's Paused strip ("Paused" and Menu), not the Pause menu. Screenshot `cover\90_after_fold_s.png`.
- Expected (ARCHITECTURE 7.5 step 3 and 3.6 step 4): a resize or a focus loss during a running wave calls `BattleScreen.pause()`, which opens the Pause menu.
- From the code: the strip only appears through `freeze()`, which only the HUD pause button calls (battle_screen.gd:246 and 311 to 314). In landscape that button sits at the top right of the inner screen, about (1686, 160) px, where a hand can touch it while folding. If the clock was already frozen, `apply_layout()` and `leave_app()` skip `pause()` and only save. The most likely cause is an accidental touch, but I could not prove it.
- Nothing was lost either way. The save written at the fold and the save written when I opened the menu afterwards both hold tick 2576, 320 gold, 20 lives and the same creature. The perf log shows no ticks after the fold.
- To recheck: fold mid-wave holding the phone by its edges only, and see whether the Pause menu opens. The Home key mid-wave on the cover does open the Pause menu (section 2).

**p2. A creature popped at the moment of a pause stays drawn whole until play resumes.** Owner: render-engineer (src/render/). After the fold, the map showed two Scamps. The saved and live battle held only one (id 79, health 34 of 40). The second Scamp had full health and a sparkle by its ear: its pop had started and was frozen with the clock. It vanished on Resume (gold went from 320 to 325). Screenshot `cover\90_crop.png`. A player who taps it while paused gets nothing. Suggest letting the pop finish, or hiding a popping creature, while paused.

**p3. A button keeps a lighter look after it was tapped.** Owner: ui-engineer, with the art director for the theme's hover style. After Pause, then Settings, then Back, the Settings row on Pause stays lighter than the other rows. Abandon does the same after its question is cancelled. Screenshots `cover\M15.png` (second panel) and `cover\M16.png` (second panel). This looks like the hover style staying on after a touch. It reads as "selected" on a screen with no selection.

**p4. On the cover screen the bottom dock and sheets reach into the gesture strip.** Owner: ui-engineer. The game runs in immersive mode, so the safe area has no bottom inset. The navigation bar's frame is the bottom 39 px (y 2277 to 2316). The open wave preview's cells end at y 2292, and the Build and Sell buttons at about y 2276. Taps there worked every time, and in immersive mode the first swipe from the edge only shows the bar. The contract (ARCHITECTURE 7.4) measured a 171 logical px bottom inset, which the phone does not report here. No change is needed for play. Suggest keeping a small gap above the gesture strip, so a thumb near the edge does not start a system swipe. Screenshot `cover\55_w7_crop.png`.

### Question for the DM

**q1. A forced stop with the app in front goes back to the last save, not to the kill moment.** No owner until the DM decides. Steps: resume 1-1 mid-wave, play 3 s, `am force-stop` with the game in front, relaunch, Continue. The game came back at tick 252 with 55 gold, the moment it was last paused, not the kill moment (65 gold). Screenshot `cover\M12.png`. This is what ARCHITECTURE section 6 designs: the app saves on pause and focus loss, and a forced stop skips both. A player's own ways out all go through a focus loss, and those came back exact (section 2). Only a crash or an out of memory kill in the foreground can lose time, at most back to the last wave start or pause. Is that fine for 3.10?
