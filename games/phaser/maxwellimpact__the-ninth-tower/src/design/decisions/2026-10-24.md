| 2026-10-24 | **Armour gems built, with a trimmed list of elemental attackers and two bonus gems.** The plan, reworked with the user's direction: not every foe attacks with an element, warlocks cast elemental magic, and storm and sun are bonuses rather than resists. **Elemental attackers** (enemies.json `element`; `Enemy.atkEl`): only foes whose attacks already burn, chill, poison or are plainly that kind of spell. Fire: ember imps, magma slugs (trails), the cinder drake, Vulk. Frost: frost wolves, ice wraiths, hex sentinels (shards), Isolde, the avalanche troll's stomp. Venom: gloom spiders, marsh witchlings, Mother Murk. Shadow: wailing banshees, Malgrim's bolts. Everyone else hits plain, including most foes that resist something. **Hex warlocks cast their region's school** (fire in Cinderreach, frost in Frosthelm, venom in Mistfen, shadow elsewhere): the bolt takes its colour and leaves a short burn, chill, poison or hex-slow (new: before, a bolt was plain damage). **In armour** (head, body, trinkets), share 6/10/15/20% by grade, stacking, capped at 50%: ember, rime, venom and gloam resist their element's blows and shorten its status (burn; chill and freeze; poison; the slow from that element's orbs and bolts); **storm is Static** (melee attackers take the share x 2 of ATK as a jolt, at most every 0.6 s); **sun is Radiance** (heals received + the share). The gems tab says what each gem does in the chosen piece and sums the hero's armour gems, in place of "NO EFFECT IN ARMOUR". **Checks:** `tools/gem-bench.js` (new): `checks()` passes every exact number (Royal pair 0.6x, cap 0.5x, wrong element or plain foe 1x, burn and chill 40% shorter, jolt 80% ATK once per 0.6 s, heal 100 to 140); the fights (3 seeds) show damage per hit falling 25-32% with a Royal pair and 12-15% with a Clear pair against each element and not at all with the wrong gem, burning time 24 to 15 hero-seconds a minute, storm +12% damage out, sun +30% healing. **Standard fight bench** (7 fights x 2 seeds; no elemental foes or gems in it, so the code path is the same), damage taken a second, old run 1 / run 2 → new run 1 / run 2: goblins3 20.5/17.6 → 21.0/23.6, goblins6 32.1/33.9 → 31.6/34.3, mixed10 50.8/45.5 → 52.3/49.6, archers 18.4/15.8 → 17.4/19.0, elitePack 64.8/44.1 → 66.6/52.9, undercroft 24.1/30.3 → 27.4/24.8, tightMixed 28.4/27.2 → 30.6/39.5.
