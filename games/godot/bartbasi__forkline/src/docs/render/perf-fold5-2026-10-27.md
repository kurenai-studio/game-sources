## Headroom for the expansion's sim work

- Against the 2 ms p99 line: none. Whole-wave p99 is already 2.05 to 2.16 ms at 114 live. Mean tick has doubled against V2 at the same level (0.47 to 0.51 ms at 78 live then, 0.93 to 0.95 ms at 113 now), roughly linear in live creatures. Extrapolating, 200 live would be a mean near 1.7 ms and a p99 near 3.5 to 4 ms on the phone. The balance bots peak near 116, so the real game stays near today's numbers, but the contract's 200 is not within the 2 ms line on this phone.
- Against the frame: the sim runs 20 ticks a second times the speed, so 3x is 60 ticks a second. That is 0.5 tick per frame at 120 Hz and 1 tick per frame at 60 Hz. (The V2 report said 1.5 ticks per frame. That was wrong.) At 60 Hz one tick of 2.2 ms p99 plus about 2 ms of scripts is about 4 ms of a 16.7 ms frame. At 120 Hz the cost is spread: 8.3 ms frames held at 106 live. So the added expansion cost can grow the tick by roughly a factor of 2 before the frame at 60 Hz is at risk, and less if the display stays at 120 Hz (frames above 8.3 ms drop to 60 fps for that frame, which is still inside the 20 ms budget).
- Suggested guard: the DM or sim engineer repeats this exact run after the expansion's sim work lands. If the whole-wave p99 here passes about 3 ms, the 120 Hz frames start to slip.

## Worst frame and the wave-start save

All five runs have one bad frame (33 to 53 ms) in the first 2 second window, when only 12 creatures are live. Nothing else in the wave was above 8.3 ms except one 14.7 ms frame in run 2.

Runs 3 to 5 timed the saves. Wave-start save: **2.4 ms** (run 3), 2.8 ms (run 4), 1.8 ms (run 5). Call-early save: 5.6, 10.2 and 5.9 ms. Resume: 2.5 to 2.7 ms. The 33 to 39 ms frames came in the window right after a wave-start save of under 3 ms, and the call-early saves (up to 10.2 ms, about 75 live) left no frame above 8.3 ms. So the save write does **not** explain the worst frame. The likelier cause is first-time work as the creatures first appear (new looks drawn into the atlas, pools filling, first particles), which is render side and in my area. I did not prove it. Possible fix to try in a later task: draw the creature looks of the coming waves into the atlas when the level loads.
