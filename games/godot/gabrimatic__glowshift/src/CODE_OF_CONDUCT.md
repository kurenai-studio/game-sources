# Code of Conduct

Glowshift follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, give constructive feedback, and leave out harassment, personal attacks, and discriminatory language. This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer, [@gabrimatic](https://github.com/gabrimatic). For content on GitHub, you can also use GitHub's [report abuse](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Reports are handled privately.

The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and block participants who break these rules.
