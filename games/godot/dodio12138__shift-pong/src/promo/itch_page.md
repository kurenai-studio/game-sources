## SHIFT PONG

Real table tennis — topspin, backspin, sidespin, strokes you time and power yourself — and then the rules start shifting.

**Hold SHIFT** to open the skill wheel and bend a rally your way:

- **Lane Shift** — past the net, the ball blinks onto another line.
- **Spin Shift** — the moment it lands on their side, topspin turns into backspin.
- **Gravity Shift** — their balls get heavy and drop into the net.
- **View Shift** — scramble their view of the ball.
- …and eight more: decoy balls, an invisible ball, a giant racket, a net that shoots up.

Take **four** into each match. Every fourth point, the **rules shift** for both players: giant rackets, spoons, a narrowed table, heavy balls.

### Modes
- **SHIFT RUN** — six opponents in a row, a legend at the end. Win to draft a new skill; two hearts.
- **Quick start** — one game to 7, straight in.
- **Cups** — singles and team knockouts, plus four legendary bosses.
- **Training ground** — a six-lesson tutorial, drills, free play, endless skill duels.

### Controls (keyboard + mouse — not for phones)
| | |
|---|---|
| Mouse | move |
| Q W E / A S D | strokes: top row loops and smashes, bottom row pushes; left / right column for sidespin; hold longer for more power |
| Space | serve (hold to pick the spin, release in the bright zone) |
| **Shift** | skill wheel (release to cast) · 1–4 cast directly |
| T | focus: slow time |
| C | camera · B replay · V challenge a call · Y timeout |
