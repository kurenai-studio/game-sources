# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Discuss the work, not the person doing it. Assume the other party
is acting in good faith until you have real reason not to.

## Not welcome here

Harassment of any kind, including unwelcome personal attention; insults and demeaning
remarks, whether or not they are framed as jokes; slurs or sustained hostility directed
at anyone's identity; publishing someone's private information; and deliberately
derailing a discussion to waste other people's time.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, in pull requests and in commit messages — anywhere
the project is being worked on.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer, [@horakdani](https://github.com/horakdani) — through
GitHub's own private reporting on this repository, or by any contact method listed on
that profile. They are read by the maintainer and kept confidential; the reporter is
never identified without being asked first.

Responses range from a private word to editing or deleting a comment to blocking an
account from the repository, depending on what happened and whether it continued after
being pointed out. The maintainer decides, and will say what was decided to the person
who reported it.

## Credit

The commitments above are the ordinary ones. If you want the longer and more carefully
argued version of this document, read the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) —
nothing here is meant to be weaker than it.
