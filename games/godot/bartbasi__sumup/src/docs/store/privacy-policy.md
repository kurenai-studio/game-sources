# SumUp Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

SumUp is a number puzzle game for Android made by Play, Inc. This policy explains what the game does with your information. The short version: nothing. SumUp collects no data at all.

## What SumUp collects

Nothing. SumUp has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no leaderboards. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, and it does not know who you are.

## What stays on your phone

SumUp saves a few things so you can pick up where you left off:

- your current game (the board, your score and the last move, so Undo works)
- your best score and best tile
- your settings (sound on or off, and which look you chose)

These are kept only in the app's own private storage on your phone. They never leave your device and Play, Inc. cannot see them. Uninstalling SumUp deletes them. You can also clear them at any time in your phone's settings, under Apps, SumUp, Storage, Clear data.

## Internet and permissions

SumUp works fully offline. The game never connects to the internet, and it asks for no Android permissions.

## The Buy me a coffee button

SumUp has an optional Buy me a coffee button on its start screen, on its play screen, in its Pause menu and in its About screen. Tapping it opens a web page at buymeacoffee.com in your phone's web browser. The game itself sends nothing when you tap it. If you visit that page or choose to support us there, Buy Me a Coffee's own privacy policy applies to that visit, not this one.

## Google Play

If you install SumUp from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your device under Google's own privacy policy. Play, Inc. does not receive any personal information from this.

## Children

SumUp collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If SumUp ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say so before that version of the game is released.
