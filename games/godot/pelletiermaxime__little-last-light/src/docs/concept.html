Little Last Light · working concept

A little lantern.
A growing defense.

Place your turrets. Dodge with your lantern. Find the sweet spot where your defenses cover the enemies chasing you.

One arena, automatic shooting, and a few satisfying choices.

01 / The run

Start small. Build up. Survive.

1

Defend

Start with your lantern and one turret. Enemies approach; your turret fires automatically.

2

Earn

Brightness generates energy and attracts more enemies. Pressure rises with survival time; building stays locked.

3

Improve & retry

After defeat, spend your earnings on turrets and rearrange your layout for free. Start a fresh run when ready.

Early runs should be short and earn a useful improvement. Energy and turret layouts persist between runs and are saved locally.

02 / Your playstyle

Move to dodge. Settle to defend.

Turrets stay where you place them. Enemies chase the lantern, so dodge while drawing them through overlapping firing zones.

Find the sweet spot

Dodge without leading enemies out of your turrets’ reach. Overlapping ranges let several turrets fire at the same threat; your position shapes the enemies’ approach.

Chosen direction: fixed turrets, a mobile lantern. Range circles show where turrets can hit enemies—not a boundary the lantern must stay inside.

03 / The interesting choice

Another turret—or a stronger build?

Spend banked energy during preparation. Place a new turret to cover a gap or overlap firepower. Turret upgrades are a later addition.

A nimble build

Faster movement, longer turret range, piercing shots. Give yourself room to dodge while drawing enemies through your firing zones.

An idle-friendly build

More lantern health and armor while settled. Combine these with overlapping turret coverage to hold a defended spot with less input.

Standing still would activate a defensive bonus after a short delay. Moving removes it. Idle means low input during a run; surviving still depends on the build.

04 / Build the smallest version

Prove this loop before adding more.

  1. Move and dodge while fixed turrets defend you.
  2. Earn energy as enemy pressure grows.
  3. Bank earnings on defeat and clear the arena.
  4. Buy turrets or reposition your existing layout.
  5. Start a fresh run with your improvements.
05 / The destination

Reach the final boss at 15:00.

The long-term goal is to survive fifteen minutes, then defeat a final boss to win the run. Early attempts can be short; improving your defense should help you reach farther on later runs.

Keep dodging fair

The lantern stays much faster than enemies. Basic pursuers take time to turn, so a sharp dodge creates an opening. Danger comes from routes being crowded and attacks you must read.

Build difficulty through variety

Future enemies use different movement patterns and combinations. Tougher bosses test your defense and movement instead of ordinary enemies eventually outrunning you.

The fifteen-minute boss, encounter schedule, and victory flow are future work. M6 establishes the basic slower pursuer first.

What could grow later?

Turret upgrades, armor bonuses for a stationary lantern, turrets with different jobs, and enemies that challenge different builds.

Prices and difficulty are still being tuned. Existing time-based spawn and health increases are temporary while we build the enemy roster.

M4 implements saved runs and preparation; M5 improves the HUD and recap; M6 introduces slower enemies with gradual turning. Stationary bonuses and the final boss remain future design ideas.
