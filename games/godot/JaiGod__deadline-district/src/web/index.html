DISTRICT

The whole city pulled an all-nighter, and now it wants your brains. Hold the line with a flashlight, ten upgradable weapons, barricades and turrets in this original top-down zombie wave-survival shooter made in Godot 4.

▶ Play in browser
Free · no install · keyboard + mouse or twin-stick touch · best waves save automatically

Screenshots

Features

10 weapons

Pistol, SMG, shotgun, rifle, minigun, flamethrower, crossbow, rocket launcher, tesla gun and katana. Each has ammo, reloads and 3 upgrade levels.

Weapon bench

Between waves, walk to the bench to buy guns, upgrades, ammo, medkits, repairs and perks.

Build defenses

Drop barricades to funnel the horde and auto-turrets to cover your back. Zombies chew through both.

8 zombies + 3 bosses

Runners, brutes, spitters, exploders, screamers that buff the horde, shielded riot guards… and a boss every 5 waves.

Flashlight lighting

A custom Compatibility-safe shader casts your flashlight cone, muzzle flashes, fire and explosions into the dark.

3 maps

Cubicle Plaza, Rush Hour Overpass and Midnight Mall, from Normal to Nightmare.
