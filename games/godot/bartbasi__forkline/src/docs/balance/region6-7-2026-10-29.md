# Balance report: regions 6 and 7, tiers 2 and 3 (E6.2 and E7.2)

Date: 2026-10-29. For the game designer, the level designer, the roguelite engineer and the DM. Written by the balance analyst. It answers ARCHITECTURE's E6.2 and E7.2 rows and follows the shape and the bars of the region 5 report (region5-2026-10-28.md).

**What I measured on.** A clean `git archive` of **3fb2285** in my own scratch folder, with the E5.3 patch (`e53.patch`) applied. The patch changes `data/stats/towers.json` (the Bramble Cottage: damage 10, 14, 20 and 24, rings 2.0, 2.5, 3.0 and 2.5) and some docs that no bot reads. I read no data from the main working tree, so none of the region 5 level edits, the E5.4 run data edits (tunnel segments, Mossbud pack) or region 8 are in it. That matters for the runs: **tiers 2 and 3 carry tier 1's content as E5.1 built it**, not as E5.4 fixes it. The bots are the working tree's `tools/balance/`: B6's bots, E5.2's additions and the additions listed near the end, every one off by default (a default run equals the old one: 800 of 800 rows equal, section 7).

Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67/` (git-ignored), one JSON file per job. Tables come from `summary.gd pass=1` and `runs_summary.gd`; some small tables were added up from the raw files and say so. Seeds are in each table. The campaign pass used `SEED_SHIFT=100000`, as E5.2 did. How to repeat: the last section.

**Not done, on purpose.** Regions 1 to 5 were not played again (E5.5 does region 5; the bots' defaults did not change). The pass skipped its timing run (`SKIP_BENCH=1`); the timings below are from the crowded wave jobs. Tier 1 runs were played only on seeds 1 to 100, as the baseline for sections 6 and 7, not as a full 200 seed report. Forced stacks and packs were played only where section 6 says so.

Reads:

- **The Scarecrow card costs the bots 12.7 points at tier 2, as the Bramble card cost 23 at tier 1.** A Scarecrow deals no damage, and a bot builds the first tower of every type it has as soon as it can afford it, on a spot ranked third or lower by road seen. A bot that builds it after 9 waves recovers about a third (+4.9). A stronger Scarecrow does not (+1.9, inside the noise), so this is not about the tower's numbers. It is about what a bot spends on it.
- **The Snow Fort card costs 4.2 points at tier 3** (23.6% without it against 19.4%). Its damage times 1.5 gets 26.5% on seeds 1 to 50, close to the card-out reading of 28.0%. Like the Catapult, the Longbow Loft and the cottage, it cannot hit flyers.
- **The bots do under-build anti-air.** The share of their attackers that can hit flyers (Sling Post, Firework Tower) is 0.566 at tier 0, 0.455 at tier 1, 0.477 at tier 2, 0.407 at tier 3. In the runs they lose, flyers take 38.8% of the lost lives at tier 0, 47.8% at tier 1, 41.8% at tier 2 and 49.8% at tier 3. A real but small part, and only at tier 3 clearly.
- **The win rate by anti-air share does not rise with the share** (tier 0: 57% at 0.55 to 0.65, 23% at 0.65 and over). Runs with a very high share are runs with few attackers of any kind, so that table shows the strategy, not the share. The floor experiment above is the test.
- **The proposed change to my bots** (not the game, and not yet made a default): at tier 2 and above, build a Scarecrow only after 9 waves (like the Bakery) and keep anti-air at 55% of the attackers. Together on tier 3, seeds 1 to 50: 28.4% against 21.6%. That is about half of the gap to the bar's floor (35%); it is not the whole of it.

## Limits of these numbers

- Searches find or do not find. A lone search can sit on tier 3 builds for thousands of builds (6-4 seed 836401) or win nothing for 2500 and then win (7-4 seed 867401). Both seeds of a verdict must run to the end.
- The tier 1 reading (36.1%) is seeds 1 to 100 only. The Scarecrow and Snow Fort experiments are 1600 plays (SE 1.1) or 800 (SE 1.5); differences under about 3 points are noise.
- The bots are a floor and not tuned for the new towers; section 7 is about that.
- Tiers 2 and 3 are on E5.1's tier 1 data. The E5.4 changes are not measured.
- Peaks are the most crowded play found among random strategies, not a proof. Timings come from a PC loaded with 10 to 17 other Godot jobs; the Fold5 decides.

## How to repeat it

From a clean `git archive` of 3fb2285 with `git apply e53.patch` (the E5.3 patch) and the working tree's `tools/balance/` copied over it, then `godot --headless --path . --import`:

```
SEED_SHIFT=100000 bash tools/balance/run_pass.sh 2026-10-29-e67 4 r6_l1 r6_l2 r6_l3 r6_l4 r6_l5 r6_l6 r7_l1 r7_l2 r7_l3 r7_l4 r7_l5 r7_l6
SKIP_BENCH=1 SEED_SHIFT=100000 bash tools/balance/run_pass_post.sh 2026-10-29-e67 4 r6_l1 ... r7_l6
TIER=2 OUT=docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67/runs_t2 bash tools/balance/run_b4.sh A 2
TIER=3 OUT=docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67/runs_t3 bash tools/balance/run_b4.sh A 2
for P in WI WI2 NB AIR T0 NC SC WT PB PW FP ST PK; do OUT=docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67 bash tools/balance/run_e67.sh $P 3; done
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/summary.gd -- dir=res://docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67 pass=1
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/runs_summary.gd -- dir=res://docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67/runs_t2 tier=2 part=b4
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/runs_summary.gd -- dir=res://docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67 part=air
```

The lone Sling Post second seeds of 7-4 and the "every run can be won" random searches are the lines `run_pass_post.sh` and `runs_summary.gd part=flags` print. The small what-if tables (sections 3 to 5, 7, 8) add up the `wins`, `three_star_found`, `outcomes` and `rows` fields of the files named; the numbers are the files'. The work took about 9.5 hours in all, with up to 14 of my processes at a time beside other agents' jobs; a job whose output file exists is skipped.
