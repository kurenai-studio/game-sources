HOW TO PLAY

01 LAUNCH

Hold SPACE — or press and drag the LAUNCH pad — to compress the railgun spring. Release to fire the ball up the shooter lane.

02 FLIP

Use Z / ← and / / →. On touch screens, use the two large flip pads. A moving flipper transfers far more energy than a parked one.

03 OVERCHARGE

Each plasma core hit scores and flashes. Knock down all four Cyber Security nodes to raise the multiplier. Send the ball through a Hyper-Loop for a big bonus.

04 SURVIVE

Three balls per session. If a ball ever wedges in a corner, press R or use RECOVER STUCK BALL to return it to the railgun. Save your score archive from the right-hand panel and load it on any device. M toggles the synthesizer.
