Forkline: Tower Defense

Forkline Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Forkline is a tower defense game for Android made by Play, Inc. This policy explains what Forkline does with your information. The short version: nothing. Forkline collects no data at all.

What Forkline collects

Nothing. Forkline has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not collect, store, share or sell any personal information, and it does not know who you are.

What stays on your phone

Forkline saves a few things so you can pick up where you left off:

  • Your settings: whether sound effects are on and whether music is on.
  • Your progress: the stars you have earned on each campaign level, which Twists you have cleared, which towers, creatures and levels you have already met, and a few counters that unlock cards in roguelite runs.
  • The daily challenge: your best result for the day and your streak. Forkline reads your phone's date to pick the day's challenge and to count the streak.
  • Games in progress: one campaign level, one roguelite run and one daily run, each saved at the exact moment you left it.

These are kept only in the app's own private storage on your phone. Forkline never sends them anywhere, and Play, Inc. cannot see them. Forkline turns off Android's backup for them. Some phone makers' tools for moving to a new phone may still copy app files. That is done by your phone, not by Forkline. Uninstalling Forkline deletes them. You can also clear them at any time in your phone's settings, under Apps, Forkline, Storage, Clear data.

Internet and permissions

Forkline works fully offline. It never connects to the internet, and it asks for no Android permissions.

Three buttons hand a fixed address to another app on your phone. Forkline itself sends nothing.

  • Buy me a coffee (at the bottom of the front door, in About, and in the Pause menu) opens buymeacoffee.com in your phone's browser. Anything you do there is between you and Buy Me a Coffee, and their privacy policy applies.
  • Source code (in About) opens the source code page, github.com/bartbasi/forkline, in your browser. Anything you do there is between you and GitHub, and their privacy policy applies.
  • Send feedback (in About) opens your own email app with a new message to bartbasi@gmail.com. The draft names the app's version, your phone's model and its Android version, so problems can be looked into. You see all of it before you send, you can change or delete any of it, and nothing is sent unless you press send in your email app.

Google Play

If you install Forkline from Google Play, Google may collect information about the download and your device under Google's own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). Play, Inc. does not receive any personal information from this.

Children

Forkline collects no personal information from anyone, including children. Its store listing is for ages 13 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date. If Forkline ever starts collecting any data, this policy will say so before that version is released.
