# Balance report: the region 3 recheck after the new third stars (B4b)

Date: 2026-10-26. For the game designer (G4, G5) and the level designer. Written by the balance analyst. Answers docs/design/TUNING_CAMPAIGN.md 2.2, items 1 to 4.

Code and data: commit 53d6ad7 (HEAD when the pass started; it holds L7's new third stars and the new 3-6 waves, commit c6aa5e5). Played from a clean `git archive` of that commit. The bots and scripts are the working tree's `tools/balance/` (not committed), copied over the archive. Since b1137be (B3's commit) `git diff b1137be HEAD -- data/levels data/stats` touches seven level files and no stat file: the new `star3` of 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 and 3-6, the 3-6 Pip counts, and three hints. So B3's lone searches on 3-1 to 3-3 play the same levels and stand, as 2.2 item 2 says.
Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-26-b4b/` (git-ignored), one JSON file per job, with `jobs.txt`, `post_jobs.txt` and `extra/jobs.txt`. Every table below comes from `tools/balance/summary.gd` with `pass=1` run on that folder (`summary.md` there), plus `mode=need` on all 24 levels. Seeds are in each table. The pass used `SEED_SHIFT=200000` on the seed bases of `run_pass.sh`.

## Findings

**3-3 does find three-star builds.** Three searches of 6000 builds, any tower, with the new rule "Have at most 5 towers": 565, 1429 and 1497 three-star builds, the first at build 4722, 2140 and 2239. The fallback ("No tower above tier 2.") is not needed. But the first seed shows how thin the hard part is: its first three-star build came at build 4722 of 6000, so a 3000 build search with that seed would have found none. The seed 974300 is G4's, and it gives G4's numbers exactly (1429, first at 2140, 915 with 20 lives). Use at least 6000 builds for 3-3.

1. **Level 3-6 on its new file passes every check.**
   - The plain recipe on Relaxed wins with no gates (12 lives) and with its best placement (40 lives). Before: lost in wave 9 with no gates.
   - Normal: best placement 14 lives (18 of 27 placements win), as G4 measured.
   - A search without the Sling Post finds 2152 three-star builds in 6000 (first at build 2043), 1459 of them with 20 lives.
   - Random play gets three stars on 0 of 750 plays.
   - Length 8.0 minutes. Largest wave 75, most creatures at once 74 in a search play.
   - Its Twist is won (463 of 1500).
2. **The four new third stars hold against random play:** three stars on 0 of 750 plays on each of 3-1, 3-2, 3-3 and 3-6 (bar: under 10%). Normal wins by random play: 1%, 2%, 0% and 0%.
3. **All three stars is reachable on all four:** 3-1 (no Sling Post) 2172 three-star builds in 6000, 3-2 (tier 2 cap) 2256, 3-3 as above, 3-6 2152. The lone Sling Post can never earn star 3 on 3-1 and 3-6 (the ban), and on 3-2 and 3-3 B3's two 6000 build checks found no lone Sling Post three-star build under these rules (G4 3.1). Nothing in the levels changed since, so I did not run them again.
4. **The tools follow RULES 12.6 as revised.**
   - `mode=need` now skips the tower a level's star 3 bans and gives the towers of the "All three stars" search.
   - `run_pass.sh` uses them and takes `SKIP_SOLO=1`.
   - `run_pass_post.sh` adds the two 6000 build searches for a Twist whose 1500 build search wins nothing. No Twist needed them this time: all four won, 3-2's with 246 of 1500.
   - On 2-5, 3-1, 3-4, 3-6 and 4-2 the banned tower no longer gets a lone search, and their any-tower search leaves it out.
5. **Same seed, same play:** all 8 determinism jobs (the four levels, plain and Twist) repeat, replay through a save and load in the middle of a wave, and the four plain jobs match when run again in a new process.

Two things I would watch:
- **3-3's plain recipe wins on 1 of 18 placements** (the three gates, 1,3 with 5,5 and 1,7, 16 lives). Unchanged from B3, and the bar is a win, so it passes, but a first try with other gates loses in wave 3 or 4.
- **3-3's search difficulty varies a lot by seed** (first three-star build at 2140 to 4722 of 6000, 565 to 1497 found). A 2500 or 3000 build screen can say "none" on this level. It is findable, not a lottery: 1429 to 1497 builds in two of three seeds, and 61% to 65% of them keep all 20 lives.

## Suggestions

These are suggestions. I do not change rules or levels.

1. **3-3:** keep "Have at most 5 towers." It has 565 to 1497 three-star builds in 6000, in all three seeds. Do not use the fallback. If the third star ever feels like a hunt on the phone, that is the search's difficulty, not a missing build.
2. **For the next campaign pass:** give 3-3's any-tower search 6000 builds (`EVALS=6000`, one level, about 40 minutes). With the default 3000 it can say "none" on a seed that has 565 builds.
3. **Nothing else to change in region 3.** The four new third stars, and 3-6's new Pips, pass what the plan asked.

## Limits of these numbers

- Search results depend on the seed. The three 3-3 searches show the range (565 to 1497 builds, first at 2140 to 4722). A search that finds a build proves one exists. A search that finds none proves nothing.
- The lone searches of 3-2 and 3-3 and the full region 3 pass of B3 stand from `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-25-b3/` because the levels they played did not change (the diff above). I did not play them again, as 2.2 item 5 says.
- The plain recipe, wave sizes and lengths do not depend on star 3, so they equal B3's on 3-1, 3-2 and 3-3.

## How to repeat it

From a clean `git archive` of the commit, with the working tree's `tools/balance/` copied over it, then `godot --headless --path . --import`:

```
SKIP_SOLO=1 EVALS=6000 SEED_SHIFT=200000 bash tools/balance/run_pass.sh 2026-10-26-b4b 3 r3_l1 r3_l2 r3_l3 r3_l6
SEED_SHIFT=200000 EVALS=6000 bash tools/balance/run_pass_post.sh 2026-10-26-b4b 3 r3_l1 r3_l2 r3_l3 r3_l6
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/run.gd -- mode=search level=r3_l3 seed=974300 evals=6000 only=any out=PATH
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/summary.gd -- dir=res://docs/balance/raw/2026-10-26-b4b pass=1
```

A job whose output file exists is skipped. The pass took about 1.5 hours with 3 processes, the two extra 3-3 searches 40 minutes more.

## Notes on this run

- At most 3 Godot processes of mine ran at once. Nothing was ended by name.
- Tool changes (all in `tools/balance/`): `run.gd` (`mode=need` bans and `all_three_only`), `run_pass.sh` (`only=` from the need file, `SKIP_SOLO`), `run_pass_post.sh` (Twist long searches), `summary.gd` (reads those). The options the game designer and level designer use (`pool=`, `stack=`, `stop=` in `runs.gd`) were not touched.
