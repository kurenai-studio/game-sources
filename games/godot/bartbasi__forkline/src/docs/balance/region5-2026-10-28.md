Date: 2026-10-28 (the jobs ran from 2026-10-28 to 2026-10-29). For the game designer (E5.3), the level designer (E5.4), the roguelite engineer and the DM. Written by the balance analyst. It answers ARCHITECTURE's E5.2 row.

Code and data: a clean `git archive` of **e6beedc** (E5.1, region 5). HEAD has moved on to ed0ff35 (region 6 committed, region 7 in the working tree). I did not measure either, so this report has **no region 6 or 7 numbers**. The bots are the working tree's `tools/balance/` (B6's bots plus the additions listed at the end, none of which changes a result unless its option is set).
Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-28-e52/` and `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-28-e52-b4b/` (git-ignored), one JSON file per job. Seeds are in each table. The campaign pass used `SEED_SHIFT=100000`, the seeds of the 2026-10-25 pass (B3), so every old level can be compared file by file. Tables come from `summary.gd pass=1` and `runs_summary.gd`. How to repeat: the last section.

**2. The cause of the tier 1 misses is the Bramble Cottage card, as the bots play it.** Take the card and its perk out of the pool and the 16 strategies win **47.0%** (bar 35 to 55), the best 69% (bar 55 to 75), and nine of ten boss packs sit in the bar. Put them in and the same 3200 plays win 23.8%. The tunnels cost a further 10 to 17 points on top. Buffs to the tower's damage alone do not fix it; damage together with reach does (section 5).

**3. Suggested changes**, in order of weight, are in section 7.

## 5. Why tier 1 misses, and what the Bramble Cottage needs

The bots take a Bramble Cottage card in 90% of plays and then build 2.8 cottages (a quarter of their 11.9 towers), because B6 gave it a bot weight of 1.0. That weight is my guess (B6 said so). To see how much the tower is worth, I played tier 1 again with one thing changed, seeds 1 to 50 (800 plays, SE about 3 points) unless marked:

| What changed | The 16 together, Normal | Best strategy | Stacks with no tunnel | Stacks with a tunnel |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Nothing (default), seeds 1 to 200 | 23.8% (SE 1.3) | 37% | 29.6% | 11.9% |

Reading them:
- A Bramble Cottage of today's numbers costs the bots about 23 points of win rate. ## 7. For the game designer: what to tune, and why

In order of weight. These are suggestions; I change no rule and no level.

1. **Bramble Cottage (tier 1 fails on it).** At today's numbers it costs the bots 23 points of win rate, its card is -7.2 points when taken, and no tier 1 bar for Normal, boss packs or maps can be met with it in the pool (section 5). Options: (a) damage x2 and reach 250: damage 8, 14, 20 (Wild Bramble), 24 (Holly Crown), reach 250 (Wild Bramble 300), which reads 38.0% for the 16 together on all 200 seeds, with both Burrowbelly packs in the bar (52.7% and 73.9%); x3 damage with reach 250 reads 43.0%; (b) accept the tower as weak and read the tier 1 bars with the bots limited to one cottage (34.4%). If you choose (a), expect 5-4 and 5-5 to get much easier (plain recipe wins 28 of 35 and 29 of 29 placements, from 6 and 3), and 5-2 and 5-3 not to move. Then I need to play the lone searches of 5-2 to 5-6 and the Bramble's tier 1 pass again (a stronger Bramble might become a super tower for walkers; flyers still cost 34 to 60 lives, so it cannot win alone).
2. **Sling Post on 5-1, 5-3, 5-4, 5-6.** Rules that pass both the three-star and the lone Sling Post checks, without clashing with the level's Twist: 5-1 "No tower above tier 2." (796 builds, lone 0 in 2 x 6000) or "Have at most 4 towers." (696, lone 0 in 2 x 6000); 5-3 "Build no Sling Posts." (1059; the Bell ban goes) or "Have at most 4 towers." (741 builds, lone Sling Post 0 and 0 in 2 x 6000; "at most 5" fails with 661 and 768); its Twist is the Tier 2 Cap, so a tier cap is out; 5-6 "Build no Sling Posts." (756) or "Have at most 6 towers." (340 builds, first at 1563; lone Sling Post 0 in 2 x 6000); 5-4: "Have at most 5 towers." fails (lone Sling Post 633 and 325 in 2 x 6000), a tier 2 cap finds no three star, and its Twist is "No Sling Posts", so a ban repeats the Twist: "Have at most 4 towers." finds no three star at all (0 in 3000). No tower-count or tier rule works on 5-4. What is left is the ban, which repeats the Twist: either the Twist changes (to a rule that is not a Sling Post ban) or 5-4 keeps its current third star and the Sling Post stays a super tower there. This one is the game designer's call. Details in section 2.
3. **5-3 is soft.** Plain recipe keeps 20 lives against 80% more creatures with the best gate; the lone Sling Post earns three stars in 38% of builds. If the Sling Post ban (item 2) is taken, the third star is no longer easy by itself; the wave counts could rise by 30% or more (largest wave 72): the plain recipe's best gate still keeps 20 lives, though the no-gates play already loses at +10%. The level is 9.0 minutes at 1x, the longest of the region; do not add waves.
4. **b3_mossy_ruts, wave 1 of the pack** (the first Mossbud wave of a run). 22.6% of runs that reach it are lost inside it (bar 10%). Pips 18 to 12 and Scamps 12 to 8 in that wave: 6.3%. Mossbud health or regrow changes help much less (16.4% for both).
5. **mid_hedge and one tunnel stack.** mid_hedge is 0.70 times all runs without the Bramble (0.60 with it) and `top_fork, mid_hedge, bot_porch` loses 11.6% by wave 6. `top_pair, mid_tunnel, bot_yard` loses 18.7% by wave 6 on all 200 seeds, the one wall found among the tunnel stacks. The level designer should look at both. mid_tunnel and mid_burrow as segments pass without the Bramble (0.78 each).
6. **`b6_burrow_mist` is too easy when the Bramble is out** (82.1%, bar 75); `b6_burrow_deep` is in the bar (65.9%). With the Bramble in, deep is 42.3% and mist 65.6%. Retune after item 1, not before.
7. **5-5 three stars are a hunt** (first three-star build at 2531 to 2988 of 6000 in three searches; 1 build in 3000 on the main seed). It is findable (1443 and 1498 in 6000). A player will find it by design, not by chance. No change asked; the third star "at most 3 kinds" works because a lone Sling Post cannot win 5-5. If item 1 makes the level easier the lone Sling Post may start to win it: then it needs the lone searches again.
8. **5-5 and 5-6 Twists are thin.** 5-5 Five Lives: 80 of 1500 builds win, best 5 lives, plain recipe 0 of 29. 5-6 Double Trouble: 0 of 1500, 0 of 6000, then 2054 of 6000. Winnable but narrow, like 3-2 before.
9. **5-6 on Relaxed with no gates wins with 3 lives of 40.** Placing the gates matters; a hint at the gate would help.
