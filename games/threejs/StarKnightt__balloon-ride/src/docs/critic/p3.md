## A. World blockers from r2: PASS
- **N1 (ridge disc smear): fixed.** In crop `500m` (0–800, 0–200) and crop `200m` (0–800, 0–250), every ridge is a clean opaque silhouette at one haze value. There are no rings, no see-through discs and no stair-stepped blobs.
- **The fog strip** is now a soft melt into the terrain (`200m` y≈165, `lake` y≈185). That is acceptable.
- **N2 (ink through cloud): fixed.** No mountain outline crosses a cloud in any frame. The `500m` top-left, which was the failing spot in r2, is clean.
- **Residual:** far cumulus still end on a ruler-flat line exactly at the ridge tops. This is logged under C1, because it is part of how the clouds are built.

## B. Balloon model: PASS
What it gets right:
- **Silhouette.** `50m` and `ground` show a real inverted pear: the widest point is about a third of the way down, and the envelope tapers to the mouth. It is not a beach ball.
- **Gores.** The gores bulge between the darker load tapes, and the stripes run with them.
- **Mouth and skirt.** The mouth band, skirt, flying wires and basket all read in crop `50m` (620–980, 250–700).
- **Burner.** In `burner`, the twin cans, the frame, the suede uprights and the skirt with its glowing interior are convincing.
- **Scale.** In `500m` (x≈930, y≈605) a balloon over the village is about twice a house's width, which is right.

Nice-to-haves:
- **B-n1. The envelope reads glossy.** In `50m` (x≈900–960) the shading is a smooth gradient with a specular sheen on the teal. The world is flat-banded toon. Use a harder two-to-three band ramp so the envelope sits in the same paint. The outline is also thinner than the world's ink.
- **B-n2. The gold band at the equator.** It sits on the widest diameter, which pushes the read toward "striped ball" at mid distance (`500m`, `cloud`). Drop it 1–2 m below the equator, or narrow it.
- **B-n3. The teal reads sage** (`50m`, `200m`), as PROGRESS already notes.
- **B-n4. The basket is a flat tan box** in `50m`, `200m` and `cloud`. No weave or rim colour reads at chase distance. A darker rim band would help.

## C. Clouds and wind: FAIL
**C1. BLOCKER: the far cumulus are translucent pillars with ruled edges.**
- Where it shows: `500m_sunset` crop (250–750, 0–150); `500m` (x≈325–640, y≈0–90); `200m` (x≈280–600, y≈0–60); `lake` (x≈270–480, y≈0–60).
- The big mid-layer clouds have straight vertical sides and cylinder-like smooth shading.
- Their bottoms are cut dead flat along the ridge top (in `500m_sunset` at y≈88).
- The smaller cloud above them is cut flat by a second hard horizontal edge (`500m_sunset` x≈420–520, y≈27).
- They read as pale cards or columns, not heaped cumulus.
- Fix: give them the lumpy, lit-top tower read of the Summer Cycle ref. Either keep clouds beyond the ridge ring from being clipped by it, or keep their bases visibly above it.

**C2. BLOCKER: near clouds are faceted cotton balls or flat cut-outs.**
- In `cloud` (crop 600–1100, 420–720), the puff under the balloon is three or four near-uniform lilac-white spheres. Their silhouettes have polygon steps, and there is no interior form beyond one soft terminator.
- In `ground` and `ground_sunset` (x≈635–940, y≈335–560), the cloud behind the balloon is a flat pink shape with a jagged, stepped edge and no shading. It looks like paper pasted on the sky.
- Painted clouds need many small cauliflower bumps on the lit side, a crisp light/shade split, and a cooler, darker base. Right now there are 3–4 large lumps per visible face.
- The icosphere facets PROGRESS mentions are plainly visible at this distance. A near LOD is required, not optional.

**C3. BLOCKER: the rise-through frames don't read as being in a cloud.**
- `incloud` is a uniform sepia-beige veil, and `incloud_sunset` a uniform mauve one.
- Hard-edged geometry ghosts show through: a diagonal band at `incloud` x≈1200–1440, a vertical edge at x≈800, y≈380–480, and the envelope curve at `incloud_sunset` x≈1100–1300.
- The land and mountains stay legible through the veil.
- It reads as a dirty lens or a fog bug. There are no wisps, density variation or bright/dark puffs streaming past.
- The beige tint is also wrong: inside a cloud it should be white-grey with a warm side toward the sun.

What works:
- **Layering.** In `500m`, low puffs float below the camera over the patchwork (x≈580, y≈295; x≈1075, y≈265), with mid cumulus and cirrus above (the streaks in `ground`).
- **Cloud shadows.** These are good: the long soft sweeps in `500m` (x≈0–500, y≈560–760) and the blotches in `500m_sunset`.

Nice-to-haves:
- **C-n1. Wind particles are not readable in stills.** `ground` shows two white specks (x≈430/645, y≈220). `50m` shows dark specks at x≈1040–1180, y≈280–300 that read as dirt or flies, not sun-catching motes. Keep them subtle, but make them warm and bright.
- **C-n2. A white stub sits under the `cloud` puff** (x≈1025, y≈680) and casts a long shadow. It reads as an orphan cloud lump or an unexplained tower.

## D. Other balloons: FAIL
**D1. BLOCKER: no colour variety, so no festival.**
- Every distant balloon reads as the same cream / brick-red stripe with an orange band: `ground` (1113, 778), `50m` (415, 47) and (883, 140), `200m` (893, 178), `500m` (595, 85), `basket` (880, 92).
- Only `200m` (1140, 537) differs, and only to a cream/grey/gold variant.
- `bristol_mass_ascent.jpg` shows the opposite: a blue/yellow, a solid red, a dark grey and pale ones, all distinguishable as specks.
- Give at least half the fleet saturated, dominant hues: a solid red, a deep blue, a purple/yellow, a green, a rainbow. At 20 px a cream-majority balloon disappears into the haze.

**D2. BLOCKER: too few balloons on screen, all tiny.**
- No frame shows more than two other balloons, and none is bigger than about 30 px.
- One balloon should sit near enough in the chase frames to read gores (60–120 px), plus 3–4 specks in depth.
- Right now `50m` and `500m` read as a lone balloon with a couple of dots, not a festival.

## E. Cameras: FAIL
What works:
- **Third person.** `50m`, `200m` and `500m` frame the balloon centred, with the village and river beyond. This is the right composition, though the balloon covers the village in `50m`. Offsetting it a bit to the left would reveal the village (nice-to-have).
- **Burner close-up.** `burner` is excellent: the flame climbs into the mouth, wires converge, the skirt glows, and there is sky and cloud at the edges.

**E1. BLOCKER: in the `down` frame the basket rim reads as a tiled roof.**
- The bottom fifth of `down` (x≈440–1170, y≈715–900) is one huge flat orange trapezoid with fine horizontal banding.
- Among the village's roofs it reads as a building roof, not wicker.
- There is no leather rim edge, no wire, no upright and no balloon shadow. Nothing says "you are leaning out of a basket".
- Fix: narrow the slab, darken it, give it a brown leather rim lip with ink, and let one flying wire or upright enter frame.

**E2. BLOCKER: the `basket` view has no ropes or frame.**
- The brief asks for the rim and ropes to be visible. In `basket` the only cues are the rim bar (y≈770–830) and one featureless dark brown wedge on the left (x≈0–250), which is the upright.
- No flying wire, burner frame or skirt shows.
- Below the rim the inner wall is a plain tan plank surface with no weave (y≈840–900).
- Tilt the default pitch up a few degrees, or let two wires cross the upper corners. Give the inner wall the wicker shader.
