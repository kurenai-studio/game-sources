# Arena round 5, game 2: Ebru (paper-marbling fluid art puzzle) — built by Claude Fable 5.1

```text
Build a single-file browser game (one index.html with inline CSS and JS) using Three.js r170 loaded as an ES
module from https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/three@0.170.0/ (import map; addons from examples/jsm/).
A 3D art-puzzle game called "Ebru" about traditional Turkish paper marbling. Do not ask questions, build it end
to end.

WORLD: an old marbling workshop: a wooden tray (tekne) filled with thickened water, brushes of horsehair, an awl
(biz), combs (tarak), small jars of earth pigments, sheets of paper hanging to dry, warm window light. The tray
is the play area seen from above at a slight angle; the room around it is low-poly and calm. One accent colour
(#1E6FB8 ebru blue).

CORE MECHANIC: a real 2D fluid simulation on the tray surface (stable-fluids / semi-Lagrangian advection of a dye
field on the GPU with render targets, or an equivalent that runs at 60 fps on an Intel UHD 620). Tools:
- brush: sprinkle a colour as drops (each drop spreads as a ring and pushes older colours outward, like real ebru),
- biz (awl): drag a line through the paint, it drags the colour along with a smooth wake,
- tarak (comb): drag many parallel lines at once,
- big drop: one large drop that opens a circle (for the "lale" tulip and "gelgit" wave patterns).
Paint never mixes into mud: colours stay separate bands (track colour as a few separate dye layers or index).
When finished, "lay the paper": the pattern is transferred onto a sheet with a lift animation and kept.

PUZZLE: each lesson shows a target pattern (battal, gelgit, şal, taraklı, bülbül yuvası, lale, karanfil...).
The live match % compares the tray (downscaled) with the target. Solve at >= 80 %; stars for match and number of
strokes. Targets are generated by replaying a recorded stroke list in the same simulation, so every lesson is
provably solvable; the hint replays the next stroke as a ghost. Also a free mode where the player just paints and
saves sheets to a gallery (stored in localStorage as small PNG data, at most 12).

CONTENT: 18 lessons in 3 chapters (basic patterns, combed patterns, flower ebru), each unlocking new colours and
tools; short TR / EN notes from the master (usta) introduce every pattern (real names, short facts only).

INTERACTION: mouse / touch drag for strokes, 1-4 select tool, colour swatches, wheel or pinch to zoom, Z undo,
R reset tray, Enter lay the paper, H hint.

HUD: top bar (logo, lesson, match %, strokes, stars, hint, pause, sound, language), tool and colour tray,
toasts, title, chapter map with lesson stars, result screen with the finished sheet next to the target, gallery,
ending.

EXTRAS: drip and swish sounds, a calm ney-and-kanun music loop, all synthesised (CC0 one-shots from asset-forge
allowed).
```
