# Design prototype

`pieceful-prototype.html` is a throwaway browser prototype of the game, built to try the design before writing the Godot app. Open it in a browser. It needs no build step. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, so it looks right only when online.

It is a reference, not production code. Do not extend it.

## What it shows

- The home, setup, game, store and settings screens, and the finish screen with a copyable result.
- The three cut styles, Regular, Irregular and Wild, with a live outline preview and the piece-count slider.
- Pinch-zoom and pan, dragging, joining pieces into clusters, locking pieces to the board, the four sorting slots, the cleanup button and the ghost image.
- Save and resume, the weekday daily puzzle, and a simulated collection download.
- A look-and-feel panel (palette button) for colour theme, mode, accent, fonts, roundness and effects. The locked-in values are in `docs/visual-design.md`.

## What is useful when porting to Godot

- The cut generator in the section marked `CUT GENERATOR`. It is plain logic with a seeded random generator. It was checked for determinism, for piece areas that sum to the picture area, and for neighbour lists that match in both directions, at 12, 54, 150 and 600 pieces. Port it to GDScript and keep it free of scene code, as `AGENTS.md` requires.
- The cluster, lock and join rules in `settle()`.
- The shelf-packing in `pack()`.
- The slot stack behaviour in `startHand()` and `endHand()`.

## Known limits

- Pictures are drawn by code as stand-ins, with a fixed 3:2 shape.
- Sounds are generated by code, and the vibration may do nothing in a browser. A small on-screen label shows when a vibration would fire.
- It was written and published without being tested in an automated browser, so expect rough edges.
- Wild pieces can overlap slightly where tabs poke into neighbours.
- The page does not run at the 400-piece maximum smoothly on every phone. Its speed says little about Godot's speed.
