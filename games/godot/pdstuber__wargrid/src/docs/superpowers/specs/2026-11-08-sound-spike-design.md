# Sound spike: design (2026-11-08, proposed; replaces the unreleased 2026-11-07 "M7.2 basic sound" draft)

## Intent

Wargrid is silent. This spike adds a small, self-consistent set of sounds that makes a match more fun to play: you hear the fight, your base and the music. "Minimal" means (user, 2026-11-08) "consistent in itself and an actual increment in fun for the people playing the game". The style is futuristic: the user dropped the realistic preset because "I like how the futuristic ones sound way more". It matches the coloured-rectangles-shooting-lasers look.

Placement (user, 2026-11-08): now, after M7 and before Ground batch A, on branch `spike-sound` (at `main` `893332f`). A spike, not a milestone.

## Goals, non-goals, anti-goals

- **Goals:** every weapon of all three factions (USA, China, FRC) is audible; deaths and destroyed buildings are audible; an announcer tells the local player what matters; music in the launcher and in the match; volume sliders; a session-only Master mute (button in the launcher and the Esc menu, key M in the launcher and the match; user addendum 2026-11-08).
- **Non-goals:** a realistic preset or any preset choice; unit replies (select/move/attack voices); engine, money, supply or flamethrower sounds; faction-specific music; shroud culling (M7.9); an options menu beyond the sliders.
- **Anti-goals:** effects that drown the announcer or tire the ear (Zero Hour keeps effects "subtle and in the background", user 2026-11-07); any change to simulation, state hash, replays or netplay traces; any EA audio, text or data copied.

## Approaches considered

- **Effects style:** realistic "Modern" preset, futuristic preset, or both with a launcher dropdown. Both was the 2026-11-07 plan; the user dropped Modern on 2026-11-08. One preset, no dropdown.
- **Announcer** (listened to on 2026-11-08, pages under `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/synth/out/voice-spike*`): (a) Piper voice with ring mod / bitcrush / comb, (b) channel vocoder with the Piper voice driving a synth, (c) espeak-ng formant voice (not tried: not installed), (d) tones only, (e) tone + voice. **Picked: vocoder variant "c1n"** (user: first b1, then "now i like c1 typical more"; c1n fixes a c1 level bug, "now all sound great at 0db"). The radio-filtered plain voice of the old draft is dropped.
- **Positional audio:** (A) Godot `AudioStreamPlayer2D` with its own attenuation, or (B) our own gain function reproducing Zero Hour's falloff exactly on plain players. **Picked: A** (user, 2026-11-08: "use A and determine via the playtest if it sounds strange"). B is the fallback if the playtest finds the falloff wrong; keep the player wrapper small so the switch stays local.

## Decisions carried over from 2026-11-07 (user unless marked)

- Effects are generated by a repo script, `tools/audio/synth.py` (numpy + scipy), seeded, GPL-3.0-or-later; the committed WAVs are the artefacts. CC0 packs and Internet Archive libraries were rejected (reasons in `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/sourcing/REJECTED.md`).
- Futuristic clips picked per cue (the `f` flavour): prototype functions `f_*` in `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/synth/synth_abstract.py`, heard clips in `synth/out/abstract/*-f.wav`. The ambient `a` flavour is unused.
- Music (all CC-BY, re-verify each licence page at import): "Conflict" (Zhelanov, CC BY 4.0), "Enemy spotted" (Zhelanov, CC BY 3.0), "Mammoth" (congusbongus, CC BY 4.0 via OGA-BY/CC-BY), "Stay the Course" (MacLeod, CC BY 4.0), "Hitman" (MacLeod, CC BY 4.0). Sources in `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/sourcing/music/`, details in `sourcing/MANIFEST.md`. Menu track: "Stay the Course". Match playlist: all five.
- Voice source: Piper `en_US-kristin-medium` (model card "public domain", LibriVox recordings; courtesy line in credits). Raw renders of the 8 lines: `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/sourcing/voice/raw/kristin/*.wav` (22,050 Hz mono, `length_scale=0.9, noise_scale=0.5, noise_w_scale=0.5`, see `sourcing/voice/VOICES.md` and `render.py`).
- Formats: effects and voice are mono 16-bit WAV, imported with Godot's QOA compression, Mono on, Max Rate set by measurement; no `.qoa` files in the repo. Music is Ogg Vorbis, stereo, about 96 kbps via `oggenc` (Homebrew `vorbis-tools`; Homebrew ffmpeg has no libvorbis). Opus rejected: Godot imports only WAV, Ogg Vorbis, MP3.
- Licence: Wargrid-made audio is GPL-3.0-or-later; third-party files keep CC0/CC-BY terms. Only CC0 and CC-BY, each checked on its own licence page.
- Raw committed audio ≤ 20,000,000 bytes; exported `.pck` growth measured and recorded.

## Design

**Hard rule:** presentation only. No simulation, state-hash, replay or netplay trace change. Any sim-side change needs a test proving traces equal.

### 1. Effect sounds (10 files, `game/audio/sfx/`)

`rifle_burst`, `carbine`, `cannon`, `explosion`, `vehicle_death`, `building_small`, `building_large`, `infantry_death`, `click`, `error`. Each is the ported `f_*` builder with the prototype's seed (`f"{cue}-f"`, hyphenated, e.g. `rifle-burst-f`), so the samples equal what the user heard. One vehicle death clip (the heard `vehicle-death-f`), varied by random pitch instead of three variants (user, 2026-11-08, heard at ±5 %).

### 2. Cue table (presentation-only, keyed by weapon id, then damage type; deaths by armor)

| Cue | Sound | Trigger | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rifle burst | `rifle_burst` | `shot`, weapon `rifle` (USA Ranger, FRC Insurgent) | once per burst: later shots by the same attacker within `delay_ticks + 1` ticks are silent (read from the weapon definition); limit 4 |
| Carbine | `carbine` | weapon `militia_gun`; fallback for `small_arms`, `rapid_fire`, `gattling` (scout, rotary, tower, pickup and tunnel guns) | per shot; limit 4 |
| Cannon | `cannon` | weapons `cannon`, **`pickup_cannon`** (its damage type is `gattling`, so it needs this explicit row); fallback for `armor_piercing` (`light_tank_gun`, `_plus`) | limit 2 |
| Launch | `explosion`, pitched up (×1.5), −6 dB | fallback for `infantry_missile` and `explosion` (rocket, battery, `pickup_rocket`) | limit 4 |
| Impact | `explosion` at the target | every shot of a weapon whose `affects` is non-empty (splash weapons) | limit 4 |
| Infantry death | `infantry_death` | `death`, armor `human` | limit 3 |
| Vehicle death | `vehicle_death`, pitch random ±5 % | `death`, vehicle armors | limit 4 |
| Building destroyed | `building_small`, or `building_large` for a 3×3 footprint | `death`, structure armors | limit 2; FRC rubble (`hole` kinds) always `building_small` |
| Placement | `click`, pitched down (×0.8) | own building placed | UI |
| UI click / error | `click` / `error` | button press / own command error | UI |

- Burst suppression chains from the attacker's previous shot, not from the last heard one, so a 3-round clip (`delay_ticks` 4) plays one burst (assistant's call; the clip's 0.133 s spacing equals 4 ticks).
- Vehicle versus structure death is decided by object class (building or unit), not by armor name, so `helicopter` and `base_defense` need no list (assistant's call).
- The weapon of a shot is the attacker's current weapon (`kind` + `combat.tier`, as `Combat.weapon_of` does), because FRC units change weapon tier through salvage; the `shot` event carries only `attacker_id`.
- A death event carries only `object_id`; the kind, armor, owner and position come from the previous snapshot.
- A test fails when any weapon or armor in the merged definitions has no cue, so no unit is silent. Ground batch A later adds rows.
- Limits are per cue (Zero Hour default 4); a global cap of about 25 world voices plus a separate UI voice or two. Over the limit, the new event is dropped. (Cut from the 2026-11-07 draft: the 40 % infantry-death chance, the death cooldown, "explosion wins over infantry" and the per-category priority budget.)
- Positional cues use `AudioStreamPlayer2D` on the `Effects` bus, camera as listener. Pick `attenuation` and `max_distance` to come close to Zero Hour: full volume to 175 world units, −6 dB per doubling, nothing past 800. With 1 Wargrid cell = 25 world units (inferred from range ratios, [Zero Hour verification](../../reference/zero-hour-verification.md)) that is full volume to 7 cells and silence past 32 cells; the pixel values follow from the world view's cell size. The builder checks Godot's actual 2D attenuation formula first, including whether camera zoom changes the distance, and records it.
- Entity states (planning rule in [milestone prompt](../../milestone-prompt.md)): a contained unit (in a Pickup or Tunnel Hub) fires no shot (`passengers_fire` is false) and dies through `containers.gd`; a building under construction that is shot counts for "base under attack" and, when destroyed, uses its footprint's building sound; a rubble hole is covered above.

### 3. Mix (user, 2026-11-08: "zero hour proportions sounds good to me")

Approved by ear in `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/synth/out/ingame/ingame-zh.wav` (script `synth/ingame_spike.py`). Levels relative to the music's integrated loudness:

- announcer +6 dB; no ducking (Zero Hour has none: EA source `0a05454`, `MilesAudioManager.cpp` `getEffectiveVolume` is event volume × category volume × distance; speech changes only by map script);
- effects at point-blank: rifle and carbine +0.4, cannon +4.2, explosion −0.8, vehicle death +6.2 (from the 2026-11-07 retail calculation); infantry death −2 and buildings +6.2 are guesses that were heard and approved in the demo, not Zero Hour data;
- UI click and error −4.3, no falloff.

Implementation (assistant's call): the levels are baked into the files, so buses default to 0 dB and the sliders to 100 %. Music files at −30 LUFS, voice lines at −24 LUFS (−20 clips: the vocoder's peaks hit 1.10), each effect at "music + offset" measured on the looped clip. The cue table still has a `gain_db` per cue (default 0) for tuning in the playtest without regenerating. Peaks stay ≤ −1 dBFS. Overall playback is quiet next to commercial games; if the playtest finds it too quiet, raise everything together, never one category alone. Gate B ruling (user, by ear): effects sit against the music with the Effects bus 6 dB lower, so `AudioSettings.apply()` adds `EFFECTS_TRIM_DB` (-6 dB) to the Effects bus; Music and Voice stay at 0 dB at 100 %.

### 4. Announcer (local player only, `Voice` bus, one line at a time, priority queue, a line expires after 5 s)

Voice "c1n": channel vocoder, Kristin raw render as modulator, a single 110 Hz saw as carrier; 32 log-spaced bands from 120 to 7,500 Hz, 4th-order Butterworth band-passes; envelopes from a 2nd-order 80 Hz low-pass of the rectified band; bands from 2,800 Hz up use white noise as carrier; **each band's filtered carrier is normalised to unit RMS before applying the envelope** (without this, sibilant lines turn to hiss and sound quieter after loudness matching); plus 0.35 × the original high-passed at 2,500 Hz for consonants; each line normalised to −24 LUFS. Reference implementation: `c1n` in `$HOME/projects/wargrid-sound-work/synth/voice_spike_c1n.py` (with `voice_spike.py`, `voice_spike_clear.py`), seed `f"voice-{line}"`.

| Line id | Text | Trigger | Cooldown |
|---|---|---|---|
| `base_under_attack` | "Base under attack" | `shot` hitting an own building | 30 s |
| `building_lost` | "Structure lost" | `death` of an own building (not rubble) | 30 s |
| `unit_ready` | "Unit ready" | an own production queue item finishes (not `spawns_on_complete`) | none |
| `construction_complete` | "Construction complete" | an own building turns `completed` (rebuilt FRC buildings included) | none |
| `insufficient_funds` | "Not enough credits" | own command result with the insufficient-credits error | none |
| `low_power` | "Power low" | own power turns negative (never for FRC, which has no power) | 120 s |
| `victory` / `defeat` | "Victory" / "Defeat" | match status | — |

"Victory" / "Defeat" play only when the match is won, judged against the local player's team; a draw, a left opponent or a lost relay play nothing (assistant's call). Hitting rubble is not "base under attack" (assistant's call). Wording is a default the user iterates after playtests. No "unit lost" line. Unit ready / construction complete are a deliberate departure from Zero Hour (unit voices there). Cooldowns and the 5 s expiry are Zero Hour's (EVA).

### 5. Music

Launcher: "Stay the Course" looped. Match: the five tracks in order (Conflict, Enemy spotted, Mammoth, Stay the Course, Hitman), 25 s silence between tracks (Zero Hour uses 60 s; tuned in the playtest), `Music` bus.

### 6. Volume and settings

Buses `Master`, `Music`, `Effects`, `Voice`. Three sliders (music, effects, voice) in the launcher and the Esc menu (`scripts/presentation/pause_menu.gd`), saved in `user://settings.cfg` section `audio` (floats 0–1). Web: audio starts after the first click; the launcher button covers this (verify in a browser).

### 7. Event plumbing (re-pinned on `893332f`)

- Transient events: `shot` (`combat.gd:491`), `death` (`combat.gd:383,420`, `containers.gd:187`), command results (`simulation.gd:360`), all cleared every step (`simulation.gd:497-498`) and copied into the snapshot (`simulation.gd:640`).
- **Online catch-up:** `online_main.gd:51-55` runs up to `CATCH_UP_STEPS` steps per frame, so the director must collect transient events after every step, not once per frame. Do not take a full snapshot per step for this (snapshot cost matters on the perf rows); read the events through a cheap accessor.
- State-derived triggers (construction complete, production finished, power, match end) compare the previous and current presentation snapshot once per frame; state persists, so catch-up loses nothing.
- Containers: a dying Pickup or Tunnel Hub kills its passengers in the same tick; the per-cue limit handles the burst.

### 8. Tools and files

- `tools/audio/synth.py` (numpy, scipy; typed, mypy/ruff clean): ports the `f_*` builders and their helpers from `synth_abstract.py` / `synth_scipy.py`, and the c1n vocoder. `synth.py SRC_DIR OUT_DIR` writes `OUT_DIR/sfx/*.wav` and `OUT_DIR/voice/*.wav`; voice input from `SRC_DIR/voice/raw/kristin/`. Refuses a `SRC_DIR` inside the repo. Faithful-port check: the unscaled renders equal the prototype files byte for byte (before level scaling); a one-off at Gate S with the result recorded, not a CI test (CI cannot reach `$HOME`).
- `tools/audio/process.py` (stdlib + ffmpeg + oggenc): music only now. Two-pass `loudnorm` to −30 LUFS, then `oggenc -b 96`. The voice radio chain of the old draft is dropped.
- Loudness measurement: ffmpeg `ebur128` (both tools already need ffmpeg).
- `game/audio/{music/*.ogg, sfx/*.wav, voice/*.wav}`, `game/audio/CREDITS.md` (one row per music file and the voice: source URL, author, licence, changes; one sentence naming `synth.py` for the effects).
- Release: add `CREDITS.md` to the release extras and the web site; update the README licence section ("Licence for other original art and audio: undecided" becomes decided for audio).
