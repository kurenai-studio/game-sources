# Fold5 frame time, task V2 (2026-10-26)

Result: **pass on both screens.** Every wave measured held the display rate (120 fps) with a mean frame of 8.3 to 8.4 ms and a 99th percentile frame of 8.3 to 9.1 ms. The 60 fps budget (16.7 ms mean, 20 ms 99th percentile) has more than 2x headroom at 3x with up to 78 creatures on the map. The sim's tick is the thing to watch (see the findings).

Device: Galaxy Z Fold5, serial R3CWA0Q2JQP, debug build of HEAD 53d6ad7 (PerfProbe on), Compatibility renderer, Adreno 740. Both screens run at 120 Hz (the engine renders at the display rate, `max_fps=0`). Speed 3x. Screens: cover 904x2316 (portrait layout) and inner 1812x2176 (landscape layout). The phone was folded for the cover runs and unfolded for the inner runs. Folding mid-wave was not measured.

## How it was driven

Saves made by `tests/render/make_perf_save.gd` (a bot build, stopped in the build phase before the level's largest wave, written through `SaveStore` and loaded back to prove it). Pushed to `files/game_level.json`, then Play, Continue, Resume, 3x, Start wave. The Call wave button was tapped about once a second so the next wave started as soon as the game allowed, which puts two waves on the map. The summary line of PerfProbe ends when the sim leaves the wave phase, so waves called early show as one summary.

## Findings

1. **Draw calls are above the "about 200" in 9.1.** They run 129 to 421 per frame, up to about 420 around the start of a wave and in the build phase with the full info panel, and about 130 to 160 in the middle of a big wave. Frames hold 120 fps, so this is a note, not a failure. 9.1 says "about 200, measured", so the table should read "up to about 420 on the Fold5".
2. **The sim tick is the thinnest margin.** With 78 live creatures one 2 second window had a tick 99th percentile of 2.00 ms and the single worst tick was 2.44 ms (inner 2-2). Over the whole wave the 99th percentile was 1.60 ms. The planned peak is 200 live creatures, and the budget says at most 2 ms at that load. The phone shows about 2 ms already at 78 live. If a level reaches 150 or more creatures, the tick will go over its budget on the Fold5. The balance report's bot peaks stay at or under 101. Nothing to fix now, but this belongs to the sim engineer, and a heavier level should be checked on the phone. At 3x on a 120 Hz screen the game runs 1.5 ticks per frame, so 1.6 ms of tick is about 2.4 ms a frame, which still fits.
3. **First frame spikes.** The first frame after Resume or after a card is 25 to 45 ms. It does not repeat. These are one-off hitches on load, not a sustained cost.
4. 120 Hz hides how much room is left: a mean of 8.3 ms means every frame fit the vsync. Whether the game would fit at 60 Hz is not in doubt (it uses at most about 2 ms of tick plus 2 ms of scripts per frame), but the GPU time was not read.

## What was not measured

- Folding or unfolding mid-wave.
- 60 Hz (the DM may set `max_fps=60`).
- The very largest peak (200 live). No bot build in the balance report reaches it.

## How to repeat

1. Make the saves (headless, about 20 to 90 seconds):
   `godot --headless --path . --script res://tests/render/make_perf_save.gd -- levels=r2_l2,r3_l2,r4_l3 out=%TEMP%/forkline_v2 seeds=200`
   The bot seed picks the build with the biggest crowd that it can find. Known good picks on 2026-10-26: 2-2 seed 7708, 3-2 seed 7881, 4-3 seed 7770 (the seed range starts at 7700).
2. Wake and unlock the phone. Fold or unfold it for the screen you want, then check `adb shell wm size` (904x2316 cover, 1812x2176 inner). Keep the screen on with `adb shell svc power stayon usb` and put the old value back afterwards.
3. Back up what the app has, because a win or a card changes progress.json (the info cards add to "seen"):
   `adb shell run-as io.github.bartbasi.forkline cp files/progress.json files/progress.json.v2bak`
   and the same for game_level.json if one exists.
4. Stop the app, push one save. In Git Bash set `MSYS_NO_PATHCONV=1` so `/data/...` is not rewritten:
   `adb shell am force-stop io.github.bartbasi.forkline`
   `adb push r2_l2/game_level.json /data/local/tmp/game_level.json`
   `adb shell run-as io.github.bartbasi.forkline cp /data/local/tmp/game_level.json files/game_level.json`
5. `adb logcat -c`, then `adb shell monkey -p io.github.bartbasi.forkline 1`. Take screenshots with the display id, or the output is not a valid PNG: `adb exec-out screencap -p -d <id> > shot.png`. The ids come from `adb shell dumpsys SurfaceFlinger --display-id`. On this phone the inner screen is 4630947175568309891 and the cover screen is 4630946481096930692.
6. Tap points in pixels. Cover: Play (452,1636), Continue (452,354), Resume (452,1001), 3x (377,307 on the paused screen layout shown after Resume), the big Start wave and Call wave button (669,307, and then the button's right half at 700,307), card "Got it" (452,1529). Inner: Play (1355,1000), Continue (905,292), Resume (907,871), 3x (1533,160), Start wave and Call wave (1469,313). Tap the Call wave button about once a second to keep two waves on the map. Dismiss info cards fast. Do not tap the map, which selects creatures.
7. `adb logcat -d -s godot | grep perf` gives `perf fps=...` lines every 2 seconds and one `perf wave frames=... mean=... p99=... worst=... tick=mean/p99/max peak_live=...` line when the wave phase ends. Take the numbers for the table from those.
8. Clean up: restore progress.json from the backup if it changed (`cmp` first), delete game_level.json, the backup and /data/local/tmp/game_level*.json, then `adb shell am force-stop io.github.bartbasi.forkline`.
