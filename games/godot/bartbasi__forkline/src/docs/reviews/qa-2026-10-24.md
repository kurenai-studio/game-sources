# QA review, 2026-10-24: region 1 before hard stop B

Reviewer: qa-reviewer. Build: HEAD 473d768. Windows only; the Fold5 was not connected.

Severity scale: the DM's words, with the agent file's words in brackets. Blocker (Critical), major (High), minor (Medium), polish (Low).

No blocker found. Nothing crashed, no script error showed in any run, every battle ended, stars were recorded, and every save resumed to the exact tick, including after a real process kill.

## Findings

### Major

**M1. Map cells drop below 48 dp on the cover screen on 11 row maps.** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/battle_screen.gd layout). The HUD budget is in the architect's contract (ARCHITECTURE 7.4).

Steps: lay the app out at the cover size with the cover insets the tests use (1080 x 2767, safe area 0, 96, 1080, 2500). Start 1-5 or 1-6 the first time (hint banner and gate strip show). Measure `map_view` scale times `MapView.CELL`. Then tap the wave preview header, then tap the HUD pause button.

| State on 1-5 or 1-6 | Cell, logical px | Cell, dp (3.14 px per dp) |
|---|---|---|
| Hint closed, preview folded | 150 | 47.6 |
| First play: hint banner showing | 128 | 40.8 |
| Plus the wave preview opened | 95 | 30.2 |
| Plus the HUD pause strip | 73 | 23.4 |

Without insets (a plain Windows window) the same states give 48.5, 37.9 and 31.1 dp. 1-1 (9 rows) with the pause strip gives 41.6 dp with insets.

Expected: every build spot and socket is a 48 dp target (CLAUDE.md, RULES 3.1). Actual: the bars above and below the map take height from the map, so cells shrink, most of all while paused with the strip, which is when players build. 1-4, 1-5 and 1-6 have 11 rows; later regions go to 13.

### Minor

**m1. The wave preview is folded during build phases in portrait.** Owner: ui-engineer. battle_screen.gd:348 sets `preview.set_auto_expand(landscape)`, so wave_preview.gd:66 keeps it folded in every portrait build phase. Expected (RULES 9.1): open during build phases, each row with name, count and entrance. Actual: a strip of silhouettes with "4 A" style counts and no names, until the player taps the header. Opening it is what shrinks the map in M1, so the two need one answer. Question for the game designer: is the folded strip acceptable in portrait?

**m2. The "New" tag never shows.** Owner: ui-engineer. battle_screen.gd:531 marks a creature seen before its card is read, then line 541 refreshes the preview, so the tag is gone by the time the card closes. The folded strip has no tag at all. Steps: start 1-4, play to the build phase before wave 3 (Pips), close the card, open the preview: no "New" tag (checked: 0 tags). Expected (RULES 9.1, 9.7): the never-seen creature carries a "New" word.

**m3. Calling a new creature's wave early skips its first-sight card.** Owner: ui-engineer (battle_screen.gd:488 to 492 checks sights only on `build_started`). Steps: 1-4, start wave 1, call waves 2 and 3 early as soon as allowed. Pips walk on the map with no card; the card opens at the next build phase, after the player has met them. Question for the game designer (RULES 9.2 and the "no ambushes" pillar): should the card open, pausing the game, when such a wave is called early?

**m4. The Continue card shows the wrong wave in a build phase.** Owner: ui-engineer, app.gd:293. Steps: 1-4, clear wave 2, go Back to menu, Play. Continue reads "Level 1-4, Pumpkin Patch. Wave 2 of 9." The HUD on the same game reads "Wave 3/9". Mid-wave the two agree. Expected: the same number as the HUD.

**m5. "Need N" gold text goes stale.** Owner: ui-engineer. build_panel.gd:100 and tower_panel.gd:155 rebuild only when affordability changes, not when gold changes, but build_panel.gd:173 and tower_panel.gd:307 print the gold still missing. Steps: 1-1, spend to 0 gold, start wave 1, open an empty spot. Cards read "Need 100" and "Need 120". After kills raise gold to 15 they still read "Need 100" and "Need 120" (should be 85 and 105). Same for "need N more" on the tier 3 path cards.

**m6. Roguelite and Daily look open at 12 stars, then say "Not ready yet".** Owner: ui-engineer (app.gd:161 sets `runs_open` from progress; app.gd:519 answers with a notice). The contract (ARCHITECTURE 3.6, flow step 2) says they stay locked in phase 5. Region 1 holds 18 stars, so Bart can reach 12 at hard stop B. Steps: give the progress 12 stars, open Play: both cards drop the lock and read like Campaign; a tap says "This mode is coming in a later version of Forkline." Info also says "Both open when you have earned 12 stars."

**m7. Tower names are cut in Info, "Telling towers apart".** Owner: ui-engineer, info_art.gd:227 to 235. At both window sizes the captions read "Longbow Lof" and "Firework Towe", and "Pumpkin Catapult" is shrunk to about 6 dp text. Screenshots: cover `23_info_mid.png`, inner `23_info_mid.png` (paths below).

**m8. A quick double tap on Sell sells.** Owner: ui-engineer, tower_panel.gd:118 to 121 and 320. The first tap rebuilds the panel with "Tap again to sell for N" in the same place, so a double tap is both taps. On an old tower that costs 30% of what was paid. Suggest a short delay before the armed button accepts a tap, or a different place for it.

### Polish

**p1. About license row wording.** Owner: ui-engineer, about_screen.gd:79: "GNU GPL version 3, added terms". CLAUDE.md says UI copy says "with additional terms".

**p2. The hint banner stays all level.** Owner: ui-engineer. After the player builds on the highlighted spot only the ring goes (battle_screen.gd:591). The sentence stays until closed, through every wave, and costs map height in portrait (M1). Seen on 1-6 still showing at wave 10.

**p3. Back with a build or tower panel open opens Pause.** Owner: ui-engineer (app.gd:378), as the contract says (ARCHITECTURE 3.6 step 6). Most players expect back to close the open panel first. Suggestion for the architect and Bart.

**p4. A saved Relaxed game's level card shows Normal chosen next to Resume.** Owner: ui-engineer, level_card.gd:129. Resume plays Relaxed, but the card shows "Normal: 20 lives and 200 gold. All three stars." chosen.

**p5. First-sight cards and the hint are marked seen before they are read.** Owner: ui-engineer, battle_screen.gd:529 to 536. Leave or kill the app while a card is open and it never opens by itself again. Small, since rows and the build card show the same numbers.

**p6. Speed resets to 1x after Continue.** Owner: ui-engineer. RULES 4.8 says the chosen speed stays until the player changes it, inside a level. Question: is a reset on resume fine?

**p7. Leaks at exit.** Owner: sim-engineer (src/sim, src/save). Quitting the real game from the front door reports 10 ObjectDB instances leaked and 9 resources still in use: data_check.gd, catalog.gd, level_data.gd, json_file.gd, game_data.gd, settings.gd, progress.gd, battle.gd, saved_game.gd (GDScript objects, which points at cyclic script references). The test run reports 70 and 2. Harmless at exit, but noise that can hide real leaks.

**p8. Range decimals differ.** Owner: ui-engineer (battle_text.gd). The upgrade row reads "Range 2.5 → 3" while the tier 2 stat row reads "3 tiles".
