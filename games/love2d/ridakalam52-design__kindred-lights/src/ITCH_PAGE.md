# itch.io page text: Kindred Lights

**Title:** Kindred Lights

**Short tagline:** Sing into the dark. Someone will answer.

**Genre:** Adventure / Exploration · **Platforms:** Windows, macOS (also any OS with LÖVE 11.5 via the .love file) · **Length:** about 6–9 minutes · **Made for:** thatgamejam #02

---

## Description

You are a small light, alone in a dark and quiet land.

When you sing, other sleeping lights hear you, and they answer. Each friend you wake follows close
behind you, adds its own voice to an echoing chord, and makes your shared glow bigger. Deep mist that
one light could never cross parts for many. In the dawn fields, a soft heartbeat begins, and flowers
bloom wherever your song falls. Sing on the beat and your friends sing with you.

At the end of the journey, the Old Light waits on the hill.

Kindred Lights grew out of the jam's theme track. We analysed it: about 117 BPM, F minor rocking
toward D♭ major, a quiet start, a slow swell, a rhythm that arrives halfway, and a gentle fade. The
game follows the same arc in four chapters: **Hush, Swell, Pulse, Glow**. The music you hear while
you travel is made live by the game's own synthesizer, in the track's key and tempo. It grows one
voice at a time as friends join you. In the finale, the theme track plays in full and unmodified,
and the whole world answers.

No enemies, no timer, no way to fail. Just a little light finding others.

*Headphones recommended.*

## Controls

| Action | Mouse | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| Drift | hold left button (you float toward the cursor) | WASD / arrow keys |
| Sing | right button | Space (also J / E / Z) |
| Pause | n/a | Esc (Q on the pause screen returns to the title) |
| Fullscreen | n/a | F11 or Alt+Enter |

**Tips:** Sleeping lights call out softly every few seconds, so listen and watch for their rings.
The dots in the top-left corner show how many friends you have found.

## How to run

- **Windows:** download `KindredLights-win64.zip`, unzip the whole folder, and run
  `KindredLights.exe`. The game isn't code-signed, so if SmartScreen warns you, click *More info*,
  then *Run anyway*.
- **macOS:** download `KindredLights-macos.zip`, unzip it, then right-click
  `Kindred Lights.app` and choose *Open*. The app isn't signed or notarized; if macOS still blocks
  it, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and choose *Open Anyway*.
- **Linux / other:** install LÖVE 11.5 and run `love KindredLights.love`.
