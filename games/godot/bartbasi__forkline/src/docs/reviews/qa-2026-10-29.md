**L1. On the cover, the map moves down when the last wave starts.** Owner: ui-engineer (src/ui/battle_hud.gd:298).

- Steps: on the cover shape, play any level to its last wave and start it. Seen on 8-6 (wave 12), cover window 362 x 926.

The HUD grows by about 50 logical px (20 px in the 0.4 window) and the whole map moves down that much in the middle of the wave. The bottom of the map loses the same height. Screenshots: `coverç_r8_l6_stump_step.png` (wave 11), `cover9_r8_l6_preview_w12.png` (before wave 12) and `coveré_r8_l6_toad_hop.png` (wave 12), side by side in `m_hud.png`.
