| 2026-10-21 | **The music comes back after another app.** On a phone, switching apps (or a call, or locking the screen) makes iOS suspend or "interrupt" the game's sound, and it stays that way when you come back; only the title screen and the first tap after loading ever resumed it, so the music could stay off until something happened to restart it. Now the sound engine notices when the phone takes the sound and resumes it as soon as the game is on screen again (when the page is shown, focused, or the context changes state), and again on any tap or key, which is what iOS sometimes insists on; a tap after being away also plays a silent blip, which clears the WebKit case where the sound says it's running but stays mute. Holding the phone upright still pauses the sound on purpose (`audio.held`). The music scheduler also skips the steps it missed while its timer was frozen in the background, instead of playing them all at once on return. Checked in the dev game: suspended sound resumes by itself within a second; a tap clears the away flag; with the phone "upright" it stays paused, and resumes when turned back; a 5 s gap in the scheduler jumps ahead rather than bursting; no errors |
