## Game

•  **Controls a snake** (often represented by a line or a series of segments) that moves continuously across a grid.

• **Goal** : to guide the snake toward food items (such as an apple or a candy) that appear randomly.

• **Growth**: Each time the snake eats an item, its length increases and the score goes up.

• **Game over**: The game ends if the snake’s head hits the walls (the edges of the screen) or collides with its own body.
