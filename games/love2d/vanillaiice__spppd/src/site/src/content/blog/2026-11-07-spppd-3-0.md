SPPPD 3.0 is out. The [changelog](/changelog/) lists everything in it. This is the
story behind the bigger parts.

## One version to race on

Until now, nearly every update changed what the game sends online, so a friend who
hadn't updated couldn't join your room. That stops with 3.0. Later 3.x versions only
add to what they send, so a 3.2 and a 3.0 race each other fine, and you update when
you feel like it. The one catch is getting there: 2.x games can't join a 3.0 room, so
everyone needs 3.0 once.

Online also holds on better. If your connection drops for a moment, the game tries to
get you back into your seat, and the race carries on meanwhile with your car shown as
a ghost. Updating the server doesn't end races any more either: they pick up again a
few seconds later.

## Two at one screen

Plug a pad into a desktop and set PLAYERS to 2. The screen splits side by side
rather than top and bottom, because the camera looks up the screen at the road ahead,
and a wide, short strip would show you very little of it. Each driver has their own
car, paint, handling and gearbox, their own dash, and their own engine on their side
of the speakers. One driver alone plays exactly as before.

## A lot to play in

Freestyle used to be a circuit with nobody else on it. It now opens on THE LOT: a
donut ring round a tyre stack, a slalom of cones, a long wall to slide along and two
blocks of containers to thread between. The cones get knocked about without wrecking
you, and they're back in place once you drive off. Freestyle can still take any
circuit, and the pause menu swaps car and paint without leaving.

Hold gas and brake together near a stop for a burnout. Each drivetrain does its own
thing: rear-drive cars swing their tails round, front-drive ones barely turn, and
four-wheel drive creeps forward. On the grid it's a good way to wait for the lights.

## Time trial counts every lap

Time trial had a bug that hit exactly the people who play it most. A single moment
with all four wheels off the road threw the lap away, and a driver sliding wide does
that nearly every lap, so best laps and ghosts never came. The test drivers that check
each version stay neatly on the road, which is why it went unnoticed. Now every lap
counts by default, and CLEAN rules keep the old strictness for anyone who wants it,
telling you the moment a lap is lost and why.

## Your controls

CONTROLS has a screen of its own. Change any action's key or pad button there. A pad
works on a phone too: touch the stick and the touch controls step aside. On touch you
can drive the manual gearbox, turn auto gas off, and size the buttons to your thumbs.

Phones got faster as well. The slowest phone SPPPD is tested on, a Galaxy A04, went
from 54 to 59 frames a second at MIRAGE at midnight under boost, its hardest scene.

## What's next

The [roadmap](/roadmap/) has the plans: Elimination races, a career, cop chases, quick
matches online with strangers, and drift combos in freestyle. 3.x updates add these
without splitting anyone up online.
