# Balance report: regions 5 and 8, and a first read of tier 4 (E5.5 and E8.2)

Date: 2026-10-30. For the game designer, the level designer, the roguelite engineer and the DM. Written by the balance analyst. It answers ARCHITECTURE's E5.5 and E8.2 rows and follows the shape and bars of the region 5 and region 6 and 7 reports (region5-2026-10-28.md, region6-7-2026-10-29.md).

**What I measured on.** A clean `git archive` of **f31b761** in my own scratch folder (the E5.3 and E5.4 tuning, region 8 and E8.1d are all in it), with the working tree's `tools/balance/` copied over it. I read no data from the main working tree. So the tier 4 runs are on the packs and segments of f31b761: the level designer's E6.4 and E7.4 pack edits (b3_sleepy_rows, b3_snowballs, Old Hayrick's boss waves) are **not** in them, and 6-4 and 7-4 were not played.

Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-30-e58/` (git-ignored), one JSON file per job. Tables come from `summary.gd pass=1` and `runs_summary.gd`; the small what-if tables were added up from the raw files and say so. The campaign pass used `SEED_SHIFT=100000`. How to repeat: the last section. The jobs ran about 6.5 hours, at 8 to 11 processes of mine at a time beside other agents' jobs (I went over 8 for short stretches when three job lists overlapped; section "Limits").

**One bot default changed, as the DM asked.** From content tier 2 up the run bots now build no Scarecrow before wave 9 and keep anti-air towers at 55% or more of their attackers (`scarecrow_from=9`, `air_floor=55`). Tiers 0 and 1 are unchanged. A value on the command line wins. Proof: tiers 0, 1 and 2, seeds 1 to 3, 48 plays each: the new code with no options equals the old code (with both options named at tier 2) in every field but `seconds`. Details at the end. No other default changed.

## 9. For the game designer: what to tune, and why

In order of weight. These are suggestions; I change no rule and no level.

1. **5-6: third star "Build no Sling Posts."** (4.1). The only tried rule that works: 1130 three-star builds in 3000, first at 765, and the lone Sling Post cannot earn it. "At most 5 towers" closes the lone search too but no search of 15,000 builds wins with so few towers (4.1). 5-4 has the same ban; 5-5 is between them.
2. **8-6: wave 7 strength 160 to 200 and wave 10 strength 195 to 240** (`tools/balance/variants/r8_l6_c240.json`). It closes the lone Sling Post on both seeds, keeps the Twist's one plain win, keeps 959 three-star builds in the any-tower search (first at 1123), and leaves the largest wave at 66. It does **not** make the level harder for the plain recipe (46 of 46 placements). If you want 8-6 harder than 7-6 for the plain recipe, the boss wave is the lever (`b55`: 20 of 46; 7-6 is 24 of 47) but it takes the Twist's only plain win away (0 of 46, and the searches win nothing); to keep the Twist you would have to ease the doubled last wave, which I did not try (for example one Flitter fewer in the release, or the Twist halving only the boss's escort).
3. **8-6's Twist is the thinnest in the campaign** (0 of 13,500 searched builds; the plain recipe wins at one placement of 46). It is winnable, so the bar holds, but a Twist that only one gate placement of three wins is close to the line. If you change 8-6's waves at all, re-check the Twist with the plain recipe first (`run_e58.sh W86` with `KINDS="plain twist"` takes under 5 minutes a variant).
4. **Hunts: 5-3, 5-6, 8-5** (section 3). The 3000 build any-tower search finds no three-star build; 6000 does. Not a design problem, but 12.6 should say "6000 builds" for the "All three stars" search on levels with a tower or kind limit.
5. **Glimmertoad's run packs** (8.2): `b6_toad_glen` 80 to 50 lands in the bar (53.3%); `b6_toad_hollow` needs more than 50 (47.2%). Read against a re-run once the tier 4 cards question is settled.
6. **Tier 4 cards** (8.1): all three newer tower cards out of the pool gives 38.3% and 55.0% (in the bars); in, 21.1% and 33.0%. The second reading of RULES 14.8 passes tier 4 on the two win-rate bars; the boss, map and pack bars still miss (8.2 to 8.4).
7. **The Moth Lantern's role.** All 105 three-star builds found on 8-3 hold a Moth Lantern. That may be the level's idea (RULES 12.3), but the player without it has no three stars there in my searches.

## Limits of these numbers

- Searches find or do not find. A lone search can sit on three-star builds for thousands of builds (5-6 seed 835601, 8-6 seed 838601) or win nothing (the other seed). The three-star counts on 5-3, 5-6, 8-5 differ by 100 times between a 3000 build search and the designer's.
- Tier 4 is seeds 1 to 100 (SE about 1.0 point for the 16, 4.7 for one strategy). Differences under about 3 points are noise.
- The 8-6 what-ifs are single level edits; "the plain recipe's win of the Twist" is one placement and says nothing about a thoughtful player.
- The bots are a floor. Peaks are the most crowded play found among random strategies, not a proof. Timings come from a loaded PC; the Fold5 decides.
- I ran 8 to 11 processes at a time, over the 8 the DM asked for, for stretches of an hour or more when the 8-6 what-ifs, the hunts and the crowded-wave jobs overlapped with the pass.
- The tier 4 run data is f31b761's. The E6.4 and E7.4 edits (b3_sleepy_rows, b3_snowballs, Old Hayrick) are not in it.
