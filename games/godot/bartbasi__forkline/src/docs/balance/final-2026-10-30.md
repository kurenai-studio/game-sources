# Final balance report for version 1 (B7)

Date: 2026-10-30. For the game designer and the level designer. Written by the balance analyst, for the DM to pass on. It answers task B7 on commit **5b960f2** (E8.3 in, every region tuned) and follows RULES 12.6 (levels and Twists) and 14.8 and 15 (runs and the daily). Nothing in the game's data or rules was changed; the misses below come with suggested fixes.

**What I measured on.** A clean `git archive` of 5b960f2 in my own scratch folder, with its own `tools/balance/` (no bot, `run.gd`, `runs.gd` or `run_bot.gd` file changed for this pass) plus three new job scripts (`run_b7.sh`, `run_queue.sh`, `assemble_b7.sh`). I read no data from the working tree. At most 8 Godot processes at once, each wrapped in `timeout`; the other agents' test runs counted against the same 8. Raw numbers: `docs/balance/raw/2026-10-30-b7/` (git-ignored), one JSON file per job. Tables come from `summary.gd pass=1` and `runs_summary.gd`; the small tables marked "added up" were summed from the raw files with `jq` and a small script, and say so. How to repeat it: the last section.

### Misses, and what I would do about each

1. **3-5 Two Shores: a lone Sling Post earns three stars ("No super tower").** One search of four (seed 1063501, 6000 builds) found 59 three-star builds, the first at build 5600. The build is nine Sling Posts, none above tier 2, 15 lives (the star 2 line). Fix, for the level designer and game designer: the star 3 rules I tried do not close it (2.4): "at most 8, 7 or 6 towers" lets the lone Sling Post earn three stars far more often (2232, 1996 and 1828 of 6000 for 8, 7 and 6 towers), "Upgrade no towers" is not reachable by any search (0 three-star builds in 6000 with every tower), and "Build no Sling Posts" is out because 3-4 and 3-6 next door ban it (RULES 12.5). What is left is the lever E8.3 used on 8-6: a small wave change that takes the lone build below 15 lives. It wins with exactly 15: all 5 lives it loses leak in the last wave, which brings 32 Pips. The lever is that wave's Pips (32, say to 38), the same lever E6.3 and E7.3 used on 6-4 and 7-4. The plain recipe (best 12 lives on 3-5) and the any-tower hunt (744 and 1067 three-star builds) would need playing again after it. I did not test a wave change.
2. **Tiers 2, 3 and 4: both Normal bars missed on the first reading, met on the second** (30.0%, 28.4%, 24.8% of the 16 together against a floor of 35%; the best strategy 50.0%, 39.5%, 36.5% against 55%). Under RULES 14.8 the second reading meets both bars at all three tiers, so they count as met. The cause is spread over the three newer tower cards: taking any one out gives back 4.5 to 4.9 of the 19.6 points at tier 4, and any one alone in the pool costs 7.8 to 11.0 (section 3.2). So no one card can be tuned to fix it. My suggestion: write the second reading into 14.8 as the bar from tier 2 on ("a player can always pass on a tower card"), since all three tiers meet it with room (38.8% to 44.4%; 56.0% to 59.5%). The habit I would test if you want the first reading to meet its bar is a floor on the Longbow Loft (the boss and armor tower): with the cards in, 26% of tier 4 plays never build one (6% at tier 0) and those win 6% (3.2); that is worth about 4 points of the 19.6 by a crude count.
3. **b6_toad_hollow (tier 4 boss): 46.6% of 2356 boss runs won, 3.4 under the floor.** The second reading is 67.6% and meets the bar, so under 14.8 the bar is met. If you want the first reading in the bar too, the lever the designer named in TUNING_R8 (7, suggestion 2) is Glimmertoad's health: 3600 to 3300, with 8-6's boss wave from 55 to 60 to keep 1980. E8.2 measured 1.5 points of win rate for each point of boss strength; 300 health is 8% of 3600, so I would expect about 5 to 6 points, enough to land near 52%. That is an estimate from the slope, not a test.
4. **b4_yawning_watch (tier 3 pack): 12.7% of 3032 runs lost inside it, bar 10%.** The second reading is 7.3% and meets the bar. For the first reading the nearest lever is the one E8.3 used on b4_blinking_watch (12.6% to 9.6%): its last wave's Dusk Moths from 4 to 3. 187 of the 385 runs lost inside it died in that wave. Not tested on this pack.
5. **Older content at later tiers, reported and not tuned (14.8):** the lists are in 3.6. Worth the roguelite engineer's look: `mid_planks` (a tier 2 segment) falls to 0.52 times all runs at tier 4 in the second reading (2.7 SE under, 12 seeds).
6. **Thin Twists** (they pass): 6-6 Double Trouble wins 2 of 1500 builds; 4-1 Thin Purse 78. If a player finds 6-6's Twist hard, the plain recipe wins 20 of 35 placements of it, so the gates are not the problem; the search is the thin part.
7. **RULES 12.6 wording:** a hunt (two searches of 6000 builds) should follow a first search of 3000 builds that found no three-star build for a tier cap too (3-5 needed it: first three-star builds at 3808 and 4450 of 6000).
8. **Noise, not misses:** tier 0's best strategy at 75.5% (0.5 over the ceiling, 0.2 SE) and `mid_eye` at 0.748 (0.002 under) are the same rows B5 reported; nothing moved.

### 5.3 Limits

- Searches find or do not find. A lone search can sit on three-star builds for thousands of builds and a second seed can win nothing: 3-5 above is the third time (after 5-6 and 8-6). Two seeds a level is the rule; I played four on regions 1 to 4 and on the four changed levels, and one more seed on the 19 levels where the lone Sling Post wins thousands of builds (a third seed, folder x: 23 of the 23 searches finished, 0 three-star builds in them). Read a pass as "no lone attacker was found", not "none exists".
- Run bars carry a standard error of 0.8 points for the 16 together and 3.5 for one strategy; stacks and packs on natural seeds carry 3 to 5 points, so the ones near a bar were forced.
- The bots are a floor. A player who reads the preview, aims at Strong for the boss and passes on a card should do better, and the second reading is the fair floor the rules ask for.
- The campaign long searches of 5-1 to 5-5, 6-1 to 6-3, 6-5, 6-6, 7-1 to 7-3, 7-5, 7-6 and 8-1 to 8-5 are reused (1.2 and 1.3). All their cheap checks were played again and equal.
- No tick timings: the benchmark was not run (`SKIP_BENCH`); a busy machine would not make them mean much, and the Fold5 decides. Crowded-wave saved games are E5.5's.
- The machine was shared: other agents' test runs counted against my cap of 8 Godot processes, so for some stretches I ran 6 or 7. Nothing went over 8.

### 5.6 How to repeat it

From a clean `git archive` of 5b960f2, after `godot --headless --path . --import`, with the three new scripts in `tools/balance/` (they are in the working tree next to this report):

```
export OUT=docs/balance/raw/2026-10-30-b7
bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh NEED
for P in CA R D CB; do bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh $P; done           # appends to $OUT/queue.txt
bash tools/balance/run_queue.sh $OUT/queue.txt 8                        # runs until you end it by the PID in $OUT/queue.txt.sentinel
SKIP="r6_l1 r6_l3 r6_l5 r6_l6 r7_l2 r7_l3 r7_l5 r8_l2 r8_l3 r8_l4" bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh NB
bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh POST; bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh RPOST; bash tools/balance/run_b7.sh X   # after the first jobs are done
bash tools/balance/assemble_b7.sh $OUT docs/balance/raw/2026-10-30-e58 docs/balance/raw/2026-10-29-e67 docs/balance/raw/2026-10-25-b3
godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/summary.gd -- dir=res://$OUT/final pass=1
for T in 0 1 2 3 4; do godot --headless --path . --script res://tools/balance/runs_summary.gd -- dir=res://$OUT/r/t$T tier=$T part=b4; done
```
